# Changelog

What changed for customers and for the team, newest first. One line per merged pull request
that changes behaviour; internal-only work can be grouped. Versions follow phases
(`0.<phase>.<n>`), tagged on main when a phase demo works (docs/hygiene.md).

## Unreleased
- The Catalog's discounts and coupon codes (ALY-150 part 1 of 3, D-039, spec 014, migration 0040; no new dependency, no new template). A feature of the Features screen, **off for every shop until switched on**, so nothing changes for a shop that does not: a coupon code a customer types (a percentage or an amount, or buy N get M free; the whole order or chosen items and categories; a minimum order; total and per-customer limits; dates; a price group; stackable or not) and automatic discounts; the discount shown line by line and in the total before payment and kept as a snapshot on the order; a limited code cannot be over-used and cancelling an order gives its use back; a Discounts screen for owners; a coupon box on the order page and "coupon CODE" in the WhatsApp order, in English and Arabic; a line in the weekly report.
- Documents package 2, receipts and invoices from sales and delivery to the customer, still hidden (ALY-139, D-042, spec 013, migration 0038; no new dependency): for a shop with the `documents` module on, an order paid by link gets a receipt, an order put on a customer's credit account gets an invoice, a payment on a credit account gets a receipt that says what is left, and an order cancelled after its receipt or invoice was issued gets a credit note, once, linked to the original, in the cancellation's own transaction (a payment reported twice or six times at once makes one document; a document that cannot be made never stops the sale and says why on the record; a shop without the module is untouched). Staff can write down a payment taken by hand (cash, bank transfer, cheque, card terminal, with a reference, who and when) for an order, a booking (a deposit receipt for part, a receipt for the rest), an invoice or a customer's balance, and the receipt follows; owners and admins, and agents only if the shop allows it. An invoice can be asked for on any order or booking, and an issued document cancelled with a reason (a credit note; the document stays, void). Each document goes to the customer: inside their 24-hour window as a WhatsApp document message with a short caption in their language, outside it as the PDF in the header of a utility template; by email with the PDF attached when the customer has an address on file (never to an example domain); with a link to a hosted page (`/d/<shop>?t=...`, a signed token made for that page only, 90 days, no bare ID) that shows the document and a Download PDF button in English and Arabic, and a calm "expired" or "not valid" page otherwise. Consent and the window apply like any message; a document that fails is retried with growing waits and then shows the owner "not delivered"; staff can resend to the customer or to another number or address, and the shop chooses what goes out on its own (receipts, invoices, deposit receipts; WhatsApp, email; at once or only when staff press send). Three templates (`document_receipt`, `document_invoice`, `document_credit_note`, English and Arabic) are written and **not submitted to Meta**. Bookings have no payment step, so a booking's receipt comes from a payment recorded by hand. API only: no screen (ALY-146). Not tried: a real WhatsApp send, a real email, a real phone; Arabic is a draft until ALY-66.
- The Catalog's feature engine, second part (ALY-148, D-039, migrations 0036 and 0037; no new dependency): **a shop's own fields**, **changing a shop's type**, and **templates made in the back office**. An owner opens Catalog, Features, Manage fields, and adds fields such as Serves, Warranty months or Concentration (text, a number with a unit, one from a list, yes or no, a date), for the item or for each size or colour, and chooses whether customers see them and whether they are searched or needed; fields appear in the item form, the items list's search, the customer's item sheet, the Excel and CSV files and the import, and an order keeps what the customer saw. A field is archived, never deleted. Each type of business starts with a few fields of its own that the owner can edit or archive. Catalog, Features, Change type previews what another kind of business would add (features, categories, fields, the section's name) and adds it; nothing is switched off, removed or changed. In the back office, **Templates** lets ALYR make a template for a new kind of client from the closest one (switch features, name the section, list categories and fields, save), pick it when setting up a new shop, or move a client's business to it after reading a preview; every change is on the audit log. The back office is in English only. Spec `specs/012-feature-engine/spec.md`.
- The Catalog's feature engine, first part (ALY-148, D-039, migration 0035; no new dependency): the Catalog is one system and a kind of business is a configuration of it. A registry lists what the Catalog can do (sizes and colours, choices and extras, counted stock, a number each day, times it can be ordered, a pickup day, price groups, pack sizes, quick quantity, "same as last time", the item code, low-stock and sold-out messages, "tell me when it is back", share links, QR codes, tags), each in plain words in English and Arabic, and every shop has its own switches. Owners and admins open Catalog, Features to switch a feature on or off, see what each changes and where and how many items use it, name the section ("Menu", "Products", "Price list", "My shop") and choose the order page's layout. Switching a feature off hides it and stops it applying to new items; nothing already made is changed or deleted. The item form, the order page (which now really reads the pickup day, quick quantity, "same as last time" and item code settings), the import and export files, the weekly report, the home cards, the WhatsApp card and the alerts follow the features. Every existing shop behaves as before: the migration gave each one its features, and a test compares a shop of each of the eleven business types with a capture of the code before the change. The fields `report_lines` and the four per-type layout flags, which nothing read, are gone. Spec `specs/012-feature-engine/spec.md`.
- Documents package 1, the engine, hidden until the screens exist (ALY-138, D-042, spec 011, migration 0034; new dependencies `fpdf2` and `uharfbuzz`, and `pypdfium2` for tests only): a shop can make receipts, invoices, quotes, delivery notes, statements, credit notes, deposit receipts and proformas through the API (`/api/documents`). Each is numbered without gaps per shop, kind and series (prefix and yearly restart are the shop's choice; a failed issue gives its number back; twenty at once never share a number), copies the seller and customer onto itself when issued, is hashed (SHA-256 of canonical JSON, checked by `/verify`), and can then never be changed or deleted: the database refuses, and the only thing that can happen is that a credit note covering all of it voids it. The PDF is drawn on A4, A5 or an 80 mm roll with the brand's fonts, Arabic joined, with diacritics and mixed with Latin names and numbers, Arabic first and mirrored by default; Arabic cannot be switched off for a consumer document; a shop with no commercial registration number issues receipts, not invoices, and each says so. A retried issue with the same key returns the same document. Owners and admins issue and credit; agents make drafts and, if the owner allows, issue their own. Settings (seller, look, logo, colour, language, terms, numbering, tax off by default, cash rounding as its own line on the paper) and a preview that takes no number. A shop's branches each have their own numbering and may print their own address and phone. The module `documents` is off for every shop, in no product and no starting setup; the back office can switch it on for one shop. Not built: screens, sales hooks, sending, export, QR. The PDF is kept in the database, not R2. Arabic is a draft until ALY-66; the Arabic-invoice rule's wording and the retention period are not confirmed (ALY-51).
- The Catalog as a support layer: item codes and ordering by code on WhatsApp (ALY-136, D-039, migration 0033; no new dependency, no new template). Every item gets a short code (101, 102, ... per shop, set by the database, never changed or reused; not the SKU), shown on its card, list row, edit sheet (with a copy button), the order page, the share sheet and its printed card, and in the export (read only). A customer can give the chat an item link, a category link, a code (`214`, `#214`, `code 214`, «رقم ٢١٤», several with quantities), a SKU or a name; the chat answers with the item's card and asks them to confirm, builds one draft order with a live total, and Place order goes through the ordinary order path; a token like `OUD-12` is only ever a SKU, never a name search (the audit's hazard). Price and stock are read again before the order and any change is said; voice messages, pictures and "talk to a person" go to the team inbox; too many unknown codes hand the sender to a person. On the order page, anyone with a shop's link can pick items and tap Order on WhatsApp: the message is a readable order for a shop that answers itself (the default) or the codes (under 140 characters) for a shop whose number runs on ALYR; Catalog settings gets "How customers order". The chat simulator takes a shop's address, so the demo shops can be talked to. Examples (sample items) have no code until kept. Not tried: a real phone, Instagram's in-app browser, Meta.
- The Catalog is switched on (ALY-113 part 1, D-039, migration 0032; no new dependency): every new business starts with its type's own catalog (`ALYR_CATALOG_FOR_NEW_SHOPS` is on by default and can be set to false), and every business that already existed except travel and documents gets the Catalog module, a wholesaler also the orders module; nothing else about them changes, a shop the back office had switched off stays off, and the back office can switch the Catalog off or on for one business (`POST /api/admin/businesses/{id}/modules/catalog`). The sentences onboarding shows each type were proved on the finished order pages and workspace in a real browser in English and Arabic, and the ones that promised more than the screens do were corrected (a size's own photo, who gets the stock alerts, what a shared link needs). "Coming" is gone from the workspace, onboarding and customer pages: ALYR Payroll is no longer listed until it is built, the locked deposit and "more than one customer" rows are gone, the payment provider says "Set up with ALYR", the test pay page no longer promises a gateway, and a test fails if such wording comes back. Changes to the Catalog's settings, staff numbers, price groups and categories are now on the activity record with who made them. The Connections screen's button no longer sits on its text at phone width. Still open: the public site's wording, hosting, the six Meta templates and the Arabic review.
- Catalog package 9, scenario tests, a demo shop for every type of business and the phone-width pass, hidden until go-live (ALY-110, D-039; no migration, no new dependency): a table of the 30 plan scenarios against their tests (`specs/009-catalog-scenarios/spec.md`); four races of the plan (two customers and the last item, two staff counting one shelf, a hold running out as a payment arrives, a double click on pay) now proved to be concurrent on real Postgres, a 50-round stress test of stock, and mutation checks that found a double cancel nobody tested; seven demo shops (`scripts/demo.sh shops`, `docs/demo.md`) with drawn tiles, English and Arabic items and a fortnight of orders; the sample bakery's cancelled credit orders now have their reversal (and an old one is repaired); phone fixes found in a browser pass (controls under 44 px, a cut-off shop name, clamped item names, warning and dark-mode button contrast, aligned paired fields, Arabic month and actor names, a capital on the day chip).
- Security: the booking page no longer trusts a bare contact ID (ALY-133, D-039). A customer's booking link now carries a signed token made for booking, and the link's token says what it is for, so an order link that is forwarded (the contact ID can be read out of it) opens nothing on the booking page and a booking link opens nothing on the order page. An expired, edited or old link gives a page that says so in English or Arabic and says to ask the shop for a new one, with a WhatsApp button when the shop's number is known; the public booking link shows the services and free times to anyone and cannot book. The pay and tracking pages, which open by a random ID, are written down as safe and no longer cached, and the pay page shows only the payer's first name. Links sent before this change (order links included) stop working: ask for a new one in the chat. No new dependency, no migration.
- Catalog package 4, the customer order page, share links and QR codes, hidden until go-live (ALY-112, D-039, migration 0029): a shop with the Catalog on gets a new order page with photos, English and Arabic descriptions, search that finds Arabic in any common spelling, category chips, sizes and choices with named errors, steppers that honour the smallest order and pack size, "only 3 left" only when true, sold-out items greyed or left out, plain words for hours and cut-offs, a refresh every 20 to 30 seconds, a review that shows a price or stock change before payment, "same as last time" that names what is gone, a compact wholesale table for a price group, the layout the shop's type of business chooses (menu, photo grid, compact list or table, and the section's name), and amounts in Arabic as «24 ر.ق». The pay page says when an order was released and offers a way back. A customer's own link now carries a signed token (`ALYR_LINK_SECRET`; the API will not start without it outside a developer machine) and a bare contact ID in a link or a request grants nothing; public links (an item, a category, a table, a QR code) carry no customer and send an ordering customer to the shop's WhatsApp. The workspace Catalog gets Share (copy, WhatsApp, QR codes for the counter and tables, SVG, PNG, print) and three settings; orders show the table. A shop without the Catalog keeps its page. New dependencies: `qrcode-generator` (the QR codes), `jsqr` (a test).
- Fix: two requests that were the first to ask a shop for its default price group at the same moment could collide on the group's name and fail one of them (seen once in an existing test while building ALY-112); the second now reads the first's group.
- Catalog package 6, business-type templates, hidden until go-live (ALY-107, D-039, migration 0031): each type of business starts with its own catalog, named in English and Arabic (Menu for a restaurant and a bakery, Products for a shop, a grocery, a fashion seller and the appointment businesses, Price list for wholesale), with its own starting rule for new items, low-stock number, sold-out look, WhatsApp alerts, order-page layout hints and categories, and believable sample items that are marked Sample, carry example prices, are never for sale and are removed all at once. Three new types: bakery and home kitchen, grocery and baqala, fashion and gifts. A wholesaler also gets the orders module, so its customers have an order page in the table layout and can order on account at their group's price. Travel and documents have no catalog. Onboarding's review says what the owner is getting. One setting, `ALYR_CATALOG_FOR_NEW_SHOPS` (default off), decides whether any of it happens; off, onboarding is exactly as before and the new types are not offered. Spec `specs/008-catalog-business-types/spec.md`.
- Catalog package 8, the Catalog in the weekly report, on the home screen and in the back office, hidden until go-live (ALY-109, D-039, migration 0030): the report gains a Catalog section (what sold out and for how long, customers who asked for something sold out, what is low now, the best sellers by units and by money, what did not sell; a cancelled order is not a sale; each line only when it has something to say; English and Arabic); the home screen and ALYR Agent show "Sold out now" and "Low stock", each the number of rows in the Catalog list it opens; the back office has a read-only Usage view (items, photos, gigabytes and uploads per business and month, the total against R2's free 10 GB with its 80% alert, no prices). Customers who try to order a sold-out item are now recorded, once a day per item. Also: the workspace remembers the language a person chose on that device (a link inside it sent an Arabic reader back to English).
- Catalog package 5, the Catalog on WhatsApp, hidden until go-live (ALY-106, D-039): registered staff numbers change the Catalog by message in English or Arabic (sold out, back, stock, price, undo; a photo with a caption becomes a draft), ALYR says what it understood and offers Undo, asks which item when several fit and never guesses; a customer who types an item's name gets its photo, price and an Order button; "tell me when it is back" asks once and messages once; managers are told of low stock, sold-out items, drafts waiting and refunds due; an item can have its own low-stock number. Six message templates added, not submitted. Staff numbers are registered in the Catalog settings. Migration 0027.
- Catalog 2b, customer price groups (wholesale) and SKU, hidden until go-live (ALY-127, D-039, migration 0026): a shop groups its customers (Retail, Wholesale, Staff...), gives a group its own price for an item or a size (the normal price where it has none, with a history), and the customer's group decides the price on the order page, in the cart check, in the order and on its payment link; the order keeps the group's name and every line's price. Staff set a customer's group from the conversation in the inbox; groups and prices are managed in the Catalog's settings and edit sheet. Items and sizes can have a SKU, unique in the shop, searchable and shown on orders. Row-level security and isolation tests on every new table; the order page's API is documented in `specs/003-price-groups-and-sku/spec.md` for ALY-112.
- Catalog package 7, import and export, hidden until go-live (ALY-108, D-039): download a template for your kind of business, or every item, as Excel or CSV with English or Arabic column names; upload a file and read a preview of every row (added, updated, left out, with the row number and the reason in your language) before anything is saved; names that already exist are warned about, never merged; stock numbers go into the stock log as "Import"; SKU of an item and of each size, a clash reported for the row that causes it; the item's own low-stock number (empty keeps it; customer price groups are left out on purpose); an item that runs out by a number each day needs that number for every size, and a preview that says so no longer fails when applied; an import sends the owner no low-stock or sold-out alert for each item: after it, the managers get one message saying how many items are low or sold out (inside their 24-hour window, not at night); photos are matched by file name from a zip; each import is on the activity record with who did it, and the newest can be undone once. Excel is read and written with the standard library, so no new dependency. A new table, `item_imports` (migration 0028).
- Fix: cancelling an order that was put on a customer's credit account now takes its sale off the customer's ledger, once, whoever cancels (the customer, staff or the hold sweep). The ledger keeps both entries, the customer is told their balance changed (plain text inside the 24-hour window, the existing `entry_recorded` template outside it), and the aged-debt buckets, the weekly report and the Agent no longer count the order (ALY-128, migration 0025).
- Catalog package 3, the workspace screens, hidden until go-live (ALY-104, D-039): the owner's items as photos or a list with Arabic-aware search, filters and a switch on every row; an edit sheet with photos, sizes, extras, times and history; adding many items from photos with a draft for each and a walk through them; a Stock tab with counts that show the difference and need a reason; bulk actions with a preview and one undo; roles. English and Arabic, light and dark, phone first. A new `catalog` module, off for every shop. The API gained photos on items, drafts from photos, bulk actions with undo and the agents setting (ALY-103's follow-up, migration 0024).
