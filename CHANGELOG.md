# Changelog

What changed for customers and for the team, newest first. One line per merged pull request
that changes behaviour; internal-only work can be grouped. Versions follow phases
(`0.<phase>.<n>`), tagged on main when a phase demo works (docs/hygiene.md).

## Unreleased
- Security fix: Pillow 13 (ALY-243; no migration, no new dependency): the photo library had 38 published vulnerabilities in 12.3.0, all fixed in 13.0.0, and certifi moves to 2026.10.23. The Python vulnerability check in CI and `scripts/check.sh` audited its own tool instead of our locked packages, so it passed while those were open; it now audits the lock file.
- Interim hosting on Cloudflare and Neon, not deployed (ALY-224, D-052, spec 021; no migration; new dependencies: `@opennextjs/cloudflare` in the web app's dev dependencies to build the portal for Cloudflare Workers, and `@cloudflare/containers` with `wrangler` in `infra/cloudflare/api` to run the API's container). The API has a Dockerfile and an entrypoint that starts it with the job runner; the portal builds with `pnpm portal:build`; CI builds the image and starts it against PostgreSQL. Only the fictional demo shop may go on it until the founder decides about real clients.
- Documents package 5, connected mode, still hidden behind the off module (ALY-142, D-042, spec 016, migration 0043; no new dependency): a shop that keeps another POS or accounting tool can send its sales to ALYR so its customers get ALYR's receipt or invoice on WhatsApp, and chooses **for each kind of document who owns the numbers**. With the other system owning a kind, ALYR never gives that kind a number of its own: the document carries the other system's number, says on its face that it was issued by the seller's own system and delivered by ALYR, ALYR's counters do not move, and ALYR's own automatic documents for that kind are skipped (and say so on the record), so nothing is invoiced twice; the database holds both systems to it. A sale is sent to `POST /api/connect/sales` with an API key and an Idempotency-Key, and is made once whatever the network does (the same sale twice, or six at once, is one document; the same key with another body is refused); the total ALYR works out must agree with the till's within a minor unit a line; the customer is found by phone or made; lines are linked to Catalog items by SKU without moving stock. API keys per shop (made and revoked by an owner or admin, shown once, kept as a hash, scopes, a rate limit per key with 429 and Retry-After, every management action on the record) never open the workspace or another shop. The shop confirms, once, that it has its customers' permission to be sent their receipts; ALYR records that as a consent from `connected_sale` with the key, never overrides a customer who said stop, and, because the three document templates are not yet approved by Meta, says "not delivered: template not approved" outside the 24-hour window and falls back to email. A CSV or Excel export of sales can be imported: columns found by name in English or Arabic, a mapping that can be remembered, a preview of every row with its number in both languages, one transaction to apply it through the same door as the API, and an undo of the newest import that withdraws its documents (kept, marked, never deleted) while nothing in it was sent. Webhooks (`document.issued`, `.delivered`, `.failed`, `.cancelled`, `payment.recorded`) are signed (HMAC-SHA256 with a timestamp), retried eight times over ten hours, logged with the last answer, switched off after a day of failures with an email to the owners, carry no customer data, and are guarded against sending to a private network. One screen in the workspace (Connections, Connect another system), phone first, English and Arabic, light and dark; developer guide `docs/connect-api.md`. Not tried: a real WhatsApp message, email, phone or POS; the Arabic is a draft until ALY-66; the templates are not submitted.

Share (a link to copy, send by WhatsApp or email to the customer or another number), Send again, Record payment taken by hand, Download, Print on A4, A5 or an 80 mm roll, Cancel with a reason (the sheet says what happens: a credit note, the document stays with its number). **Write one by hand**: a customer found among the shop's own or typed, lines from the Catalog (priced for the customer's group) or free text, live totals worked as the engine works them, save a draft, Issue with a confirmation that names the number; an invoice with no CR number is refused in friendly words with a link to the settings field and an offer to make a receipt. **Settings** for owners and admins: business, look (paper, colour, logo, stamp, what shows, header and footer), language and the consumer rule, notes and terms per kind, numbering with the next number and branches, currency and cash rounding, what happens by itself, the team's rights, the recent changes, and a live preview drawn by the engine from what is being typed, before it is saved, taking no number. **Home**: two cards on the ALYR Agent dashboard, Unpaid invoices and Documents not delivered, each the size of the list it opens. **Orders and Credit accounts** rows point at the receipt or invoice they made. Not tried on a real phone, a real printer or a real send; the Arabic is a draft until ALY-66.

The item form has a unit picker (piece, pack, box, portion, hour, kg, g, litre, ml, metre, tola) with a step and a smallest order; the stock list, the count sheet and the history show and take decimals (a count of 11.75 against 12.5 says "Difference: -0.75 kg"). - Decimal and weight quantities, the first Catalog feature pack, part 1: storage, the stock engine, the checkout, the API and the files (ALY-149, D-039, migration 0043; no new dependency). A quantity is now an exact decimal with three places everywhere: an order line, a hold, the stock log, a size's stock and daily limit, an item's smallest order, step and low-stock number. A customer can order 0.5 kg of dates or 250 ml of oud, a shop can hold 12.5 kg, and a line is its price times its quantity rounded half up to a dirham, once per line, the same rule the Documents engine uses. Units: piece, pack, box, portion and hour stay whole; kg, g, litre, ml, metre and tola may be sold in fractions, in steps and with a smallest order the owner sets (0.25 kg). A new switchable feature, "Sold by weight or volume", is on for a new grocery or bakery and off for every other type; every shop that exists keeps its form (the migration writes it off), and a kilo item sold in whole numbers reads "3" as it always did. The stock engine's single conditional update, the holds, the daily limits and the restock on cancel work in decimals (the 50-round stress test passes unchanged, and a second one runs in quarters of a kilo); the database refuses a fraction on a counted unit; a wrong amount gets a plain sentence in both languages ("Sold in steps of 0.25 kg"). The import and export files carry decimals, the new units have words in both languages, and a file read back is no change. The screens and the WhatsApp chat follow in part 2. The Arabic is a draft until ALY-66.
- The Catalog's pricing pack: discounts and coupon codes, delivery and pickup, cost price and margin (ALY-150, D-039, spec 014, migrations 0040, 0041 and 0042; no new dependency, no new template). Three features of the Features screen, **off for every shop until switched on**, so nothing changes for any shop that does not. **Discounts**: coupon codes a customer types (percentage, amount, or buy N get M free; the whole order or chosen items and categories; a minimum order; total and per-customer limits; dates; a price group; stackable or not) and automatic discounts; the discount shown line by line and in the total before payment and kept as a snapshot on the order; a limited code cannot be over-used, and cancelling an order gives its use back; a Discounts screen for owners; a coupon box on the order page and "coupon CODE" in the WhatsApp order, in English and Arabic; a line in the weekly report. **Delivery**: pickup or delivery, areas with a fee, a free-over amount, a minimum, days and a cut-off, a flat fee; the fee is its own line, kept on the order; staff see how each order reaches the customer; the chat asks "Pickup or delivery?"; a report line; no maps and no driver dispatch. **Cost and margin**: a cost for each item and size with its history, the margin it leaves, a warning (never a block) when a price is under the cost, what an order cost at the time, the value of the stock, cost columns in Excel and CSV, report lines; **only owners and admins ever see a cost or a margin** (costs live in tables of their own, and a test walks every place an agent or a customer can read). Totals shown before payment equal what is charged (a property test of random carts). The goods never go below QAR 1. Documents made from an order carry its discounts and delivery fee. Arabic is a draft until ALY-66.
- Documents package 2, receipts and invoices from sales and delivery to the customer, still hidden (ALY-139, D-042, spec 013, migration 0038; no new dependency): for a shop with the `documents` module on, an order paid by link gets a receipt, an order put on a customer's credit account gets an invoice, a payment on a credit account gets a receipt that says what is left, and an order cancelled after its receipt or invoice was issued gets a credit note, once, linked to the original, in the cancellation's own transaction (a payment reported twice or six times at once makes one document; a document that cannot be made never stops the sale and says why on the record; a shop without the module is untouched). Staff can write down a payment taken by hand (cash, bank transfer, cheque, card terminal, with a reference, who and when) for an order, a booking (a deposit receipt for part, a receipt for the rest), an invoice or a customer's balance, and the receipt follows; owners and admins, and agents only if the shop allows it. An invoice can be asked for on any order or booking, and an issued document cancelled with a reason (a credit note; the document stays, void). Each document goes to the customer: inside their 24-hour window as a WhatsApp document message with a short caption in their language, outside it as the PDF in the header of a utility template; by email with the PDF attached when the customer has an address on file (never to an example domain); with a link to a hosted page (`/d/<shop>?t=...`, a signed token made for that page only, 90 days, no bare ID) that shows the document and a Download PDF button in English and Arabic, and a calm "expired" or "not valid" page otherwise. Consent and the window apply like any message; a document that fails is retried with growing waits and then shows the owner "not delivered"; staff can resend to the customer or to another number or address, and the shop chooses what goes out on its own (receipts, invoices, deposit receipts; WhatsApp, email; at once or only when staff press send). Three templates (`document_receipt`, `document_invoice`, `document_credit_note`, English and Arabic) are written and **not submitted to Meta**. Bookings have no payment step, so a booking's receipt comes from a payment recorded by hand. API only: no screen (ALY-146). Not tried: a real WhatsApp send, a real email, a real phone; Arabic is a draft until ALY-66.
- The Catalog's feature engine, second part (ALY-148, D-039, migrations 0036 and 0037; no new dependency): **a shop's own fields**, **changing a shop's type**, and **templates made in the back office**. An owner opens Catalog, Features, Manage fields, and adds fields such as Serves, Warranty months or Concentration (text, a number with a unit, one from a list, yes or no, a date), for the item or for each size or colour, and chooses whether customers see them and whether they are searched or needed; fields appear in the item form, the items list's search, the customer's item sheet, the Excel and CSV files and the import, and an order keeps what the customer saw. - The Catalog's feature engine, first part (ALY-148, D-039, migration 0035; no new dependency): the Catalog is one system and a kind of business is a configuration of it. A registry lists what the Catalog can do (sizes and colours, choices and extras, counted stock, a number each day, times it can be ordered, a pickup day, price groups, pack sizes, quick quantity, "same as last time", the item code, low-stock and sold-out messages, "tell me when it is back", share links, QR codes, tags), each in plain words in English and Arabic, and every shop has its own switches. - Documents package 1, the engine, hidden until the screens exist (ALY-138, D-042, spec 011, migration 0034; new dependencies `fpdf2` and `uharfbuzz`, and `pypdfium2` for tests only): a shop can make receipts, invoices, quotes, delivery notes, statements, credit notes, deposit receipts and proformas through the API (`/api/documents`). Each is numbered without gaps per shop, kind and series (prefix and yearly restart are the shop's choice; a failed issue gives its number back; twenty at once never share a number), copies the seller and customer onto itself when issued, is hashed (SHA-256 of canonical JSON, checked by `/verify`), and can then never be changed or deleted: the database refuses, and the only thing that can happen is that a credit note covering all of it voids it. The PDF is drawn on A4, A5 or an 80 mm roll with the brand's fonts, Arabic joined, with diacritics and mixed with Latin names and numbers, Arabic first and mirrored by default; Arabic cannot be switched off for a consumer document; a shop with no commercial registration number issues receipts, not invoices, and each says so. A retried issue with the same key returns the same document. Owners and admins issue and credit; agents make drafts and, if the owner allows, issue their own. Settings (seller, look, logo, colour, language, terms, numbering, tax off by default, cash rounding as its own line on the paper) and a preview that takes no number. A shop's branches each have their own numbering and may print their own address and phone. The module `documents` is off for every shop, in no product and no starting setup; the back office can switch it on for one shop. Not built: screens, sales hooks, sending, export, QR. The PDF is kept in the database, not R2. Arabic is a draft until ALY-66; the Arabic-invoice rule's wording and the retention period are not confirmed (ALY-51).
