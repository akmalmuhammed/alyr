# Slides and social posts

Decks and posts are where ALYR is most tempted to exaggerate: there are no customers, no
numbers and no photos of real shops yet. The brand's answer is the same as on the site: the
product is the illustration, honesty is design, one idea at a time. Growth drafts; the
founder approves and publishes (`docs/lanes/growth.md`).

## Before you start

1. Load `alyr-design`; read `alyr-design/honesty.md` with today's `docs/status.md`.
2. Ask who it is for and where it will be seen: an owner at the counter (the deck on a
   phone or laptop), a programme reviewer, a scrolling owner on Instagram.
3. Check with the founder before making anything that needs a person on camera, a real shop,
   a price, a paid boost or a posting date.
4. If a slide type exists in the Artifact or Slides tooling the session offers, use it; carry
   the rules below into it.

## Formats

| Format | Size | Margins | Words |
|---|---|---|---|
| Slide | 1920 × 1080 | 96 px | One idea, at most 25 words, headline under 8 words |
| Feed post, carousel slide | 1080 × 1350 | 64 px safe | One idea a slide; a carousel 6 to 8 slides |
| Story, reel cover, TikTok | 1080 × 1920 | 64 px, plus the platform's top and bottom bars (keep text out of the top 250 and bottom 340 px) | A line or two |
| LinkedIn | 1200 × 1500 | 64 px | English first, Arabic second |

## The look, in this brand

- **Paper or Carbon** grounds, never cobalt, sand or a gradient. Light first; Carbon for the
  one command slide (the product, the call to action).
- **The 12-column grid** scaled to the canvas, hairline rules, the section rule with its
  cross, FIG captions on every product image.
- **Type:** Instrument Sans for headlines and figures (tabular), Plex Mono for labels, Plex
  Sans Arabic for Arabic, set about 10% larger than the Latin beside it. On a 1080 canvas
  shown at phone size, nothing under 32 px; headlines 72 to 120 px.
- **Volt** for the one signal on a slide (the confirmed step, the one number), never a
  background; one Volt element per slide.
- **The 45° fold and cut corner** as the only graphic device: a cut on the image plate, the
  fold's planes on the cover and the closing slide. The logo from the master files, never
  retyped, at least 140 px wide (the symbol below that).
- **The product is the picture:** real screens from the product or the site's drawings, at a
  readable scale, captioned "FIG · … · Fictional bakery". No icons as illustration, no stock
  photos, no skylines, dhows, coffee pots or arabesque, no 3D phones, no AI imagery, no emoji.
- **Motion** in reels: the brand's one gesture (arrive along 45°, settle, one Volt tick),
  plays once; captions burnt in for sound-off viewing, in the video's language.

## Words

- ALYR's voice (`alyr-design/voice.md`): the owner's outcome, short, a number or an example,
  calm. "We do the tech. You run the business." is the master line for decks.
- **Arabic register:** MSA on slides, documents and LinkedIn; light Gulf on Instagram,
  TikTok, Snapchat and WhatsApp status where the brief asks for it. Written in Arabic first,
  never machine-translated, reviewed by a native speaker before it is published. Start with a
  greeting where a person speaks («هلا»، «السلام عليكم»), never a cold pitch. No religious
  phrases in sales copy outside Ramadan and Eid greetings.
- Western digits; «ر.ق» after the amount; ALYR feminine, products masculine; product names
  in Latin with their descriptors.
- **Captions** under posts: what the post shows, one line on why it matters, one clear next
  step ("Message us", "See alyr.qa"). Hashtags few and local; no tagging of competitors.

## Honesty on slides and posts

Everything in `alyr-design/honesty.md`, plus:
- **No numbers about ALYR's customers, results or usage**: there are none yet. A "before and
  after" uses a fictional shop and says so on the image; a real merchant's numbers only with
  their written permission and the founder's yes.
- **No testimonials or merchant faces** until a real merchant agrees in writing.
- **Market facts** (WhatsApp use in Qatar, delivery-app commissions) only with a named,
  dated source in the speaker notes or the caption.
- **No prices** on a deck or post until the founder says the CR is issued; then the decided
  wording only ("From QAR 449 a month for your first product…").
- Deck slides from the playbook that are retired: "Set up in 48 hours", three plans, "local
  team", "licensed payment partner", "your data stays in Qatar", a pilot merchant's
  before/after table, founder photo. Replace with what is true: set up by ALYR in your shop,
  the product on the owner's own phone, early access, the go-live date agreed at the visit.
- Never imply WhatsApp or Meta affiliation; no WhatsApp logo as ALYR's; a deck that shows
  WhatsApp screens draws them as the site does.

## The sales deck, in this brand (10 slides at most)

1. Cover: logo, the master line, the contact line in its live state.
2. The owner's day today, in their words (no statistics unless sourced).
3. ALYR: one sentence, the company that sets up and looks after the tech.
4. The suite in order (Chat, Bookings, Agent, Accounts), one line each, Agent included.
5. ALYR Agent: the real dashboard sample, its rule lines visible ("by rules you can check").
6. Live demo: switch to the owner's own phone.
7. How we set it up: the visit, the setup, the training, go-live agreed together.
8. Price (only once the CR is issued and the founder agrees).
9. What is true today: early access, built on WhatsApp's official Business Platform,
   records kept apart, non-custodial.
10. Next step: one action.
