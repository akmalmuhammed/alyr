# Voice and writing

ALYR talks like a capable person explaining something at the counter: short, concrete,
calm, with a number or an example. It names the owner's result.

## Rules

1. **Sentence case** everywhere: headings, buttons, menu items, captions' text. Mono labels
   are set in capitals by CSS in English, never typed in capitals.
2. **One idea a sentence, under 20 words.** Short and declarative. Break a long line into two.
3. **Talk to one person, as "you".** "Your menu, in your customers' chat."
4. **The owner's outcome first,** then how. Numbers over adjectives; examples over claims.
5. **Calm confidence.** No exclamation marks, no hype, no emoji, no rhetorical questions in
   headings except in the questions section.
6. **Links and buttons say what happens:** "Email us", "See ALYR Bookings", "Send
   reminders". Never "Learn more", "Click here", "Get started", "Contact us for pricing".
7. **Never say:** seamless, revolutionary, disrupt, synergy, unlock, supercharge, leverage,
   empower, skyrocket, game-changing, cutting-edge, world-class, best-in-class, AI words
   ([honesty.md](honesty.md)). Never name a competitor (talabat, Snoonu, Keeta, Wati…).
8. **Who speaks.** The site speaks as ALYR ("we"); no founder name or photo. ALYR is the
   agent; ALYR Agent is its screen. A product never speaks in the first person.

| Say | Instead of |

| orders, bookings, payments | transactions, conversions |
| set up, connect, go live | onboard, implement, deploy |
| your team | agents, seats, users |

| payment link | checkout URL, PG |

## House style

- **British spelling:** colour, organise, cancelled, centre, licence (noun).
- **Separator:** the middle dot with spaces, " · ". **No em dashes**; use a full stop,
  a colon or a comma.
- **Numbers:** digits for every number in UI and figures ("3 orders"); "QAR 1,500" with a
  thousands comma; percentages as "8%" in English.
- **Times:** in the site's own text "4:05 PM"; in the dashboard and workspace the product's
  24-hour clock ("16:20"); inside a drawn screen, exactly what that screen prints. One format
  per screen.
- **Dates:** "Thu 15 Oct", "4–10 Oct" (en dash in ranges only, inside `<bdi>` in Arabic).
- **Product names** exactly: ALYR Chat, ALYR Bookings, ALYR Agent, ALYR Accounts,
  ALYR Shelf, ALYR Docs. "ALYR" in capitals always; never "Alyr" or "alyr" on anything public.
  ALYR Shelf is the Catalog's public name and ALYR Docs the Documents module's (founder,
  20 Oct 2026); code, decisions and the workspace keep "Catalog" (Menu, Products, Price list)
  and "Documents". A product that is not built is not named in public (Payroll, D-039).
- **The suite's order** is always Chat, Bookings, Agent, Accounts, Shelf, Docs: menus, rows,
  tags, footers, decks. In a business's tags the two that come with every product (Agent,
  Shelf) close the row, lighter, with a "+".
- Order numbers as "Order #1043" in English and «الطلب رقم 1043» in Arabic.
