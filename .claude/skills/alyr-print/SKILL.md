# A printed piece

Print cannot be corrected after it is made, it costs money, and it sits on a counter for
months. The bar is higher than for a page: everything on it must be true for as long as it
is on the counter, and every Arabic word reviewed by a native speaker first.

## Before you start: four things that need the founder

Stop and label the issue "Needs Akmal" until each is answered; you may design and prepare
the file meanwhile, but nothing goes to a printer.

1. **The logo's new R has not been proofed** (`docs/brand.md`): "the new R also needs the
   designer's proof before print". Any print carrying the ALYR logo waits for it.
2. **Printing costs money** (operating-model rule 8): the printer, the quantity, the paper and
   the price are his choice. Bring two or three options with prices.
3. **The Arabic** needs a native reviewer (ALY-66). An agent's Arabic is a draft.
4. **For a shop's QR card: the number its customers will message.** A shop cannot connect its
   own number yet (ALY-39); which number goes on the card is agreed at setup. No number, no
   card.

Also read `alyr-design` (tokens, Arabic, honesty) and, for a QR card, the router's keywords
(`apps/api/src/alyr/modules/orders/chat.py` `ORDER_WORDS`, `bookings/chat.py` `BOOK_WORDS`).

## Whose piece is it?

| Piece | Brand that leads | ALYR's place |
|---|---|---|

Never the main mark |
| ALYR's leave-behind, one-pager, business card, letterhead | ALYR | The whole system |
| An invoice, receipt or quote | The issuer (the shop for its customers; ALYR for its clients) | ALYR's invoices to clients only after the CR (D-034) |

## The QR card (counter card, table tent, door sticker)

**What the QR opens.** A `https://wa.me/<number>?text=<word>` link to the number the shop's
customers message, with a prefilled word the product already answers:
- ordering: "Order" / «طلب» (in `ORDER_WORDS`), so the reply is the order page link;
- booking: "Book" / «حجز» (in `BOOK_WORDS`), so the reply is the booking link.

A word the router does not know gets the welcome menu or "We will reply shortly". Make one
card per job or one card per language, never a prefill sentence the router cannot match.
Do not put personal data or a tracking parameter in the link.

**Layout, front:**
1. The shop's name, large, Arabic and English (Arabic first on an Arabic-first side).
2. The action, one line: "Order on WhatsApp" / «اطلب عبر واتساب», or "Book a cake tasting"
   / «احجز موعد تذوّق الكيك».
3. The QR code, then the steps in three short numbered lines (they are steps, so numbers are
   right): "1 Scan · 2 Send the message · 3 Pick from the order page".
4. The number written out (Western digits, `+974 5555 0000` grouping), for people who type.
5. The ALYR foot line.

Drawn on the brand's grid: square corners, or the 45° cut on one corner as a printed plane
(a die-cut corner costs more: the founder's choice); hairline rules; the Volt plane only in
the ALYR mark. No WhatsApp logo or WhatsApp green (`alyr-design/honesty.md`); a drawn chat
glyph from the icon set beside the word "WhatsApp" is enough.

**The QR code itself:**
- Dark modules on a light ground: Carbon on Paper or white. Never Volt (too little contrast
  to scan), never inverted, never a logo over the centre.
- Error correction M or Q; a quiet zone of at least 4 modules on every side.
- At least 25 mm square on a counter card or table tent, 40 mm on a door or window sticker
  read from a step away; larger for a window read from the street.
- Generated by code from the exact link (no QR library is in the repo yet: adding one needs
  a one-line reason in the PR, and the founder's agreement if it is new to `docs/stack.md`).
- **Scanned with two phones (iPhone camera and an Android camera) from the printed proof**
  before the run, and the reply checked: the word arrives and the right link comes back.

**Sizes** (choose with the founder): counter card A6 (105 × 148 mm), table tent A6 folded or
100 × 210 mm, window sticker 100 mm square, menu insert A5.

## ALYR's leave-behind (A5, both sides)

The playbook's structure, in this brand: front in English, back in Arabic written first and
mirrored (not translated). Headline in ALYR's voice ("Go digital. Without the IT team." or
the current homepage line), three facts that are true today, one real product screen drawn
at true scale with its FIG caption (a fictional business, captioned), and the contact block
in its live state (WhatsApp, or hello@alyr.qa). **No prices** until the founder confirms the
CR is issued (paper has no offer switch); no "live in 48 hours", no free pilot, no founder
photo or testimonials, no "licensed payment partner".

## Invoices, receipts, quotes

Not built (D-032, D-033; e-invoicing parked, D-008). Before designing one:
- The required fields are a legal question (Company lane, a lawyer): issuer's legal name and
  CR number, the customer, date, number sequence, items, amounts, totals, and whatever
  Qatar's rules and e-invoicing require at the time. Research, then ask; do not guess.
- **Arabic is required** for prices to consumers (Qatar's consumer law, D-039): Arabic first
  or side by side, formal MSA.
- Amounts in QAR with two decimals on legal documents, from integer dirhams; «ر.ق» after the
  amount in Arabic; tabular figures; every amount in one unbroken run.
- Numbers, dates and references in Western digits; dates written out on documents
  («17 أكتوبر 2026»).
- ALYR never appears as the seller on a shop's invoice (non-custodial: the shop sells and is
  paid through its own gateway, `CLAUDE.md` hard rule 3).

## Print-ready files

- **Bleed 3 mm** on every edge, **safe margin 5 mm** inside the trim for anything that must
  not be cut; QR quiet zone counts from the safe margin.
- **Type:** fonts embedded or outlined in the PDF; Latin body at least 8 pt, the action line
  at least 14 pt, Arabic set about 10% larger than the Latin beside it so they look equal; no
  tracking in Arabic.
- **Colour:** Carbon prints as a rich black for large areas and 100% black for small text;
  Volt is outside most CMYK presses' range, so ask the printer for a proof or a spot-colour
  match and check it by eye against the screen (the choice of a spot colour is the founder's).
  Never print grey text below the text-muted token's contrast.
- **Paper:** matte, uncoated or lightly coated, so it does not glare under shop lights; heavy
  enough to stand (a table tent).
- Export PDF/X with crop marks; keep the source (SVG or the code that draws it) in the work
  folder and say where in the PR. Never commit a scan of a real document.

## Don't

- Print anything that could change soon: a price, a promise, a "coming" product, a feature
  not live (card payments), opening hours the shop has not confirmed.
- Use a real shop's name on a mock-up without its agreement; mock-ups use Qirfa Bakery and
  say "Sample".
- Put ALYR's logo larger than the shop's on the shop's own card.
