# Roadmap and operating cadence reference

## Product evolution

### Now

- Keep Growth OS local and evidence-led.
- Improve source, website, and public-contact quality.
- Define narrow ICP experiments by category and municipality.
- Create transformation snapshots and internal qualification tasks.
- Run small permissioned campaigns only after human review.
- Establish the first real client outcome, delivery workflow, and baseline KPIs.

### Next

- Authentication, role scopes, organization boundary, owner assignment, tasks, and reminders
- Saved views, canonical account identity, contact expiry/re-verification, and KPI baselines
- Signed webhooks, durable queue, retry/dead-letter controls, and automation observability
- Consent service and governed channel adapters
- First-party discovery, proposal, onboarding, and delivery templates

### Later

- Managed PostgreSQL, encrypted object storage, and a semantic metrics layer
- SSO, audit export, managed backups, disaster recovery, and security monitoring
- Cross-business command center, partner/vendor portals, and governed AI agents
- Category-specific products once repeated demand and delivery evidence justify them

## Daily founder brief - read only

Call command dashboard and market summary. Report only meaningful exceptions:

1. decisions waiting now;
2. new or changed high-priority accounts;
3. approvals, replies, meetings, proposals, or blockers;
4. source or agent health issues;
5. the three highest-value actions for today.

Do not create work merely to make the brief look active.

## Research block - controlled writes

Choose one narrow category/location hypothesis. Inspect at most 25 prospects, fully read at most 10, and recommend at most five. Add only verified public contacts. Create internal tasks when permission is unknown. End with evidence quality and next human decisions.

## Weekly founder review - read only

Compare available event and entity state with the prior period only when timestamps support the comparison. Cover:

- funnel movement and stagnation;
- category potential versus actual traction;
- outreach quality, response, objections, and suppressions;
- client/project outcomes, health, blockers, and due milestones;
- data freshness, verification, and automation reliability;
- what to stop, start, continue, and test next week.

## Monthly strategy review

Review ICP, category economics, entry offers, sales cycle, conversion, delivery capacity, service margins where confirmed, and roadmap priorities. Recommend productization only when evidence repeats across multiple accounts.

## Scheduling principles

Hermes cron sessions are fresh. Attach this skill and set the absolute project workdir so `.hermes.md` loads. Scheduled jobs should default to read-only. Do not schedule external outreach execution. Pin a reviewed model/provider and keep daily and weekly outputs short.
