# ALYR company and offer reference

## Positioning

ALYR Digital Transformation is a Qatar-based end-to-end transformation partner. It studies how a business attracts customers, sells, delivers services, manages information, and measures performance. It then redesigns the connected operating model using product design, websites and portals, CRM, automation, AI, cloud, data, cybersecurity, IT, marketing technology, and operational support.

Do not reduce ALYR to a website agency, generic AI consultancy, marketing agency, or software house. A website can be the starting surface, but the intended outcome is a measurable business operating system.

## Core engagement sequence

1. **Discover** - current-state audit, owner objectives, customer journey, workflow, systems, data, risks, metrics, and constraints.
2. **Blueprint** - target operating model, prioritized opportunities, architecture, roadmap, budget scenarios, KPI baselines, and governance.
3. **Build** - high-impact customer surface and operational backbone: website/portal, CRM, workflows, data, integrations, and security controls.
4. **Activate** - content, campaigns, training, migration, adoption, playbooks, and launch support.
5. **Optimize** - analytics, automation, experiments, service levels, AI augmentation, and continuous improvement.

| Beauty or wellness | Scheduling, reminders, repeat-customer automation, and review workflow |

| Education or training | Enrollment, parent/learner journey, CRM, and learner operations |
| Construction, interiors, home services | Quote-to-project workflow, client portal, and delivery visibility |

| Weak general digital evidence | Evidence-led transformation audit and 90-day digital operating blueprint |

When website evidence shows a more specific gap, prefer it over the generic category offer: booking gap, WhatsApp-to-CRM, bilingual journey, measurement/attribution, or trust/privacy foundation.

## Qualification principles

Prioritize accounts with a visible, defensible gap; a plausible operational impact; a reachable business decision-maker role; and an entry offer ALYR can demonstrate quickly. Do not claim the company has a problem solely because a website feature was not detected. Present it as a hypothesis to validate.

## Brand voice

Modern, premium, clear, calm, ambitious, and human. Use concrete business language. Avoid buzzword stacks, robotic wording, fear-based cybersecurity claims, and promises of total automation. ALYR earns trust through evidence, method, governance, and practical next steps.
