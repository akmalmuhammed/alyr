# ALYR Main — SMB Digitalization Platform

This is the visible ALYR workspace: brand, design system, product and growth tooling for ALYR Digital Transformation.

## Start here

1. [`ALYR_Brand_HQ/`](ALYR_Brand_HQ/) — strategy, positioning, operating model, transformation pipeline, UX principles, governance and handoff rules.
2. [`ALYR_Design_System/`](ALYR_Design_System/) — approved Carbon Volt + Monochrome brand system, Command Fold logo, cut-Y wordmark, fonts policy, icons, tokens, UX assets, website assets, social assets and showcase.
3. [`ALYR_Lead_Intelligence/`](ALYR_Lead_Intelligence/) — lead intelligence product for prospect discovery and transformation outreach.

## ALYR purpose

ALYR helps Qatar/GCC SMBs move from fragmented manual operations to measurable digital systems: better workflows, websites, CRM, portals, automation, AI, data, cybersecurity, marketing and operating visibility.

## Repository separation

- `ALYR_Main/` is the ALYR SMB digitalization business and its products/assets.
- `Heldat_Logo_System/`, `asset-continuity-*`, `heldat-site/`, `packages/` and `supabase/` are the separate Heldat product workspace.
- `client-work/` contains client engagements and prototypes, not ALYR brand masters.
- `LUCID_Logo_System/` contains a separate client identity system.

## Brand source of truth

Never redraw or retype the approved ALYR logo. Use the masters and rules in `ALYR_Design_System/`.
