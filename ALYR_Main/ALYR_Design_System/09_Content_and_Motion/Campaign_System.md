# Campaign System

## Campaign architecture

Every campaign is built from four layers:

1. **Master identity:** unchanged logo, Carbon/Monochrome foundation.
2. **Campaign signal:** one idea expressed through Volt, a diagram or a proof number.
3. **Content format:** point of view, operating model, proof or invitation.
4. **Conversion path:** one landing page and one specific next action.

## Approved campaign headlines

- Your workflow is the product.
- Make every handoff visible.
- Automate the delay, not the judgment.
- Growth needs an operating system.
- AI without workflow redesign is another disconnected tool.
- From manual follow-up to measurable delivery.

## Visual rule

Campaigns may introduce photography, client-industry colors or data graphics, but the ALYR mark and primary CTA remain in the core system. No campaign may introduce a new permanent logo color.

## Proof standard

Quantified claims must identify whether they are actual, projected, benchmarked or illustrative. Obtain client permission before naming a company or showing identifiable work.
