# ALYR Logo Usage Rules

## Master identity

The ALYR identity combines two locked assets:

- **F1 Command Fold:** a three-plane symbol representing diagnosis, redirection and forward execution.
- **ALYR wordmark:** a custom geometric construction with a distinctive sliced/cut Y and open R architecture.

The master artwork is stored in `01_Master_SVG`. Never recreate it with a font, redraw the Y, or extract it from a screenshot.

## Logo hierarchy

1. **Primary horizontal logo:** default for websites, proposals, signatures and co-branding.
2. **Secondary vertical lockup:** narrow and centered formats.
3. **Standalone symbol:** avatars, favicons, app icons and small spatial markers.
4. **Wordmark:** controlled layouts where the symbol already appears nearby.
5. **AY monogram:** editorial and event support asset; never a replacement for the master logo in first-contact applications.

## Color variants

- **Carbon Volt:** default on White, Paper or Mist.
- **Reversed:** White wordmark/symbol with Volt signal plane on Carbon.
- **Monochrome Black:** one-color print, engraving, stamps, legal and low-color production.
- **White monochrome:** single-color reverse where Volt cannot be reproduced.

## Clear space

Define **X** as the diagonal stroke thickness of the custom A.

- Horizontal logo: minimum `1.5X` on every side.
- Vertical lockup: minimum `2X` on every side.
- Symbol/app icon: minimum `0.75X` within the outer container.
- Co-branding: at least `3X` between ALYR and a partner mark, separated by a neutral rule when required.

## Minimum size

| Asset | Digital | Print |
|---|---:|---:|
| Horizontal logo | 140px wide | 35mm wide |
| Vertical logo | 92px wide | 24mm wide |
| Standalone symbol | 18px | 6mm |
| Wordmark | 92px wide | 24mm wide |

Below 24px, use the optimized app/favicon symbol rather than the full lockup.

## Background control

- Use Carbon Volt on clean light backgrounds.
- Use reversed artwork on Carbon or photography with a verified dark quiet area.
- On photography, add a solid Carbon holding device when contrast or visual noise is uncertain.
- Never place Volt directly on yellow-green imagery.
- Do not place the monochrome Black version on Carbon.

## Never

- Change the cut, angle or spacing of the Y.
- Typeset ALYR to approximate the wordmark.
- Rotate, skew, condense or stretch the artwork.
- Recolor individual letters.
- Apply drop shadows, bevels, outlines or uncontrolled gradients.
- Put the mark inside an arbitrary rounded capsule.
- Use the symbol as a repeating wallpaper at high contrast.
- Animate the three planes independently in a way that suggests the logo is breaking apart.

## Motion logo rule

If animated, the planes may align into the Command Fold once, followed by the wordmark revealing along the cut-Y direction. Duration: 480–720ms. Use a restrained quint-out curve and provide a static reduced-motion fallback.
