# Design System Charter

## Purpose

The ALYR Design System makes the company recognizable, credible and fast to operate across sales, consulting, software, marketing and internal delivery. It reduces one-off design decisions and gives teams a shared language for turning complex transformation work into clear experiences.

## Principles

1. **Clarity is premium.** Make difficult systems feel understandable without making them simplistic.
2. **Signal, do not decorate.** Volt exists to carry meaning: action, progress, selection or proof.
3. **Human judgment stays visible.** Avoid anonymous AI imagery, synthetic promises and over-automation language.
4. **Operations before optics.** Screens should help people decide, act, track and recover.
5. **One identity, many contexts.** The same geometry and tokens must work in a proposal, dashboard, social profile and physical sign.
6. **Accessible by default.** Contrast, focus, keyboard operation, reduced motion and readable language are release requirements.
7. **Measured evolution.** New patterns enter the system only after repeated use and review.

## Scope

Included: brand identity, logo, colors, typography, iconography, UI foundations, reusable components, experience patterns, content design, motion, website graphics, social assets, document templates and governance.

Excluded: campaign-specific photography, client co-brand systems, product illustrations and one-off event assets. These can extend the system but must not redefine it.

## Decision roles

| Role | Accountable for |
|---|---|
| Brand owner | Positioning, master logo, public identity approval |
| Design system lead | Tokens, components, quality and release |
| Product designer | Tested patterns and product UX |
| Engineering lead | Implementation fidelity and accessibility |
| Marketing lead | Channel adaptations and message consistency |
| Operations owner | Template adoption and content accuracy |

## Definition of done

A design-system change is done when source files, exports, documentation, examples, accessibility checks, responsive behavior and inventory are updated together.
