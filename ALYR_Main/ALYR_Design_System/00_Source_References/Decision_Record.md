# Identity Decision Record

## Approved mark

- Concept: F1 — Command Fold.
- Wordmark: ALYR custom geometric construction.
- Required feature: sliced/cut Y in every wordmark use.
- Approved compositions: horizontal, vertical, standalone symbol, app/favicon and controlled one-color/reversed variants.

## Approved color direction

- Primary: P5 Carbon Volt.
- Foundation: P12 Monochrome.
- Carbon Volt values: Carbon `#15171A`, Volt `#B7F34A`, Mist `#E9ECEF`.
- Monochrome values: Black `#050505`, White `#FFFFFF`, Concrete `#A7ADB5`.
