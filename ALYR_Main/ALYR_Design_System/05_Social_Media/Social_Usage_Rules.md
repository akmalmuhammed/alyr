# Social Media System

## Profile image

Use the supplied Carbon square avatar with the Command Fold symbol. Never use the full wordmark in a circular platform crop. Keep the profile image unchanged across LinkedIn, Instagram, X, YouTube and WhatsApp Business to compound recognition.

## LinkedIn company banner

The banner is optimized for a company page. Keep essential content away from the lower-left profile overlap and right-edge crop. Review on desktop and mobile after upload because platform crops may change.

## Content composition

Use three repeatable formats:

1. **Point of view:** one decisive statement, high whitespace, one Volt signal.
2. **Operating model:** workflow, before/after or decision diagram.
3. **Proof:** quantified outcome, short context and client-safe evidence.

## Post template grid

- Square: 1080×1080.
- Portrait: 1080×1350 for feed education.
- Story/reel cover: 1080×1920 with a 250px top/bottom safety margin.
- LinkedIn landscape: 1200×627.

## Social copy

- First line must make a clear claim or name a costly problem.
- Use 1–3 short paragraphs before a list.
- Avoid emoji strings, hashtag blocks and fake urgency.
- Close with one specific action.
- Use real diagrams and examples rather than generic AI imagery.

## Campaign color

Maintain Carbon/Monochrome as 90% of the visual field. Volt should point to the key idea, number or action. Semantic red/orange/blue appear only in explanatory product diagrams.
