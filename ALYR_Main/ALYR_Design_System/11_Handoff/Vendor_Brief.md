# ALYR Vendor Brief

## What is locked

- F1 Command Fold geometry.
- Custom ALYR wordmark and cut-Y.
- Carbon Volt and Monochrome palette roles.
- Instrument Sans-led typography architecture.
- Precision industrial minimalism direction.

## Required files to read

1. `01_Brand_Foundation/Brand_Strategy.md`
2. `02_Logo_System/Logo_Usage_Rules.md`
3. `01_Brand_Foundation/Color_System.md`
4. `01_Brand_Foundation/Typography.md`
5. Relevant channel rules.

## Vendor deliverables

- Editable source.
- Production exports.
- Font and image licenses.
- Accessibility/contrast check.
- Print or device proof where applicable.
- List of assumptions and deviations.

## Approval gate

Send concepts as applications, not isolated logos. Every proposal must show at least one light surface, one Carbon surface, one small-size use and one Arabic/RTL use when the channel supports Arabic.
