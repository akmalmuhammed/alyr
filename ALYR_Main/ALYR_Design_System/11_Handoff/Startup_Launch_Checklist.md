# Startup Launch Checklist

## Identity

- [ ] Legal name, Arabic name and tagline approved.
- [ ] Domain, email and social handles reserved.
- [ ] Master logo files stored in a controlled shared location.
- [ ] Trademark and similarity review commissioned in target markets.

## Digital presence

- [ ] Website favicon, metadata, social preview and analytics configured.
- [ ] LinkedIn company profile and banner published.
- [ ] Consistent profile icon on every channel.
- [ ] Google Business Profile created/verified if relevant.
- [ ] English/Arabic language plan approved.

## Product

- [ ] Tokens implemented once and consumed by components.
- [ ] Core components pass accessibility and RTL review.
- [ ] Loading, empty, error, offline and success states designed.
- [ ] AI outputs expose source, review and approval state.
- [ ] Sensitive-data handling is documented.

## Sales and documents

- [ ] Proposal, slide cover, letterhead, email signature and business cards populated with final details.
- [ ] Case study and claim approval process established.
- [ ] CRM templates use the approved voice and status language.

## Governance

- [ ] Brand owner and design system owner named.
- [ ] Release number and changelog active.
- [ ] Vendor brief included in procurement.
- [ ] Quarterly adoption and drift review scheduled.
