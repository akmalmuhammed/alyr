# Document System Rules

## Core formats

- A4 portrait: proposals, audits, reports, letters and statements of work.
- 16:9 landscape: executive presentations and workshops.
- 1050×600 design canvas: 85×55mm business card with bleed added by printer.

## A4 specification

- Outer margin: 18–22mm.
- Reading column: 125–145mm.
- Header zone: 22mm.
- Footer zone: 18mm.
- Body size: 9.5–11pt depending on document density.
- Long tables may use 8.5pt minimum with adequate leading.
- Start major sections on a new page when context changes.

## Presentation specification

- Safe margin: 72px at 1920×1080.
- One decision per slide.
- Headline: 42–64pt.
- Body: 20–28pt.
- Source notes: 12–15pt.
- Maximum default line length: 52 characters.
- Use Carbon slides for openings, decisions and transitions; Paper slides for analysis.

## Proposal hierarchy

1. Cover and confidentiality.
2. Executive reading of the situation.
3. Evidence and current-state friction.
4. Future operating model.
5. Scope, roadmap, governance and measures.
6. Commercials, assumptions and next decision.

## Data and claims

- Label estimates and assumptions.
- Cite external sources near the claim.
- Never expose personal, passport, QID or client-system data.
- Use realistic but fictional data in public templates.

## Logo placement

Use the primary horizontal logo in headers and covers. Keep at least the defined clear space and never use the logo as a page background. The standalone symbol may mark section openings at low frequency.

## Printing

- Request a physical proof for foil, emboss, UV or signage.
- Use the monochrome SVG for engraving and one-color processes.
- Confirm Volt color with a printer proof; do not rely on screen appearance.
