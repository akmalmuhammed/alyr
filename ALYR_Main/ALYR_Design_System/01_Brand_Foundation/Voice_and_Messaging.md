# Voice and Messaging

## Voice

ALYR sounds like a senior operating partner: calm, specific, candid and commercially aware. Lead with the business result, then explain the mechanism. Avoid jargon unless the audience uses it to make a decision.

## Writing rules

- Prefer active voice: “We map the workflow” instead of “The workflow is mapped.”
- Name the result: “Reduce quote turnaround” instead of “Improve efficiency.”
- Keep headlines short and declarative.
- Use sentence case for product UI and most headings.
- Use uppercase only for small navigation labels, metadata and controlled brand statements.
- Never describe ordinary automation as “revolutionary.”
- Never imply AI is autonomous when human approval is required.

## Master messages

- Transform the business. Not just the interface.
- Strategy, systems and execution—connected.
- Make the workflow visible. Make improvement measurable.
- Build the operating system your growth requires.
- AI where it creates leverage. Human judgment where it matters.

## Service narrative

1. **See the real system.** Audit workflows, customer journeys, data and economics.
2. **Design the better model.** Remove friction and define the future operating flow.
3. **Build the enablers.** Implement software, CRM, automation, AI, portals and analytics.
4. **Activate adoption.** Train teams, measure performance and improve continuously.

## CTA vocabulary

Preferred: `Start a transformation audit`, `See the operating model`, `Review the roadmap`, `Book a working session`, `Explore the system`.

Avoid: `Click here`, `Learn more` when a specific destination is known, `Unlock the future`, `Supercharge your business`.

## Error and support tone

State what happened, what remains safe, and how to recover.

- Good: “The document did not upload. Your case is saved—try the PDF again or send it securely to support.”
- Poor: “Something went wrong.”

## Arabic direction

Arabic should be written natively, not translated word-for-word. Use Modern Standard Arabic for corporate content, with Qatar/GCC-natural phrasing where appropriate. Keep technology terms consistent through an approved glossary. All high-risk, legal and public copy requires human linguistic review.
