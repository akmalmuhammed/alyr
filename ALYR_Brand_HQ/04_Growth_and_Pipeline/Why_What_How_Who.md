# Why / What / How / Who

## Why ALYR

Because a business does not become digital when it has a new website. It becomes digital when its customer journey, internal workflow, decisions, data and accountability work as one system.

## What ALYR changes

The visible layer (website, portal, app) and the invisible layer (process, CRM, automation, data, security, operating rhythm).

## How ALYR works

Analyze → redesign → build → automate → activate → measure → improve.

## Who owns what

- Client sponsor: business priority, access, decisions and outcome approval.
- ALYR partner: transformation thesis, prioritization and accountability.
- Product/design: customer and staff experience.
- Engineering/data: architecture, integrations, QA and security.
- Operations/change: training, adoption, workflow ownership and value reporting.

## The client conversation

Lead with the current friction. Show the cost of leaving it unresolved. Connect the proposed system to a measurable outcome. Make the first 30 days concrete. Name the owner and the evidence that will prove progress.
