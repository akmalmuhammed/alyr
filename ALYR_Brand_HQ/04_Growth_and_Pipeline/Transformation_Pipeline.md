# Transformation Pipeline

## Commercial pipeline

| Stage | Exit criteria | Owner | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Target | ICP and account hypothesis defined | Growth | account brief |
| Signal | Current pain, trigger or business event identified | Growth/Research | signal note |
| Discovery | Workflow, tools, people, data and KPIs mapped | Transformation lead | discovery pack |
| Opportunity | Prioritized revenue, efficiency and risk opportunities agreed | Strategy lead | opportunity backlog |
| Proposal | Scope, outcomes, architecture, timeline and commercial model approved | Partner | proposal + decision record |
| Mobilize | Team, access, data, governance and success metrics ready | Delivery lead | kickoff checklist |
| Build | MVP or first workflow is usable by target users | Product lead | release + QA |
| Adopt | Staff/customer usage and operating playbook are live | Change lead | adoption report |
| Optimize | Measured improvement and next backlog agreed | Account lead | value review |

## Delivery tracks

- **Foundation:** audit, data model, identity, access, analytics and governance.
- **Customer:** website, landing pages, CRM intake, portal, messaging and reviews.
- **Operations:** workflows, task routing, scheduling, documents, approvals and dashboards.
- **AI/automation:** only after stable data, explicit ownership and human review boundaries.
- **Growth:** channel strategy, content, SEO, paid acquisition, referral and retention loops.

## Weekly operating cadence

- Monday: pipeline and delivery risks
- Midweek: build/review checkpoint
- Thursday: customer evidence and adoption review
- Friday: KPI movement, decisions and next actions

## Standard artifacts

Brief → discovery notes → current-state map → target-state blueprint → prioritized backlog → architecture decision record → prototype → build → QA → launch → adoption/value report.
