Account and data deletion instructions

Before deletion Delete in the app Request by email What happens

Before deletion

Export records you need and confirm that another owner can manage any household that must continue. Account deletion is different from removing the app from a device.

Delete inside Heldat

  1. Open Vault.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Open Account.
  4. Select Delete account and data.
  5. Review affected households and complete identity confirmation.
  6. Confirm the final deletion request.

If ownership blocks deletion, transfer or close the affected household first. The app will describe the required consequence.

Request deletion by email

Send “Delete my Heldat account” from the account email address to privacy@heldat.app . Include no asset documents or credentials. Identity and household authority will be verified before a destructive operation.

What happens after confirmation

  • Your active session and account access are revoked.
  • Eligible private account data is scheduled for deletion.
  • Shared household records follow the owner and membership decision.
  • Restricted security, audit or backup copies expire under the applicable cycle.
  • You receive confirmation at the verified account address.

See the retention section of the Privacy Notice for more detail.
