Contact Heldat

Choose the address that best matches your request. Beta support is handled by a person, not an automated public forum.

Product help support@heldat.app

Early access hello@heldat.app

What to include

  • The email address used for your Heldat account.
  • Your device model and operating-system version.
  • The screen or action involved and what happened.
  • Whether the issue continues after closing and reopening the app.

Never email passwords, recovery phrases, private keys, full identity documents or unredacted financial records. A support responder will describe a controlled next step if evidence is needed.

Urgent security or access issue

Email security@heldat.app with “URGENT” in the subject when you believe another person can access your household or device. Revoke the device session and review household members inside the app when those controls remain available.

Service status

The private-beta launch surface is operational. Authenticated cloud enrolment remains limited to approved pilot accounts.
