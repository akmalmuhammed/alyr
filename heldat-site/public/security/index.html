Report a security issue

Coordinated disclosure for Heldat

Contact

Send a concise report to security@heldat.app. Include affected surface, reproducible steps, impact and a safe proof. Do not include unrelated household data.

Research boundaries

  • Use only accounts and data you control.
  • Stop when another person’s data could be accessed.
  • Avoid denial of service, social engineering and physical attacks.
  • Do not make a finding public before a coordinated fix window.

Good-faith reports within these boundaries will be reviewed and acknowledged. Heldat does not currently operate a paid bounty.
