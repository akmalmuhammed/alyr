Privacy notice

Effective 31 July 2026 · Version 1.0

1. Scope 2. Data 3. Uses 4. Sharing 5. Storage 6. Retention 7. Your choices 8. Children 9. Changes 10. Contact

Heldat is in private beta. This notice describes the data practices of the current mobile and web service. Material changes will be published here before they take effect.

1. Scope and controller

Heldat (“Heldat”, “we”, “us”) provides a private asset-continuity service. Heldat is the controller for account, service and operational data described in this notice. A household owner also decides what asset and member information is added to that household workspace.

This notice applies to heldat.app, the Heldat mobile applications and related support interactions. It does not cover third-party websites that publish their own notices.

2. Data we process

Account and membership data

  • Email address, user identifier and authentication provider.
  • Household membership, role, invitations and access changes.
  • Installation or device identifier used for sync safety.

Content you choose to add

  • Asset names, categories, descriptions, identifiers and notes.
  • Property or storage addresses and precise or approximate locations.
  • Photos, documents and other supporting material.
  • User-entered values, dates, liabilities and currency.
  • Family or collaborator details entered into a household workspace.

Service and diagnostic data

  • Sync status, record versions, checksums, errors and security events.
  • Allow-listed product interactions used to measure activation and reliability.
  • Basic request information needed for abuse prevention and rate limits.

Do not add passwords, recovery phrases, private keys, payment-card secrets or similar credentials to Heldat.

3. How we use data

  • Provide accounts, household workspaces, capture, search, sync and exports.
  • Store and display the records and evidence you request.
  • Apply permissions and keep an audit trail for important changes.
  • Protect the service, prevent abuse and investigate reliability problems.
  • Measure privacy-safe activation, retention and feature reliability.
  • Respond to support, privacy and account-deletion requests.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

Heldat does not sell personal data. Heldat does not use household content for cross-context behavioural advertising.

4. When data is shared

Data may be disclosed only as needed to:

  • Other members of a household, according to the role and access chosen by the household owner.
  • Infrastructure and service providers supporting hosting, authentication, storage, delivery, diagnostics and app distribution.
  • Professional advisers or authorities when required to protect rights, safety or comply with law.
  • A successor in a genuine corporate transaction, subject to appropriate confidentiality and notice.

Current core providers may include Supabase for authentication, database and private file storage; Expo for application builds and updates; Cloudflare for the public website, network protection and privacy-oriented performance analytics; and Apple or Google for platform distribution and optional sign-in.

5. Storage and security

Heldat is designed to support encrypted local device storage, transport encryption and private cloud storage with access controls. Sensitive keys are kept in platform-protected key storage where the operating system supports it. Access between households is denied by server-side authorization rules.

No online service eliminates every risk. Keep device access controls enabled, review household membership and maintain a Recovery Kit or export appropriate to your needs. Heldat does not currently make an end-to-end-encryption claim.

Providers may process data in countries other than your own. We use contractual and technical measures appropriate to the service and applicable law.

6. Retention and deletion

Active household content is kept while the account or household needs it. When deletion is completed, active access is removed and remaining copies are deleted or de-identified according to the applicable operational, security and backup cycle.

Short-lived signup, upload and rate-limit records expire automatically. Sync safety and audit records may be kept longer where needed to prevent duplicate writes, reconcile offline devices, investigate misuse or demonstrate a requested deletion.

Some records may be retained when law requires it or when necessary to resolve a dispute. They remain restricted to that purpose.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may request:

  • Access to or correction of your personal data.
  • A portable export of supported household records.
  • Deletion of your account or an eligible household.
  • Restriction of or objection to certain processing.
  • Withdrawal of consent where consent is the legal basis.
  • A complaint to the data-protection authority available to you.

Household roles can affect which content an individual member may export or delete. Identity and authority are verified before fulfilling sensitive requests. See the account deletion instructions.

8. Children

Heldat is not directed to children and is intended for people who can enter a binding agreement in their location. Do not create an independent account for a child. Family-history information should be added only by an authorized adult with appropriate care.

9. Changes to this notice

The effective date and version appear at the top of this page. Material changes will be announced through the service or another appropriate channel before they take effect.

10. Contact

Privacy questions and requests: privacy@heldat.app. General help: support@heldat.app.
