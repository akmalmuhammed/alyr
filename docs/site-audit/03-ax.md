# Site audit 03: agent experience (AX)

24 Oct 2026, read-only. Live alyr.qa (curl, headless Edge `--eval`) and `aly-shelf-documents-pages`. **Live is not this branch:** it serves unmerged PR #105 (H1 "The tech team your business never had."), so part A treats the live HTML as the truth.

## What already works

- **The static HTML carries all the meaning.** I compared the rendered `innerText` of `/` and `/docs` with the served HTML: every line is there except the Doha clock.
- One `<h1>`, a `<main>`, and native `<details>` questions. Canonical, `hreflang` and `x-default` are right on all 14 pages.
- Meta descriptions are honest, and caveats sit beside their claims.
- `robots.txt` allows all but private paths; AI user agents get 200.

## P0

**A1. Sample numbers read as ALYR's own facts.** On `/` and `/agent` the first `<h2>` is the sample dashboard: "Week so far: 50 orders, QAR 8,520. Orders up 11%, sales up 20%…". On `/` it appears twice, with nested `h3` to `h5` headings such as "QAR 3,400 owed 30+ days". About 400 words of sample figures come before "Sample data, fictional bakery", the `figcaption` 20 KB later in the HTML. Extractors drop the figure's `aria-label`. `/agent` adds "ALYR weekly report · Qirfa Bakery · 4–10 Oct · 68 orders, QAR 12,005". An assistant can conclude "ALYR raised a Doha bakery's sales 20%".
*Fix:*
1. Make the `figcaption` the figure's first child; CSS can still show it at the bottom.
2. In showcase mode, render the Agent drawing's headings as `p`/`div`.
3. Add `data-nosnippet` to sample figures. Google honours it for AI features [G1].
4. In the twins (A3), replace each figure with one line: "[Sample screen of a fictional bakery; numbers made up]".

*Effort* M. *Honesty* The caption still appears once, only earlier.

**A2. Nothing states briefly what is true today.** `llms.txt`, `llms-full.txt` and `index.md` all return 404, and no page has JSON-LD. Inline React data is 61% of the homepage's 417 KB, and the product list appears three times before the `<h1>`. A fetcher that truncates gets menus and sample numbers.
*Fix:* Publish `/llms.txt` (Appendix) and `/index.md`, generated from `copy.ts` by `build-site.sh`. *Effort* S. *Honesty* Each line mirrors the site, not plans. *Caveat:* Google says no "AI text files" are needed [G1], and no major assistant says it reads `llms.txt`. The cost is low and the gain uncertain. The file also serves as the truth source that the twins and lints check against.

**B1. Live, branches and skills disagree.** alyr.qa runs on unmerged PR #105. On `origin/main`, `alyr-site-page` still says "ALYR Payroll is listed as coming" and has no Shelf or Docs. This branch's skill describes the old hero and the "Not yet" blocks the 21 Oct rulings removed. `status.md` (on #105) says ALY-210 is "not published", but live serves it. Local `main` is 90 commits behind `origin/main`. An agent starting from `main` would rebuild Payroll "coming".
*Fix:* Merge #102 to #105 in order. Have `build-site.sh` write `out-site/version.txt` (commit, branch, date), and have `status.md` name that commit. *Effort* S. *Honesty* `status.md` says what is live, not only what is merged.

**B2. The site's rules are prose, not checks.** `design-check.mjs` checks colours and fonts only. No check covers prices while `OFFER_LIVE` is off, AI words, "coming" or Payroll, words outside `copy.ts`, or FIG captions; critics check them by eye. The 08-site-log fix pass caught "Coming" twice for Payroll that way. `build-site.sh` is not in CI or `check.sh`.
*Fix:* Add `scripts/site-check.mjs`, run in CI after `build-site.sh`:
- grep `out-site` for a banned list kept as JSON beside `honesty.md` (AI words, `Payroll`, `coming soon`, `Early access`, `QAR \d` outside `figure` while the offer is off);
- assert per page a canonical, both `hreflang`s, JSON-LD with no `offers` or ratings, `figcaption` first, and no headings in a `figure`;
- check that the sitemap equals `PATHS` and that `llms.txt` names every product.

*Effort* M. *Honesty* The list is `honesty.md`'s, so the prose and the check cannot drift.

## P1

**A3. No markdown twins.** *Fix:* `build-site.sh` writes `index.md`, `chat.md` … `ar/docs.md` from the copy objects (the `page.md` convention [L1]). Each page gets `<link rel="alternate" type="text/markdown">`, and `_headers` gives `.md` the type `text/markdown`. Cloudflare's "Markdown for Agents" needs Pro and labels output `ai-train=yes` [C2]. Our own twin is free and drops the sample figures. *Effort* M. *Honesty* The twin makes the same claims as the page (no cloaking), and `site-check` compares them.

**A4. No structured data.** *Fix:* `_site/structuredData.ts` builds the JSON-LD from `copy.ts`/`site.ts`, with `inLanguage` per locale:
- `Organization`: `name`, `url`, `email`, `areaServed: QA`, `knowsLanguage: [ar, en]`, `description`.
- `WebSite` on `/`.
- Per product, `Service`, or `SoftwareApplication` with `applicationCategory: BusinessApplication`: `provider: ALYR`, `areaServed: QA`, description = the page's meta description.
- `FAQPage` from the visible `<details>` on `/` and each "Before you ask".
- `BreadcrumbList` Home › Product.

*Effort* M. *Honesty limits:* no `offers`, `price`, `aggregateRating` or `review`. No `legalName`, `taxID`, `address` or `foundingDate` before the CR. No `sameAs` to accounts that do not exist. Nothing marks Docs as available. That means no rich results, which is accepted (FAQ rich results are limited to government and health sites since 2023, from memory). The markup must match the visible text [G1].

**A5. AI crawler settings are not verified.** `robots.txt` comes from Next, with no Cloudflare `Content-Signal`. A spoofed user agent cannot show whether Cloudflare blocks verified crawlers.
*Fix:*
1. The founder checks Cloudflare › AI Crawl Control: OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, Claude-SearchBot, Claude-User, PerplexityBot and Googlebot should be allowed.
2. He decides on training (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended [O1, A1, G2]). Allowing them helps future models know ALYR.
3. `robots.ts` names those agents and adds `Content-Signal: search=yes, ai-input=yes, ai-train=<decision>` [C1].

*Effort* S. *Honesty* Signals are preferences [C1]. User-triggered fetchers may ignore robots.txt [O1, P1].

**A6. "ALYR Agent" sounds like AI.** Only the eighth homepage question says the suggestions come "from fixed rules". *Fix:* Add one sentence to `/agent`'s lead, its meta description and `llms.txt`: "applies fixed, written rules to the shop's own records; it is not an AI model." *Effort* S. *Honesty* True (D-012, the FAQ).

**A7. Sample names could be read as customers.** The name check on Qirfa and the others is still open, and "Ward Events, 104 days" reads like a real debtor. *Fix:* Finish the check, and have `llms.txt` call them fictional. *Effort* S.

**B3. No `AGENTS.md`, and `CLAUDE.md` leaves out the site.** Its hard rules cover the back end. Its Layout section lists only `/` and `/chat`, and it never names `.claude/skills`, `docs/site-*` or `shoot.mjs`. *Fix:* Add five "Site hard rules": prices only through `OFFER_LIVE`; no AI words; nothing coming; fictional samples, captioned; no deploy without the founder's yes. Add an `AGENTS.md` that says "read CLAUDE.md". *Effort* S.

**B4. No single command checks the site.** `check.sh` needs PostgreSQL and runs the API suite. Every cycle rebuilt its probes outside the repo (`C:\t\fix\crit.mjs`, `C:\t\suite\snap.mjs`). `verify.md` says `shoot.mjs` "has no `--eval`", but it has one.
*Fix:* Add `scripts/site-check.sh`: tokens, lint, types, `build-site.sh`, `site-check.mjs`, and a committed `site-probes.mjs` (320 px overflow, 44 px targets, axe) run against `out-site` served with gzip. Move `shoot.mjs` to `scripts/`. *Effort* M.

**B5. Docs drift.** `docs/README.md` omits `site-products-2/` and `site-audit/`, and points to `docs/work/`, which does not exist. `brand.md` line 121 still says "Payroll dashed" and "Not yet". The product row in `status.md` says "ALYR Payroll coming". *Fix:* Correct all three, and add a one-line index of the build logs. *Effort* S.

## The five changes that would improve AX most

1. **Label sample figures for machines** (A1).
2. **Generate `llms.txt` and `.md` twins from `copy.ts`** (A2, A3).

## Appendix: `llms.txt`, true on 24 Oct 2026

```markdown
# ALYR

> ALYR is a technology company in Doha, Qatar, that sets up WhatsApp-based tools for owner-run businesses: orders, bookings, credit accounts, items and stock, and receipts, in Arabic and English. A person from ALYR visits the shop, sets everything up, trains the team and keeps improving it. ALYR is new and is choosing its first businesses in Doha now.

- For: bakeries and home kitchens, restaurants and cafés, salons and barbers, home services, fashion, abayas and gifts, groceries, wholesale and trading, in Qatar.
- How it starts: a check-up of about an hour at the shop, then a short list of what costs the owner time or money, and what ALYR would set up first.
- Customers order and book on a page that opens from the WhatsApp chat. No app, no account, no password.
- Built on WhatsApp's official Business Platform. ALYR is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, WhatsApp or Meta.
- ALYR never holds a shop's money. Payment links are built and tested on a test page. Card payments are not live yet; once a shop's payment provider is connected, they go to the shop's own account.
- Prices are not published yet. Ask by email.
- Contact: hello@alyr.qa. A person from ALYR replies. The WhatsApp line is not open yet.
- The screens and numbers drawn on alyr.qa are samples from fictional businesses (Qirfa Bakery, Dafira Salon, Zilal Cooling). They are not customer results.

## Products

- [ALYR Chat](https://alyr.qa/chat): WhatsApp orders and one team inbox. Answers customers' messages with an order page, sends order tracking and status updates, and lets anyone on the team step into a chat.
- [ALYR Bookings](https://alyr.qa/bookings): Appointments from the WhatsApp chat. Confirms at once, reminds the customer two hours before, and the team marks who came. Today it takes one booking at a time for the whole business.
- [ALYR Agent](https://alyr.qa/agent): The home screen of the workspace: what needs the owner now, the week against the same days last week, and up to three suggestions. Each suggestion comes from a fixed, written rule over the shop's own records, not from an AI model. It suggests; the owner decides. Comes with every ALYR product. A weekly report on WhatsApp starts once WhatsApp approves its wording.
- [ALYR Accounts](https://alyr.qa/accounts): Credit accounts. Record a credit sale with one line from a staff phone, see who owes and for how long, and send reminders whose tone follows the age of the debt.
- [ALYR Shelf](https://alyr.qa/shelf): Everything a shop sells: photos, prices, sizes and choices in Arabic and English. Every sale counts the stock down, the last one sells once, and the order page says Sold out. Comes with every ALYR product.
- [ALYR Docs](https://alyr.qa/docs): Receipts, invoices and credit notes from sales and recorded payments, numbered without a gap, Arabic first, final once issued. A shop without a commercial registration gets receipts; invoices once it registers. Set up with the shop, like every ALYR product.

## Pages

- [Home](https://alyr.qa/): what ALYR does, how it works, owners' questions

## Optional

```
