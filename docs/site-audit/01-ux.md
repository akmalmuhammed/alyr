# 01 · UX audit of alyr.qa: small text, length and the path to the action

Read-only audit of the live site on 24 Oct 2026: `/`, `/chat`, `/bookings`, `/agent`, `/accounts`, `/shelf`, `/docs` and their `/ar` pages, in light mode at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844, with the offer switch off and contact in the email state (as live). No code was changed. Screenshots are in `docs/site-audit/ux/` (first screens of all 14 pages at both widths, full pages, and section shots named `<page>-<lang>-<width>-at-<section>.png`).

**Which code is live.** The brief names `aly-shelf-documents-pages`, but the live homepage ("The tech team your business never had.", "Book a check-up") and `/chat` (the X-ray list) come from **`aly-210-tech-team-homepage` at c2998ac**, which sits on top of the Shelf branch with ALY-154, ALY-209 and ALY-210. `copyShelf.ts` and `copyDocs.ts` are identical on both branches; `copy.ts`, `HomePage.tsx`, `ProductPage.tsx` and `Shell.tsx` differ. Every key below is from c2998ac. That branch's `docs/status.md` still says ALY-210 is "not published"; it is live.

**How the counts work.** One item is one visible piece of small or secondary text, counted once per place it appears. Words inside a drawn product screen are not counted; labels the site adds around a drawing are. Screen-reader-only text is listed, not counted. English and Arabic have identical items on all 14 pages (checked by probe), though Arabic sets them at 13 to 14 px rather than 11 to 12 px mono capitals.

## 0. How to keep samples honest without the captions

`honesty.md` §4 requires every drawing of a sample business to be marked as fictional. Today that is done 27 times in text: 14 FIG captions, 12 "Sample conversation with a fictional …" notes and one "The real screen, with fictional businesses" line. `/chat` says it three times, and the homepage spotlight says it three ways around one figure (the switch label "Sample business", FIG 02, and the under line).

**Recommended: the mark goes into the drawn window chrome, once per drawing.** Each drawing already has a chrome line that names the shop. The word "Sample" goes there:

| Drawing | Where the mark goes | Text |
|---|---|---|
| Workspace window (`.aw`, dashboard, Bookings, Credit, Stock, Documents screens; the room's desk) | After the shop name in the title bar: "Qirfa Bakery / Home" becomes "Qirfa Bakery · Sample / Home" | new key `fig.sample`: "Sample" / «مثال» |
| Drawn WhatsApp phone (`ProductDemo`, `ReportPhone`, X-ray phone) | The header's second line, "Business account", becomes "Sample business" | `businessMeta` → "Sample business" (all demos) |
| Customer pages in the phone frame (`CxTop`: tracking, order, booking pages; the Shelf and Docs moments) | Beside the shop name in `CxTop` | `fig.sample` |
| Flow diagram (`FlowDiagram`) | One mono tag at the end of its tab row, beside Pause | `fig.sample` |
| Report card image | Already printed on the card ("marked Sample" in its alt text) | none |
| Agent figures with the switch | The switch label "Sample business" already marks the set | keep `agent.switchLabel` |

**Why this one.** It travels with the drawing: an owner who screenshots a figure and forwards it on WhatsApp keeps the mark, which a page-level line cannot do. It sits where the eye already reads the shop's name, so it costs no extra line and no extra reading. It is design, not a footnote, as `alyr-design` §1 asks ("even the honesty labels are design"). It marks each drawing once, so "sample" is never said twice.

**Rejected.** *One line per page* ("Every business on this page is fictional") breaks §4, which asks that every drawing be marked, and a screenshot loses it. *One mark per figure set* works on the homepage but leaves a drawing unmarked whenever a set spans sections.

**Two captions stay**, because the drawing means nothing without its words: the homepage trust pair ("The same order page, in English and Arabic") and the Docs CR foot ("The foot of a receipt with no CR on file"). Both drop the "FIG nn" number and keep the sample word. Screen readers already hear "fictional" from each figure's `label` and `aria-label`, so nothing is lost for them.

**The fix pull request must also change the skills**, because they require FIG captions today: `alyr-design/SKILL.md` principle 5 and §9 ("Every sample is captioned"), `alyr-design/honesty.md` §4, `alyr-design/components.md` (the FIG caption row), and `alyr-site-page/SKILL.md` ("FIG caption on every drawing"). The rule becomes: every drawing is marked "Sample" once, in its own chrome; a caption only where the drawing needs words.

## 2. UX findings

P0: untrue, broken or against a decision. P1: should fix. P2: polish.

### P0

**P0-1. The records name the wrong live branch.** The brief and the Shelf branch say that branch is live; the site serves `aly-210-tech-team-homepage` (c2998ac), whose own `docs/status.md` says ALY-210 is "not published". A fixer working from the brief would edit a homepage that no longer exists. **Fix:** the fix pass branches from c2998ac. The status header and the lane brief's "Now" record ALY-210 as published (date and Cloudflare version from the founder's deploy).

### P1: first five seconds

**F1. Product pages on phones open on words, not the product.** At 390 px the H1 and lead fill the first screen: the demo's tabs start at about 540 px on `/bookings` and 660 px on `/docs`, and no chat bubble shows until the second screen. `/docs` runs a 6-line H1 into a 9-line lead. **Fix:** a lead of no more than 3 lines at 390 px. For example, `/docs` `hero.lead`: "When a payment is recorded, ALYR Docs issues the next numbered receipt, Arabic first, final once issued." The 24-hour and CR conditions already sit in the tab captions and the "No CR yet?" block. Apply the same cap to each product page's lead.

**F2. The demo and flow drawings open empty.** Before they play, the first screen of `/chat` shows an empty phone, and "One order" on `/` shows empty lanes with "Nothing new yet" (`home-en-1440-at-see.png`). **Fix:** make the first step's end state the HTML (the first bubble and its panel row drawn), and start the animation from there. That is the rule in `motion.md` ("nothing is hidden in the HTML").

### P1: reading load and the scan path

**R1. Eyebrows repeat the heading below them or the bar above them.** There are 9 eyebrows on `/` and 6 to 8 on each product page, most in 11 to 12 px mono capitals (`.eyebrow`, `site.css` line 40; 11 px on phones, line 640). The scan path then runs: tiny capitals, big heading, lead, for every section. **Fix:** remove them as listed in §1, keeping three: the Trust question, the Agent spotlight (renamed) and the product hero's state.

**R2. Mono capitals at 11 px are used for information, not only for labels.** The Agent who-tags ("SALES THIS WEEK" ×16) and the Shelf and Docs tags carry content in the hardest type to read. **Fix:** where kept, `.tags span` becomes `font-family: inherit; text-transform: none; letter-spacing: 0; font-size: var(--fs-14)`, or the nearest token step at 0.875 rem.

**R3. "Behind the scenes" repeats across the suite.** "Stop means stop" appears on 4 pages, "WhatsApp's rules, handled" on 4, "records kept apart" on 4, and the flow's first two steps ("A customer writes to your number", "An X word, so ALYR answers") on 3. **Fix:** keep each line only where it is the product's own behaviour (consent on `/chat` and `/accounts`). Elsewhere, close the group with one link: "How ALYR handles WhatsApp's rules, consent and your records" → `/#trust`.

### P1: section count and page length

**L1. The homepage is 17,716 px at 1440 and 21,380 px at 390, across 10 sections plus the closing call.** The products "room" alone is 4,848 px at 1440: a pinned, tilted desk whose corners are unreadable at that size (`home-en-1440-at-products.png`), with 230 px of empty Carbon before its heading. **Fix:** unpin it. Show six plain rows (name, descriptor, one sentence, link, the crop at true size), the layout phones already get without the pinned index. About −2,450 px at 1440.

**L2. Two homepage sections tell the same story.** "What a check-up finds" (problem → fix → product) and "By business" (your day → with ALYR → products) do the same job for the same owner. **Fix, needs the founder (D-048 fixes the order):** fold the six problems into the By business tabs as each tab's "Your day today" list, and drop the Why ALYR table to its single strongest row in the hero lead. That saves about 2,030 px at 1440 and 3,235 px at 390. Labelled "Needs Akmal".

**L3. Product pages pad every section with a literal, not the token.** `.sec { padding-block: clamp(4.5rem, 10vw, 8rem) }` (`site.css` line 261) gives 256 px between sections at 1440, against the homepage's `--sec` (6 rem max). This breaks the tokens rule and leaves about 135 px of blank canvas under the bars (`bookings-en-1440-at-screen.png`). **Fix:** `.sec { padding-block: var(--sec); }`. About −500 px per product page at 1440; no change on phones.

### P1: the path to the one action

**A1. One action, two names.** `/` says "Book a check-up" (header, hero, sticky bar, How, closing call, per D-048). The six product pages say "Email us" for the same mail link. **Fix, needs the founder:** one label site-wide. "Book a check-up" follows D-048; extending it to product pages is his call. Until then, the closing call on product pages can carry the check-up wording in its body.

**A2. The header's section links leave the product page.** On `/bookings` the header's "How we work" and "Questions" go to `/#how` and `/#questions`. The product bar's "How it works" and the page's own Questions stay on the page. That is two "how" links, one above the other, going to different places. **Fix:** on product pages the header shows only Products, the language link, the theme switch and the action; the section links live in the product bar.

**A3. `/chat` has no Questions section;** the other five do. **Fix:** add `QuestionsBlock` with "Can I use it on its own?" and "How long does setup take?", to match.

### P1: phones and the sticky bars

**P1-a. Three sticky layers on the homepage, two on product pages.** On `/` at 390 the header (56 px) and the contact bar (about 70 px) take 15% of the screen; in the products section a pinned row of names adds a third layer. On product pages the header and the product bar take 105 px at the top. **Fix:** drop the room's pinned index on phones (L1 does this). The product bar hides when scrolling down and returns when scrolling up, as the header could too.

**P1-b. Sideways strips with no cue.** At 390 the product bar's links are clipped ("Work…", "What…" on `/agent`), and so are the demo tabs ("Ca…" on `/bookings`) and the products index ("04" cut). **Fix:** product bar on phones: two links, "How it works" and "What's in it". Demo tabs: wrap to two rows under 30 rem, or show a 24 px fade on the end edge (mirrored in Arabic) as the scroll cue.

**P1-c. `/agent`'s opening is 4,046 px on a phone** (the whole dashboard plus the feed inside the hero). **Fix:** as on `/` (D-048), stop the phone dashboard after "Needs you now" and the four numbers, then a "See the whole screen" disclosure (native `<details>`).

### P1: anything confusing for an owner

**C1. "Coming" lines on a public page.** "Several chairs at once is next" (`home.business.rows` salon note), "Next for ALYR Bookings" (Bookings `notYet`), "the next step for Bookings" (the `chairs` question on `/` and `/bookings`). D-039 says nothing coming is shown, and to an owner it reads as a promise. **Fix:** cut the "next" clauses. For the question: "Today ALYR Bookings takes one booking at a time for the whole business. That suits one chair, one room or one practitioner. Tell us how many you run, and we'll tell you honestly whether it fits."

**C2. Three names for one screen.** "Insights" (homepage eyebrow), "ALYR Agent" (product) and "home screen" (lead). **Fix:** "ALYR Agent" in the eyebrow (§1.2).

### P2

- **N1. FIG numbering skips 03 on `/`** (01, 02, 04, 05 with the offer off). This goes away when the captions go; if any stay numbered, number them at render.
- **N2. The theme switch and the language link are fine at both widths** (44 px, in the phone menu). The language link lands on the same page. No change.
- **M1. Motion that holds reading back:** the `/chat` "Why it helps" statement lights word by word on scroll (unlit words at 3.35:1); show it lit. `data-reveal` fades leave blank canvas on a fast phone scroll; start them 25% before the section arrives.
