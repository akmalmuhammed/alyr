## The tone rules (same file)

- Their bakery's name, their night, their customer's name on the cake. Never "businesses like yours".
- Short lines, one idea per screen, times on a clock (11:52 pm), money in QAR.
- State it ("It's on tomorrow's list."), never "it can help with".
- No "AI", "smart", "seamless", "solution", "platform", "unlock".
- Honest by label: every scene says Built, Set up by ALYR or Sample; the leak figure says "your estimate"; the weekly numbers are a sample week; no invented market statistics or customers.
- WhatsApp line until Meta approves ALYR as a Tech Provider: "Works on WhatsApp, the app your customers already use." Not "keep your number and app" (ALY-39).

## The bar (29 Oct 2026, the task for ALY-263)

"Super modern, interactive, amazing creatives and visual elements, enterprise-grade, super confident, real scenario." Shown on his laptop in a shop to an owner who has never heard of ALYR: it must grab attention in the first ten seconds. One idea per screen, big type, real drawn chats and real product screens, smooth scene transitions in ALYR's 45° motion language. Nothing generic, nothing that looks like slides.

Must have, as he listed it: the nine scenes and their beats on arrow keys, space and Page Up/Down; a slim progress line; a "jump to scene" menu; N for presenter notes (never shown by default); R to restart; a setup panel before scene 1 (the bakery's name and a shop colour, carried through every scene); scene 2's estimate with its formula on tap and a changeable conservative share labelled "your estimate", the ten pain cards, and the tapped pains reordering scene 4; scene 3 as a working split screen (the cake, a pay link and Paid, the order landing, tomorrow's list, 11 of 12 → 12 of 12 → "Full for Thursday. Next free: Saturday."); scene 5's one order through the six products, before and after, "it grows with you"; scenes 6 and 7 (Google listing with an Order button, a review request and a review labelled "Set up by ALYR"; the Sunday report card and the Agent home labelled "Sample week"); the labels everywhere; Arabic parity marked as a draft (ALY-66); offline once loaded, for this route only; light by default, dark supported; `noindex`, not linked from the public site.

Hard honesty rules, as he listed them: no "AI", "smart", "seamless", "solution", "platform"; no invented customers or market statistics; no prices; no "keep your number / your WhatsApp Business app" ("Works on WhatsApp, the app your customers already use."); no WhatsApp or Meta logos; card payments labelled as going live with the shop's own payment provider.
