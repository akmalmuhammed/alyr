# ALY-263 · Research: in-person product demos (29 Oct 2026)

Short on purpose: no research agent was run. These are patterns **known from practice and earlier ALYR work, not browsed today**; each says what ALYR's own version is.

| Pattern | Why it works | ALYR's version |
|---|---|---|
| Open on the customer's own moment, not the product (the "day in the life" demo) | An owner recognises their own night faster than a feature list | Scene 1 is their bakery's name at 11:47 pm with 14 unread chats, on Carbon |
| Ask for the buyer's own numbers before showing value (discovery inside the demo) | A figure they gave cannot be dismissed as marketing | Scene 2: three steppers, the formula on tap, the share low and stated, "your estimate" |
| Let the buyer touch it once or twice | Touch turns watching into owning | The pain cards and the cake builder are real buttons the owner presses |
| Split screen: customer side and back office together | Shows cause and effect in one look | Scene 3: the customer's phone beside the workspace, both moving on one beat |
| One object travelling through the system | "Integration" is invisible; a moving order makes it visible | Scene 5: order #1043 lights six products in turn along a 45° line |
| Before and after as the same picture | The buyer sees what goes away | Scene 5: seven islands fold into one workspace |
| Presenter view with notes and a clock | The presenter keeps pace without slides | N shows the "Says" lines, the target time and the elapsed time |
| Clicker keys | Presenters use a clicker in a shop | Page Up and Down, arrows, space |
| End on a small concrete next step | A date is easier to agree than a contract | Scene 9: "Book your check-up" and a day |

## Things to avoid

- Slides: a title, three bullets and an icon. Every screen here is a drawing of the product or a single line of type.
- Fake dashboards with round numbers, logo walls, testimonials, market statistics ("80% of orders come after hours").
- Count-ups, confetti, glow, gradients, 3D phones, people photos, stock bakery images.
- Talking about "the platform", AI, automation; reading the screen aloud.
- A demo that needs the internet in a shop with weak signal: it must run offline once loaded.
- Words the owner cannot check: response-time promises, "live", "keep your number".
