# Requests from public authorities

Adopted by the founder on 31 Oct 2026. Applies to any request from a government body, regulator
or police for the personal data of a business on ALYR, its staff or its customers.

No lawyer has reviewed this yet (ALY-51). Until one has, it says nothing about challenging a
request; we told Meta the same.

1. **Check it is lawful first.** The request goes to the founder. Nothing is handed over until
   the founder has checked who is asking, under what law, and that it binds ALYR.
2. **Give the minimum.** Only the records the request names, for the people and dates it names.
3. **Keep a record.** For every request, write down the date, who asked, what they asked for, the
   legal reason given, what we decided and why, and what we handed over. Keep the record out of
   this repository, which is public: a request can name a real person.

**Where this is used:** Meta's App Review data handling questions (ALY-262), which ask what
policies ALYR has for these requests. So far there have been none: the company was registered
on 26 Oct 2026.
