## The answers

Each answer says only what is true today. "Built" means built and tested, not yet running for a real shop.

1. **Who are we?** A Qatar company that sets up and runs the technology for businesses their owners run: bakeries, salons, groceries, restaurants, home services, shops. We bring it to you and look after it.
2. **What problem do we solve?** Customers order, book and ask on WhatsApp. Messages wait in the rush and after hours, bookings are missed, payments are chased by hand, and owners have no time to learn software.
3. **Who is it for?** Owner-run businesses in Qatar that talk to customers on WhatsApp, with one person or a small team. **Not for (yet):** clinics and anything with health data (needs the lawyer first), large chains with their own IT, and businesses that do not use WhatsApp with customers.
4. **What do we do today?** One workspace with:
   - **ALYR Chat:** WhatsApp orders, a shared team inbox, order tracking.
   - **ALYR Bookings:** a booking page and reminders before every visit.
   - **ALYR Agent:** the owner's dashboard and the Sunday report card, with rules you can read (it counts and flags; it does not guess).
   - **ALYR Accounts:** credit accounts for customers who pay later.

   Also part of ALYR: the Catalog (items, photos and stock), and receipts and invoices. All of it is built and tested; none of it runs for a real shop until the registration and the WhatsApp connection are in place.
5. **What can we do for you?** Inside the subscription:
   - we set it up in your shop;
   - load your items, services and prices;
   - connect WhatsApp and your own payment account;
   - train your staff;
   - print your QR cards;
   - answer when you need us, in Arabic and English.
6. **How do we operate?**
   - In person.
   - Month to month.
   - Your customers and their data stay yours.
   - No commission on your orders.
   - Payments go to your own account.
   - Only the official WhatsApp connection.
7. **Why us?**
   - We set it up for you instead of handing you software.
   - Arabic and English, made in Qatar for how Qatar shops work.
   - WhatsApp first, because that is where your customers are.
   - Rules you can read, not a black box.
8. **Why now?** Customers in Qatar already expect to order and book on WhatsApp, and Meta's official platform now lets a shop keep its number and its WhatsApp Business app.
9. **What does it cost?** Not in public until the registration (D-034). At the counter, only after the registration, from the back-office price list: the first product from QAR 449 a month, each added product QAR 150, ALYR Agent included, a setup quoted at the visit. The first month is paid, with the money back if the agreed result is missed.
10. **What happens after I say yes?** The plan for each shop (a target, not a promise in public):
    - Day 1: we set up your items, prices and hours with you, and you try it as a customer.
    - Days 2–3: WhatsApp and payments are connected and your staff are trained.
    - Day 7: your QR cards and chat link are out.
    - Day 30: we look at the agreed result together.
11. **What do I have to do?** About an hour with us at setup. Share your chat link and QR cards in the first week. Answer the chats ALYR hands to you.
12. **Is my data safe?** Each business's data is walled off from every other business in the database. ALYR staff see a shop's setup, never its customers' conversations. Data is processed under Qatar's privacy law (PDPPL).
13. **What if I want to leave?** Stop at the end of any month with 7 days' notice. You can export your items and prices and take them with you.
14. **Who is behind ALYR?** ALYR, a company being registered in Qatar. Once the registration is issued, its number and address appear in the footer, on quotes and on invoices. No founder name or face (the founder's decision).
15. **What is the goal?** To be the tech partner for owner-run businesses in Qatar, then across the GCC.
16. **What's coming?** We announce something when it is built. Payroll is the next product.
17. **Who uses it?** No business yet: the honest answer, until there is one. Instead we offer a demo of your own business on your own phone, and the money-back first month.

## Never say

- "AI", "smart" or "learns" about the product: it runs on rules.
- "Live", "customers", or any number about real use, until it is true.
- A price, an offer or "live in 48 hours" in public before the registration.
- "Keep your number" before ALYR is a Meta Tech Provider.
- That Meta or WhatsApp endorses us; never show their logo.
- A competitor's name, in an attack.
- Anything about the founder (the brand speaks).

## Getting clients

**Every business, measured by sector.** The visit uses the company story above plus one opener for the shop in front of you:

| Sector | Opener (the pain in their words) | Products to show |
|---|---|---|
| Bakeries, sweets, home kitchens | "How many orders come on WhatsApp the week before Eid?" | Catalog, Chat, Agent |
| Salons and barbers | "How many bookings didn't show up last week?" | Bookings, Agent |
| Groceries and baqalas | "Who owes you money right now, and how much?" | Accounts, Chat |
| Restaurants and cafés | "What do you pay the delivery apps on orders your regulars would place directly?" | Catalog, Chat |
| Home services, AC repair | "Who answers when a customer messages while you're on a job?" | Bookings, Chat, Agent |
| Fashion and gifts | "How many people ask 'is this available?' and never hear back?" | Catalog, Chat |

**Phase A: until the registration is issued (now).**
1. Visits as the ALYR team, logged in the sales tool: sector, area, the two numbers, objections in the owner's words, next step.
2. Each good visit ends with a non-binding letter of interest. No signature as ALYR, no money (`docs/company/customer-agreement/notes.md`).
3. **Founding shops** (proposal, for the founder to confirm): the first ten who say yes get their price fixed for 12 months, setup first, and a direct line to ALYR. In return they give written consent to a case study and one referral. Still paid from the first month.
4. Partners who reach many shops at once: payment providers (SkipCash, Sadad, Dibsy), QDB's incubation hub, suppliers who visit shops every week.
5. Content from the growth plan (`docs/growth/plan.md`), so the visit is expected, not cold.

**Phase B: registration issued, up to 10 paying shops.**
- Turn the letters of interest into quotes, in the order they came.
- Set up in person and hit the agreed result in 30 days.
- Publish the first case study.
- Every shop's chat link and QR card shows ALYR to its own customers.

**Phase C: 10 to 50.**
- Take the sector that converts best, from the sales tool, to the next area.
- Partner offers.
- Web Summit Qatar, 31 Jan to 3 Feb 2027.
- Referrals from live shops on the same street.

**Measured weekly:**
- qualified owner conversations;
- visits, demos and letters of interest by sector;
- after the registration: shops paying, shops live, and shops still paying in month 3.
