The Schedule holds your price, your setup fee, your go-live date and the result we agree for your first month. Where the Schedule says something specific, it applies.

## 1. ALYR Chat

1.1 ALYR Chat is software that ALYR sets up and runs for your business on WhatsApp's official Business Platform. Your customers order, book and follow their orders in the WhatsApp chat, and your team answers them from one shared inbox, each person with their own sign-in.

1.2 ALYR Chat is in early access: it is new, we set it up with each business ourselves, and we keep improving it. We agree your go-live date with you at the visit.

1.3 Payment links work only once your own account with a licensed payment provider in Qatar is connected to ALYR Chat. That is not available yet; we will tell you in writing when it is.

1.4 The WhatsApp number your customers message is agreed with you at the visit. The number and its accounts with Meta are held in your business's name, and you give ALYR access to them. We never deregister, move or give anyone else access to your number without your written instruction.

## 2. What we do

2.1 **When you start**, for the setup fee, we load your menu, products or services, with prices, in Arabic and English; set your opening hours, booking times and reminders; give each person on your team a sign-in; train your team at your counter; and give you your chat link and QR codes for your tables, door, Instagram and Google Maps.

2.2 **Every month**, for the subscription, you get ALYR Chat, with every update and nothing to install; sign-ins for your team; a weekly report of your week on WhatsApp; a person from ALYR who answers your messages; and changes to your menu, prices and opening hours whenever you ask. We do not promise a response time.

2.3 **When you need more**, such as a website, your Google listing or connecting a tool you already use, small jobs are part of the subscription. For a bigger job we give you a price in writing before we start, and we start only if you agree.

## 3. What we do not do

3.1 ALYR is not WhatsApp or Meta, and is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, WhatsApp or Meta. Meta sets WhatsApp's rules, approves message wording, sets its fees, and decides whether a number may keep sending.

3.2 We never receive, hold or move your money.

3.3 You are the seller to your customers. Your goods and services, prices, delivery, refunds, complaints and licences stay your responsibility. We do not do your accounts or tax, give legal advice, or supply phones or internet access.

3.4 We do not promise sales. The one result we promise is the one in clause 5.

## 4. Your part

4.1 Give us correct menus, prices and opening hours, check them before go-live, and tell us when they change.

4.2 Give sign-ins only to people who work for you, and remove a person's sign-in (or tell us) on the day they leave. You are responsible for what your team does in ALYR Chat.

4.3 Follow Meta's terms and policies for businesses on WhatsApp, including the WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy and the commerce policies it refers to, which limit what you may sell and send (for example medicines, alcohol and tobacco). We go through them with you at the visit. Keep a valid payment method on your Meta account for Meta's fees; without one, Meta may stop your messages.

4.4 Get a person's agreement before you send them any marketing message (an offer or promotion that you start), as WhatsApp's rules and Qatar's data protection law require. ALYR Chat records consent, blocks marketing to anyone who has not given it, and never messages again anyone who has asked to stop. Do not ask us to get around this.

4.5 Use ALYR Chat only for your own lawful business. Do not resell or copy it.

## 5. Your first month, with your money back

5.1 Before we start, we agree one result with you, for example a number of orders or bookings taken through the chat, and write it in the Schedule.

5.2 The result is counted in your ALYR workspace, where you can see it too, over the 30 days starting on your go-live date.

5.3 If the result is missed, we refund your first month's subscription. The setup fee is not refunded.

5.4 Your part: in the first 7 days after go-live, put up the QR codes we give you and share your chat link with your customers. If you do not, the refund does not apply. Send us a photo or screenshot when it is done, so we both have a record.

5.5 At the end of the 30 days we check the count with you. If the result was missed, we pay the refund within 14 days, the same way you paid, without your having to ask. This applies once per business.

## 6. Price and payment

6.1 Your monthly subscription is from QAR 449. Your monthly price is confirmed in writing at the visit and written in the Schedule. The setup fee is a one-time fee, from QAR 1,500, quoted at the visit and written in the Schedule.

6.2 You pay the setup fee when you sign, the first month's subscription on your go-live date, and each later month in advance, on its first day. Months are counted from your go-live date: if you go live on 10 March, each month runs from the 10th to the 9th. We send an invoice for each payment, and you pay in the way the Schedule says.

6.3 **No commission.** ALYR takes no commission on your orders, bookings or payments. Your payment provider charges its own fees under your agreement with it, and ALYR takes no share of them. No other provider's fees apply unless the Schedule says so.

6.4 **WhatsApp message fees.** Meta decides which WhatsApp messages carry a fee and how much it is. Those fees are Meta's, at Meta's rates at the time, and Meta bills them to your business as the holder of the account (1.4). ALYR adds nothing to them and collects none of them. This stays so if Meta changes which messages it charges for, or what it charges. When we learn of a change that affects you, we tell you.

6.5 **Late payment.** If a payment is still unpaid 14 days after it was due, we may pause ALYR Chat after telling you in writing at least 7 days before. We charge no interest and no late fee.

6.6 **Price changes.** We may change your monthly price by telling you in writing at least 30 days before. You can stop before the new price starts, and you pay the old price until then.

6.7 Prices are in Qatari riyals. If a tax such as VAT comes to apply, we may add it from the date the law requires, and tell you as early as we can.

## 7. Starting and stopping

7.1 This agreement starts when we have both signed it, and runs month to month.

7.2 You can stop at the end of any month. Tell us in writing (WhatsApp or email is enough) at least 7 days before that month ends; if you tell us later, it ends at the end of the next month. In your first month you can tell us up to its last day, so you see the result first.

7.3 We can stop at the end of any month by telling you in writing at least 30 days before.

7.4 You can end this agreement straight away, in writing, if (a) we seriously break it and do not put it right within 14 days of being told; (b) a fault on our side stops ALYR Chat working for you for more than 3 days in a row; or (c) we have not got you live within 30 days after the go-live date in the Schedule, unless you caused the delay. In case (c) we refund the setup fee in full. We can end it straight away if you seriously break it and do not put it right within 14 days of being told.

7.5 When this agreement ends, your access stops and anything owed for the time before the end is still due. We remove ALYR's access to your WhatsApp number and help you use it with the WhatsApp Business app or another provider, as far as Meta allows. If you end it under 7.4, we also refund any part of a month you paid for and did not get. Clauses 9 to 13 continue after the end.

## 8. Pausing the service

8.1 We may pause all or part of ALYR Chat for late payment (6.5), or if your use breaks the law or WhatsApp's rules, or puts your number, our service or other businesses at risk. We tell you why first (or straight after, if it is urgent) and switch it back on once the reason is fixed. The subscription stays due while it is paused. Pausing never deletes your data.

8.2 Meta can limit or block a number, a message template or an account under its own rules. That is Meta's decision, not ours. If it happens, we help you find out why and appeal.

## 9. Your data and data protection

9.1 Your data is yours: your customer list, conversations, orders, bookings, credit accounts and reports. We use it only to run ALYR Chat for you, to support you and to keep it secure. We do not sell it, use it to market to your customers, or share it with anyone except the services in Part B of the Schedule, or when the law requires. ALYR staff do not read your conversations with your customers unless you ask us to help with one, we need to in order to fix a fault, or the law requires it. We may use totals that identify no business and no person to run and improve ALYR.

9.2 When this agreement ends, ask us within 30 days and we send you, free, within 14 days, a copy of your customer list, orders, bookings and credit accounts as spreadsheet files. 60 days after the end we delete your data from ALYR Chat, except what the law requires us to keep, such as our invoices to you. Backup copies are gone within `[TO FILL: 30]` days after that. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

9.3 Qatar's Personal Data Privacy Protection Law (Law No. 13 of 2016) applies to the personal data of your customers and your team in ALYR Chat: their names, WhatsApp numbers or IDs, messages, orders, bookings and balances. For that data you are the controller, and ALYR processes it on your behalf, for as long as this agreement lasts and only to run ALYR Chat for you. We (a) follow your instructions, which are this agreement and the way you set up and use ALYR Chat; (b) protect it with appropriate technical and organisational measures, including keeping each business's records separate inside the database itself, and let only people who need it see it, under a duty of confidentiality; (c) use only the services in Part B, which may hold it where Part B says; hold the services we choose to these same duties and stay responsible for them (Meta works for you under its own terms); and tell you at least 30 days before we add one, so you can object or stop; (d) help you answer your customers' requests to see, correct or delete their data, and your questions about how we protect it; (e) tell you without delay, and within `[TO FILL: 24]` hours of finding it, about any breach that affects the data, so you can tell the regulator in time; and (f) return or delete the data at the end, as 9.2 says.

9.4 You tell your customers, in a short notice we can help you write, that you use ALYR Chat for their orders, bookings and messages. Tell us before go-live if your business handles health information, children's information or other data the law treats as special (a clinic, for example). That needs the regulator's permission, which is yours to get as the controller, and WhatsApp's rules limit health information in chats.

## 10. Confidentiality

Each of us keeps the other's non-public business information confidential and uses it only for this agreement, while it lasts and for 2 years after. This does not cover what is already public, what the law, a court or a regulator requires to be shared, or what we share in confidence with our own advisers. We do not name you as a customer in public without your written agreement.

## 11. Availability

ALYR Chat is in early access, and we do not promise any level of uptime. It depends on WhatsApp, and on internet and hosting providers, that we do not control. We save each message before it is sent and try again if WhatsApp is slow; we tell you when we know something is wrong; we fix faults on our side as fast as we reasonably can; and we do planned work outside your busy hours where we can.

## 12. Liability

Our total liability to you under this agreement is limited to the amount you paid ALYR in the 12 months before the event behind the claim. Neither of us is liable for indirect losses, or for lost profits, sales or opportunities, as far as Qatar's law allows. We are not responsible for what Meta, payment providers or internet providers do, unless our breach of this agreement caused it. Nothing in this agreement limits liability for fraud or gross error, or any other liability that Qatar's law does not allow to be limited, and nothing in it takes away a right you have under Qatar's consumer protection law.

## 13. General

13.1 **Notices** under this agreement are in writing and may be sent by email or WhatsApp to the contacts in the Schedule.

13.2 **Changes** to this agreement are made only in writing agreed by both of us, except price changes under 6.6, and changes the law or Meta's rules require. We tell you about those at least 30 days ahead, or as soon as we can if the change comes sooner, and you can stop before they apply to you.

13.3 **Transfer.** Neither of us may transfer this agreement without the other's written agreement, except that ALYR may transfer it to a company that takes over ALYR's business, after telling you in writing.

13.4 **Whole agreement.** These terms and the Schedule are the whole agreement between us about ALYR Chat.

13.5 **Language.** This agreement is signed in Arabic and English, side by side. If the two versions ever differ, the `[TO DECIDE: Arabic]` version applies.

13.6 **Signing.** This agreement may be signed on paper or electronically, including by accepting it in your ALYR workspace. Each signed copy counts as an original.

13.7 **Law and disputes.** The law of the State of Qatar applies. If we disagree, either of us tells the other in writing and we try to settle it by talking within 30 days. If we cannot, the competent courts of the State of Qatar decide.
