## Your first month, with your money back
Before we start, we agree one result with you in writing, for example a number of orders or bookings taken
through the chat. It is counted in your ALYR workspace, where you can see it too, over the 30 days after you
go live. If the result is missed, we refund your first month's subscription in full, within 14 days after the
30 days end, the same way you paid, without your having to ask. This applies once per business. To qualify,
put up the QR codes we give you and share your chat link with your customers in the first 7 days.

## Stopping
You can stop at the end of any month with 7 days' notice (in your first month, up to its last day, so you see
the result first). You pay for the month you have started; we do not refund part of a month you chose to stop
in, except as below.

## When we refund more
- If you end the agreement because we seriously broke it and did not put it right within 14 days, or a fault on
  our side stopped ALYR working for you for more than 3 days in a row, we refund the part of the month you paid
  for and did not get.
- If we have not got you live within 30 days after the date we agreed, unless you caused the delay, we refund
  what you paid. `[TO DECIDE: setup fee wording while there is none, D-092.]`

## How refunds are paid
- **Card payments** are taken by Paddle.com Market Limited, our merchant of record. We ask Paddle to refund the
  card, and the money returns to the same card in the same US dollar amount, normally within 5 to 10 business
  days of the refund. `[TO CHECK: Paddle's current refund timing.]` You can also contact Paddle:
  https://www.paddle.com/help/contact
- **Bank transfers** are refunded by transfer to the account you paid from, in riyals.
