# Doodle study: prototype log

28 Oct 2026, Site and brand lane. The founder asked for an animated doodle-art background for
the homepage, "in framer … let me see the prototype". The concept is in
[01-concept.md](01-concept.md).

This is a Framer prototype only. Nothing on alyr.qa changed and nothing was published. No
Linear issue was given, so the lane brief and `docs/status.md` are not updated: the overall state
did not change.

  - **1024:** scale 0.68.

    the first screen, which keeps 360 px for it under the hero.
  - **Arabic:** the whole drawing is mirrored, and "#1001" is drawn unmirrored.

**The drawing in brief:**
- One single-weight line, 1.4 px on screen at every scale.
- Every ruled line is regenerated by a seeded "hand": it overshoots its ends by about 2 px,
  bows by under a pixel and wavers by under half a pixel.
- Three takes of every line, shown one at a time at 8 fps, make the boil. The thread itself
  does not boil: it is the one steady line.
- Ink is `ALYR/d-hair-2`, which has the same values as `--hair-strong` (Carbon at 14% on
  Paper, white at 13% on Carbon).
- The thread's last 150 units are `ALYR/line-strong`. Its tip is a Volt dot with a Carbon
  (chalk in dark) ring.
- The thing just fixed gets a short Volt highlighter stroke under its ink: the van's job card, a
  ticket's top edge, a notebook tick, the receipt's first line, a week slot or this week's
  bar.
- The headline, lead and buttons sit on empty paper: no stroke of the drawing crosses them at
  1440, 1024 or 390, in either language.

## The loop: 24 s, one day

Times are from the component's own schedule (`buildDay`). The measured run is below.

| Time (s) | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0.15–1.55 | The bakery's, then the salon's shutter rolls up. |
| 1.6–4.6 | Seven loose messages leave the phone (bubbles, a voice note, two "?"); a tangle line draws between them. |
| 4.8 | The thread enters along the street from the text side. |
| 6.0 | It passes the van: the job card ticks (the first Volt). |
| 6.85–8.95 | It lassoes the tangle; the "?" marks fade. |
| 9.06–10.09 | Five bubbles fly to the rail and become tickets, one Volt edge each. The first is "#1001". |
| 11.43–11.91 | Three ticks on the notebook. |
| 12.65–13.15 | The receipt prints and its line takes the Volt. |
| 14.81–16.32 | Seven of the salon's week slots fill, one at a time. |
| 17.35–17.83 | The two bars rise in their card; Volt on this week's top. |
| 18.1–21.9 | Calm: the phone shows one neat line and three wisps of steam rise from the karak glass. |
| 20.4–22.6 | The thread runs back along the street to where it started. |
| 22.9–23.9 | The thread, tip and bars fade; the shutters close over the day's work; inside resets. |
| 24 = 0 | Shutters down. Next morning. |

**Still frame** (reduced motion, Framer's canvas) is 19.2 s: everything put right, the Volt on
this week's bar, the tip resting at the card.

**Measured** in the local harness, headless Edge, 1440 English, with no screenshots during the
run. A sampler read each Volt mark's opacity every frame for 26 s.
- Every Volt mark lit within about 0.05 s of the schedule, after a constant 2.05 s offset from
  the page mount to the start of sampling:
  - the van at 3.96;
  - tickets from 7.01 to 7.97;
  - ticks at 9.38, 9.62 and 9.86;
  - the receipt at 11.10;
  - slots from 12.76 to 14.26;
  - the bar at 15.77, off at 20.88.
- **No more than one Volt mark at a time** besides the tip. The first run found two notebook
  ticks and two calendar slots sharing a time; they were spread at least 0.24 s apart, and
  their keyframes were fixed.

## Behaviour checked (harness, headless Edge)

- **Pause:**
  - Pressing Pause at about 7 s holds the tip still for 4 s.
  - The button's name changes from "Pause the drawing" to "Play the drawing".
  - Play moves it again.
  - The same holds in Arabic at 390: «إيقاف حركة الرسم» and «تشغيل حركة الرسم».
  - Pausing also stops the boil.
- **Off screen:** scrolled 3,000 px away, the tip did not move for 3 s, and it moved again on
  return. This was checked in both languages.
- **Reduced motion:** the 19.2 s still shows, there is no Pause button, and nothing moves.
- **Seamless loop:** the 24 s state equals the 0 s state (shutters down, nothing drawn). The
  recordings run across the seam.

## What Framer could not do, or did only by workaround

- **The canvas does not run CSS animations**, so the first canvas shot showed the start styles:
  shutters down, and the tip floating over the headline. The component now computes every
  animated value at 19.2 s from the same keyframe stops, with the same easing, and writes them
  inline on the canvas and with reduced motion.
- **The canvas may not measure the component** (its rect came back as a placeholder), and on
  the phone the drawing escaped its box. Layout is now pure CSS: the stage is anchored by
  `bottom` and `right`/`left` at a fixed scale, and the street runs 1,800 units past the start
  edge and is clipped. Measuring only sets where the thread enters. On the phone the instance is
  absolutely positioned at the bottom of the first screen, which has 360 px of padding.
- **Line endings:** a Windows edit gave the stored source CRLF, and Framer's `files.write` diff
  then failed ("Invalid Context"). The file was deleted, recreated with LF, and the instance was
  inserted again. A new code file's controls apply only after it compiles, so they were set in
  a second program.
- **No `ariaLabel` on SVG nodes,** so the logo has no accessible name in the prototype. The site
  uses `role="img"` and `aria-label`.
- **No `dir` on text:** the `withArabic` override, as before, applies in Preview only.
- **Motion plays only in Preview,** and the agent cannot sign in to Preview. The timing above is
  from the local harness running the same source.
- **The strip's label and hint overlap** at 1024 (the linter's "−100 px"), as on every earlier
  prototype page. The laptop hint was shortened to "One day in 24 s. Moves in Preview."

## Departures from the rules (each needs a yes before this goes near the site)

1. **An illustration on the homepage.** `alyr-design` §1.6 says "The product is the
   illustration" and allows no people, skylines or coffee pots. The founder asked for doodle art,
   so this study follows his request. It keeps the rest of §1.6:
   - no people, no skyline, no landmark, no logos;
   - one karak glass as the only local touch.
   If he adopts it, §1.6 needs a written exception and a D-number.
2. **It loops.** That is allowed by D-059, with Pause, an off-screen stop and a reduced-motion
   still. `motion.md` rule 1 still reads "plays once" and does not name this figure.
3. **Values that are not tokens:**
   - the pen width (1.4 px, and 0.9 px for digits and hatching);
   - the tip's 4.2 px radius;
   - the highlighter widths (4–6 px);
   - the 8 fps boil;
   - the thread's ease-in-out and the shutters' ease-in.
   Colours, the arrival ease (`--ease`) and the type are tokens. If the drawing is adopted, these
   should become `motion` and `drawing` tokens.
4. **Volt on light paper:**
   - the tip has a Carbon ring;
   - the "fixed" mark is a Volt highlighter stroke behind ink, never a Volt line on Paper.
   This is consistent with "Volt only where something is true", but it is a new use.

## Open for the founder (Needs Akmal)

1. **Is a drawn street right for ALYR's homepage at all,** given the brand's "the product is the
   illustration"? This study is the case for it; the call is his.
2. **The two weeks' bars:** this week's bar is taller, with no numbers. It still suggests ALYR
   raises sales. Keep it, make the bars equal, or drop the card?
3. **The karak glass** as the one local touch. No barjeel, palm or figure was drawn, for the
   reasons in 01-concept.md.
4. **"#1001"** is the site's first sample order number, drawn by hand on the first ticket.
5. **Density and contrast:** the ink is 14%. If it should be quieter or bolder, that is one
   value (`C.ink`).
6. **Pace:** 24 s for the whole day. Each step is about 1 to 2 s; the calm holds about 4 s.
7. **The Arabic** strings are the live site's; the Pause names are the site's existing draft
   (ALY-66).

## Not checked

- Framer's own Preview: the loop, Pause, the off-screen stop and `withArabic` there. Only the
  canvas (the still) and the local harness were seen.
- **A hidden tab.** It uses the same pause path as off screen (`visibilitychange`), but was not
  switched in a real browser.
- Safari, Firefox, a real phone and touch (Edge headless only).
- 320 px. Framer's phone breakpoint is 390, and the harness was not run at 320.
- The 1024 and 390 layouts in dark in Framer (dark was seen at 1440 in both languages on the
  canvas, and at 390 Arabic in the harness).
- axe, a screen reader and keyboard order on the Framer pages. The drawing is `aria-hidden`
  and the Pause button is a real button with a name. In the harness, Tab was not walked.
- Contrast was not measured. The headline, lead and buttons sit on empty paper (no stroke
  crosses them), and the ink is a token at 13 to 14%.
- CPU and battery cost on a slow phone. The drawing is 106 SVG paths (69 of them boil takes
  swapped at 8 fps) and about 60 CSS animations.
- How it sits with the rest of the homepage below the hero; only the first screen was built.
