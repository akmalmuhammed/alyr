# Discovery on a real business, recorded by hand

For ALYR staff. This is how a business gets a real Discovery check-up before Google is connected
(D-169, `specs/065-discovery/spec.md` "As built: a real business"). Nothing here reads Google or any
outside place, sends anything or uses a model: you look at the public pages yourself and record what
you saw. The owner reads the result on their own Discovery screens as "Recorded by ALYR".

**Before you start.** The client has agreed in writing that ALYR may look at their public listing and
website and record what it sees (the authority letter; how long ALYR keeps it is ALY-51). Record a
rating, a date and whether the business replied for each review, **never a reviewer's name or words**
(the form and the API both refuse them).

1. **Open the business.** `/ops/clients`, the client, the business's row, **Discovery check-up**. If the
   page says Discovery is not switched on, press **Switch Discovery on for this business** (audited).
2. **Places.** One row for each place the check covers, named as the business writes it, with its website
   and its WhatsApp number (`+974...`). **Save places.**
3. **Turn on the recorded source.** One source is on at a time. A real business is never offered the
   made-up sample (D-169), so this is the only source it has.
4. **Record what you saw**, place by place, with the day you saw it and where (the public Google Maps
   page, the contact page of the site). Tick only the blocks you looked at: a block left alone is "not
   checked" and scores nothing, which is different from "nothing there". Say it out loud when you looked
   and found nothing: **No listing found**, **None shown** (hours), **None set** (categories), **None on
   the page** (WhatsApp link). Reviews go one a line: `4, 2026-11-10, no` (stars, the day it was posted,
   whether the business replied). Enter every review you can see, or at least the newest ten to twenty,
   and put the listing's own review count in the listing block.
5. **Run check-up.** Only you can: the owner's button is off for a recorded source. The score is the same
   twelve rules as any source; with one place and a few blocks it is coarse, so do not quote it as a trend
   after one round.
6. **The owner fixes things by hand** (or you do, with their written yes, as Manager on the listing).
   They press "I did this". It is **not** confirmed by the next check-up: it is confirmed by the next
   **observation**. Record a new round after the change, then run the check-up. If the rule now passes
   the finding is verified; if not it opens again.
7. **Old rounds.** After 14 days the log and the owner's screen call the recording old. Record again.

What you cannot do here, on purpose: approve or apply a change (a recorded source makes no proposal and
nothing is asked on WhatsApp), schedule a check-up, or enter a review's words. A correction is another
round; rows are never edited or deleted (the database refuses it).

**Switching it off.** Press **Turn it off** on the same page: what was recorded is kept and the owner's
Discovery goes back to "not connected yet". Erasing the business erases the recordings.
