# Name Ideas for the Asset Tracker

## Naming direction

The name should feel:

- **Personal**, like someone or somewhere the family trusts
- **Warm**, rather than technical or financial
- **Private and dependable**
- **Timeless enough to last across generations**
- Broad enough to hold **assets, documents, memories, stories, and handoffs**

Possible brand format:

> **[Name] by ALYR**
> Private asset continuity for your family.

---

1. **Alma by ALYR** — the most emotional and timeless

## Recommendation

### **Alma by ALYR**

**Why:** It is simple, beautiful, personal, and emotionally aligned with a product that protects both the material and human meaning of family possessions.

**Descriptor:** Private family asset vault
**Primary line:** *Everything that matters, remembered.*
**Alternative line:** *A lasting home for your things and their stories.*

---

## Before choosing the final name

This is a creative shortlist, not a legal clearance result. Before launch:

1. Check trademark conflicts in the intended countries and product categories.
2. Check the domain and matching social handles.
3. Search the Apple App Store and Google Play for confusingly similar products.
4. Test the final three names aloud with potential customers.
5. Confirm that spelling and pronunciation remain easy across target languages.
