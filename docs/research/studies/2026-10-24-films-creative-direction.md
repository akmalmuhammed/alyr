## Question it answered
What films should ALYR make, built on what idea, and how do they stay true to what is built today? It sets one 90-second hero film, six 30-second product films, cut-downs, a shared series system and a production plan.

## Key findings
- One idea: ALYR takes the other job. It comes to your shop, sets your business up as one system, and keeps improving it with you. Line: "You have two jobs. We take one." It hands over to the homepage headline and "Book a check-up".
- Hero: three concepts judged on one test (after one viewing, can an owner say what ALYR does?). A, "The other job", recommended, with C ("Dashed to solid", the working drawing) as its middle act; B ("Big team, one owner") becomes a 6-second bumper.
- Hero story: Huda of the fictional Qirfa Bakery, still answering chats at 23:40; a check-up visit draws her business as dashed lines that turn solid as one order travels Shelf, Chat, Accounts, Docs and Agent; Sunday 09:00 her week on one screen; at 21:10 she switches off the light. 107 words of English voice-over in 90 seconds.
- Twenty principles, each tied to a confirmed source (Slack 2014, Apple "The Underdogs" 2019, Shopify 2019, Square 2017, Squarespace 2025, Mailchimp "Momentum" 2020 and others). Examples: one idea a film carried by one turn; a clock for the hero; sound designed first; readable with sound off. LinkedIn: 79% of feed videos watched with sound off; Meta: captions lifted view time about 12%.
- Research limits it states: nobody watched the films with sound; several brief references could not be confirmed and are not cited (Stripe Sessions openings, some Linear, Notion Mail and Arc launch films, an Ordinary Folk Apple film, Giant Ant for Stripe or Google).
- Honesty pass: eleven moments from the brief would claim too much and were changed. The hero order goes on account, not a card payment; the Sunday report appears with its approval condition; statements dropped; Docs shows receipts and says "Set up for you"; no film says "your number" or "live"; no clinics; no prices; "Agent" always with its rule line.
- Six product films share six beats (pain, friction, fix, hand-off, result, end card) across three fictional businesses (Qirfa Bakery, Dafira Salon, Zilal Cooling).
- Series grammar: arrive along 45°, settle, confirm with one Volt tick; open cold on the pain, close on the fold; one true silence a film; sonic logo under 1.5 s; mix to −14 LUFS, −1 dBTP.
- Arabic is its own film. Recommendation: Gulf Arabic voice, MSA on screen; Arabic may run 95 s.
- Production: hybrid route, one live-action shoot day in Doha (about 55 of the hero's 90 s plus product-film inserts), the rest made in code. Make the ALYR Chat film first, in code. Costs come as quotes. Retire the three October 60-second films as masters.
- The doc records founder answers dated 24 Oct to questions 1 to 6: actors on screen yes; the line yes; keep the monthly review; the check-up is free; Gulf voice with MSA on screen (narrator gender still open); budget per tier after quotes. Questions 7 to 10 (call-to-action target, name checks, timing, retiring October films) are open.
