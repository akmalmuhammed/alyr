## Question it answered
How do small businesses in Qatar and the Gulf market themselves, which advertising, data and WhatsApp rules apply to ALYR's own marketing, and how present are competitors and neighbours on social media? Three research passes. The search budget ran out, and several official Qatar law sites would not load, so many legal points are unverified and listed as questions for the lawyer, not advice.

## Key findings
- **Click-to-WhatsApp ads.** If a person messages from a click-to-WhatsApp ad or a Facebook Page button (on the phone app) and the business replies within 24 hours, all messages are free for 72 hours (Meta pricing page). Meta moved to per-message pricing on 1 Jul 2025. Qatar got its own rate line from 1 Jul 2026, with higher utility and authentication rates (USD 0.0091 to 0.012 per message, YCloud; Woztell says 0.0110 to 0.0144). Qatar marketing is about USD 0.0341 (likely, unverified). No Qatar ad-cost benchmark (CPM, CPC) was found; UAE Meta CPM averaged about $19.20 (Superads).
- **Qatar reach (DataReportal Digital 2026, ad reach).** TikTok 2.95M (flagged as too high), Facebook 2.40M (76.6%), YouTube 2.31M (73.8%), Instagram 1.70M (54.3%), Snapchat 1.24M (39.5%), X 650K (20.8%). 3.10M internet users (99%).
- **Snapchat in Qatar (Snap and GWI, Oct 2025).** Users are 316% more likely to be Qatari nationals, 38% more likely to be Gen Z and 25% more likely to be high-income than average; 47% more likely to swipe up on Stories than the GCC average; about 70% interested in health, fitness and beauty.
- **Programmes and partners.** Web Summit Qatar runs 31 Jan to 3 Feb 2027 with a startup programme. QDB requires a Qatari applicant, or a 51% active Qatari partner, for finance and advice; its Incubation Hub claims 6,000+ incubated companies. Sadad, Dibsy and Tap all state Qatar Central Bank licences or approval and promote payment links over WhatsApp. Ooredoo Money runs a merchant sign-up campaign until 4 Mar 2027.
- **Qatar advertising rules.** No influencer licence is in force; the Shura Council only proposed one (23 Dec 2024). The Ministry of Culture advertising and PR licence costs QAR 5,000 to issue and QAR 5,000 a year to renew (cut from QAR 25,000 and 10,000 in January 2024; Qatar Living, Oct 2026). Law No. 7 of 2019 requires Arabic names and trademarks, with Arabic most prominent and fines up to QAR 50,000. Whether it covers social ads is unverified.
- **Privacy (PDPPL, Law No. 13 of 2016).** Electronic marketing needs prior "affirmative, explicit and unambiguous" consent. Each message must name the sender and offer an opt-out. Fines go up to 5 million (DLA Piper).
- **Neighbours.** UAE Advertiser Permit in force from 1 Feb 2026 (free for three years; promoting your own product on your own account is exempt). Saudi Mawthooq licence costs SAR 15,000 for three years, with penalties up to SAR 5M.
- **WhatsApp Business rules.** Opt-in must name the business and may be collected by any method. Sending limits per business portfolio from 7 Oct 2025 (new portfolios start at 250). Per-user caps on marketing messages.
- **Competitors' social presence (counts read 18 Oct 2026).** Global WhatsApp tools are big on LinkedIn but English-first: Wati 222,372 LinkedIn followers (Arabic homepage, USD prices from $119 a month), respond.io 99,565, Gallabox 7,694 (the most Gulf-active, with a Mall of Qatar testimonial). Gulf platforms: Foodics 428,775, Tamara 317,630, Salla 165,872, Zid 115,639. Fresha has 90,460 and lists Qatar (Lusail). In Qatar: Snoonu 141,620, Dibsy 8,625, SkipCash 8,079, and Fatora (the closest local analogue, Arabic-first, free tier then QAR 130 a month, 2,897 LinkedIn followers).
- **Patterns that work.** Founder and staff posts beat brand posts (SkipCash CEO 146 reactions vs company 17). Partnership news gets the most engagement (Tamara and Tamimi: 345). National and religious moments in Arabic do very well (Salla's prayer post about 10x its usual). Event posts beat product posts for WhatsApp tools (Gallabox 44 to 54 vs 2 to 5). The Qatar bar is low: local fintechs have about 3K to 9K LinkedIn and 0.7K to 12K Instagram followers.
