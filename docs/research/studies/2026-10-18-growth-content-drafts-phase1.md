## Question it answered
What would ALYR post first, where and when, if the growth plan were approved? Three drafts: a calendar for Phase 0 and the first four weeks of Phase 1, a first batch of founder posts for LinkedIn and X, and seven short-video scripts. None of it is published or approved.

## Key findings
- **Calendar.** Phases, not dates: week 1 starts once the founder has claimed the handles and approved the bank of posts. Phase 0 claims @alyr (or the closest name) on Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, X and Threads, opens a WhatsApp Channel, writes two-line bios in English and Arabic, names an Arabic reviewer and sends the lawyer questions.
- **Four weekly themes:** week 1 who we are (pinned "What ALYR is", manifesto, Owner playbook 01); week 2 the product, honestly (FIG 01, Rule 01, no-shows playbook); week 3 owners' voices before customers (Myth / fact, agent feed, a WhatsApp poll); week 4 Doha rhythm (holiday hours, timed for National Day on 18 Dec, and a month in review). Shipped every Thursday. Rule for every post: no price, no offer, no "sign up" before the CR.
- **Daily habits:** reply to every comment in the first hour, 10 replies a day to others in the Gulf startup and SME scene on LinkedIn and X, and answer DMs.
- **Founder posts (LinkedIn L1 to L5, X X1 to X8),** in English, in the founder's voice. Topics: why he is building ALYR (orders as voice notes, payment as "send me the transfer screenshot"); the first real round trip on Meta's test number on 12 Oct (order, test payment, booking); why "our agent has no AI" (every alert shows its rule, for example "no answer for 10 minutes"); building a company as one person with six lanes; National Day messages for owners. Links go in the first comment on LinkedIn. Some lines are marked "[confirm]" for the founder.
- **Video scripts (V1 to V7),** all from one 1080×1920 master, message on screen in the first second, captions burned in both languages, no WhatsApp logo, samples labelled. Coded in HyperFrames: V1 "This afternoon, done for you" (12 s agent feed for a sample salon), V2 "The order, in the chat" (15 s, sample bakery), V6 "Rule of the week" (8 to 10 s). Filmed on the founder's phone: V3 "Five settings that stop lost orders" (30 to 40 s), V4 "Friday rush" street question (with written consent), V5 "On the road" visit days, V7 founder talking head (45 to 60 s).
- **Start with what exists:** the four films made on 18 Oct (a 48 s launch film and 60 s Bookings, Chat and Agent films) get 9:16 and 1:1 cutdowns of 15 s and 6 s first.
- **Voice:** the founder's own voice in both languages until a Gulf Arabic AI voice passes a blind test with 3 to 5 listeners.

## What we decided because of it
- Nothing. The drafts were never approved or published.

## What changed since, and conflicts
- **Founder-led posts conflict with D-043** (19 Oct, accepted by the founder, on branch `origin/company-introduction`, not merged): the brand speaks alone, no founder name or photo on the site, deck or profiles, and people on visits are "the ALYR team". The founder LinkedIn and X batch, the founder-to-camera videos (V3, V7), "On the road" (V5) and the founder's headline "Building ALYR in Doha" go against this. To reuse them, they would need to be rewritten in the brand's voice or kept to the founder's private network.
- **Positioning and words.** The drafts predate D-047 ("Stop losing orders in the chat.") and D-048 ("The tech team your business never had.", "Book a check-up", "rule" becomes "reason/why" on the homepage). The "RULE ·" lines and "FIG" series need checking against D-048 and the founder's 24 Oct "Sample" mark that replaced FIG captions (brand.md).
- **AI wording.** L3 and X3 say "our agent has no AI" and "We'll add smarter things when they're reliable". D-047 and D-048 say the agent is shown, never claimed, with no AI words. A post about AI, even denying it, needs checking against that rule.
- **Still true:** no prices before the CR (D-034), the Carbon and Volt look (D-040), sample businesses labelled, and no "live in 48 hours" or "keep your number" (D-025).

## Use it for
- Read the full drafts when the Growth lane writes the first real posts or video scripts. The calendar structure, series and script timings can be reused. The voice and positioning must be brought up to D-043, D-047 and D-048 first.
