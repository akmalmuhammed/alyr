## Question it answered
What exact words (English final, Arabic draft) should the suite homepage and the three new product pages (`/bookings`, `/agent`, `/accounts`) use, and which claims are true enough to publish?

## Key findings
- Positioning: ALYR is "the business agent for owner-run businesses in Qatar": a company that sets up the tech, plus a suite on one workspace. "Agent" in the old sense, someone who works on your behalf and reports back, by fixed rules the owner can check. The word never stands alone; a rule line or "by rules you can check" always sits beside it.
- Three ranked hero options. Recommended: "Know what needs you today. / And how your week is going." The figure beside it shows exactly that, it holds for every business type, and it names a worry (what am I missing while I serve) rather than a growth promise. Option 2 kept the live "Go digital. Without the IT team."; option 3 ("Every order, booking and riyal owed. On one screen.") was judged to over-promise.
- Full homepage copy: Products menu with states, an Agent spotlight with six numbered callouts, the suite as ruled rows, "By business" rows each with an honest limit (no table bookings; one booking at a time; clinics keep appointments, not patient files), how it works in four steps, trust, 16 questions, closing call, footer.
- Full copy for `/bookings`, `/agent` and `/accounts` on the shared template. Also what `/chat` must lose now that bookings and credit have their own pages, including the sample "Smart thermostat" renamed "Digital thermostat" under the word ban.
- `/agent` shows six real rule lines, word for word from the engine, and two principles: compare the same days, never yesterday; small numbers stay counts ("7 · last week 4").
- A claims table of 45 rows with state, source and condition. Key conditions: the Sunday report was built but never sent to an owner (it needs the `weekly_report` template approved); payment links run on a stand-in page (ALY-17), so the sample row "QAR 3,706 paid through ALYR links" must be hidden; "staff see no shop-wide money" is true on the Agent screen only.
- Do-not-say list: AI, smart, intelligence (including "business intelligence"), learns, predicts, forecast, assistant, agentic, health score, top performer, projected revenue, "statements" for Accounts, "your data stays in Qatar".
- Arabic: never translate "Agent". Not «وكيل» (in Gulf business Arabic it means a registered commercial agent, and in tech Arabic an AI agent) and not «مساعد». Keep "ALYR Agent" with the descriptor «نشاطك بالأرقام», and carry "on your behalf" with «نيابةً عنك». Includes a term table and fixes to the Arabic sample fixtures (gender and dual agreement).
- Prices (from QAR 449 a month, QAR 150 per added product, Agent included, setup from QAR 1,500) sit in an appendix behind the offer switch.
- Not checked: native Arabic review (ALY-66), Meta template approval, inbound mail to hello@alyr.qa, the legal question on clinic data, whether the sample trade names are free in Qatar.

## What we decided because of it
- D-037 (17 Oct): pages `/bookings`, `/agent`, `/accounts`; the shared product page template; no AI words; no "paid through ALYR links" on samples (as this file advised); prices off until the CR.
- D-035 (17 Oct): "ALYR Agent" stays in Latin with «نشاطك بالأرقام», included with every product.
