## Question it answered
What does the Brand & Growth Playbook look like in practice? It is the playbook's visual companion: logo directions, palette, type, social templates, deck slides and the field kit, all drawn as specimens.

## Key findings
- Logo: three directions. A, the cobalt rounded-square "a" mark with lowercase "alyr", recommended because it works at 16 px and as a WhatsApp profile photo. B, a bilingual wordmark (alyr / ألير), for print. C, a bubble monogram, not recommended because it is too tied to chat. The Arabic wordmark was a placeholder needing a lettering designer.
- Colour: cobalt #2747F5 (6.35:1 with white text), ink #0D1017, mist #F4F5F7, a new sand #F3EDE4; product hues Chat green #12A150, Pay #E2552B, Books #7A5AF0, Sites #D99A0B. Ratio rule 70% neutral, 20% ink, 10% colour.
- Contrast checks it recorded: white on Chat green 3.37:1 fails; white on Pay 3.77:1 (large text only); ink on Sites 7.77:1.
- Type: Bricolage Grotesque and Geist for Latin, Alexandria and IBM Plex Sans Arabic for Arabic; Arabic about 10% larger, never letter-spaced or italic; Western digits in UI.
- Four social templates at 1080×1350: before/after reel cover, an Arabic carousel cover (delivery app vs direct order: QAR 37.50 vs QAR 50.00 on a QAR 50 order at an illustrative 25% commission), a testimonial card, and a tip-of-the-week card.
- Three of ten client deck slides, including a pricing slide: Starter 199, Growth 449, Business 899 QAR a month, plus 0.5% of online payments and a free 14-day pilot, all marked "draft prices".
- Field kit: an A5 leave-behind in English and Arabic ("Set up for you in 48 hours", the three prices, a QR to the rep) and an Arabic same-day follow-up message on a phone.
- Voice board "Numbers over adjectives", for example "Live in 48 hours. QAR 199 a month."
- The sheet itself labels the sample merchants, numbers and Arabic copy as drafts for layout.

## What we decided because of it
- D-040 rejected this look: Carbon and Volt is ALYR's brand; cobalt, per-product colours, lowercase "alyr", the "a" mark and these typefaces are retired.
- D-025 and D-034 kept "48 hours", QAR 199 and the free pilot off the site.

## What changed since, and conflicts
- Every visual value here is replaced by `design/tokens.json` (Carbon, Volt, Paper; Instrument Sans, IBM Plex Sans Arabic, IBM Plex Mono). See docs/brand.md.
- Prices, the pilot and "48 hours" contradict D-034 (from QAR 449, no free trial, money-back first month).
- The products shown (Pay, Books, Sites) do not exist; the suite is Chat, Bookings, Agent, Accounts, Shelf, Docs (D-035, D-046).
- Its testimonial card and stat chip ("0 missed orders · 30 days") are placeholders; the honesty rules forbid using them as real proof.

## Use it for
- History only: to see what the retired cobalt brand looked like, or to reuse a layout idea (testimonial card, leave-behind structure) rebuilt in Carbon and Volt. Do not reuse its colours, prices or promises.
