## Question it answered
What is ALYR's 90-second hero film, frame by frame? It is a production sheet: three concepts, seven acts on a time scale, 22 storyboard frames with English and Arabic voice-over, a sound plan, cut-downs, an honesty pass and the founder's decisions. Marked "Draft · Not approved".

## Key findings
- Logline: an owner who does two jobs, one they love and one that eats their nights, gets a tech team that takes on the second.
- Three concepts tested on one question (after one viewing, can an owner say what ALYR does?): A "The other job" recommended because it is the only one an owner feels; B "The org chart" becomes the LinkedIn cut ("Big businesses have a team for this. You have us."); C "One order" becomes the how-it-works film.
- Seven acts follow the work loop: Two jobs (0:00–0:10), The scatter (0:10–0:30), Check (0:30–0:40, the turn), Set up (0:40–1:04), See (1:04–1:14), Improve (1:14–1:22), The other job taken (1:22–1:30).
- Two worlds: live-action macro shots (hands and objects only, no faces) and the brand's working drawing with the product's real screens. The film crosses at the check-up and returns at closing time.
- Set-up act shows Shelf, Chat, tracking, Docs (bilingual receipt), Accounts (one-press reminders) and Bookings, then ALYR Agent with a "Needs a look" card and its reason.
- Sound: pings tuned to one key rise from about 96 to 128 BPM, cut to two seconds of silence, then a pen on paper as the turn; each settled product gets a Volt tick a step higher; a two-note sonic logo. No stock corporate track, no "Arabian" flavour cue.
- Cut-downs: 60 s, 30 s, 15 s, 6 s, 9:16, 1:1 and 4:5, a silent homepage loop under 10 s, and a separate Arabic master (diagonals mirror, pen draws right to left).
- Honesty pass against product state on 24 Oct: no payment shown (no provider connected), no WhatsApp logo or green, no prices or AI words, Docs "built and off for every shop", weekly report left out until Meta approves. Note: "No business uses ALYR yet."
- Closing line flagged as the founder's call because it promises a scope of work: "We take on the other one." or the softer "Keep the job you love. We'll help with the other one."
- Founder decisions listed: concept A, B or C; hands only or faces; the closing line; the voice and Arabic register; budget for one shoot day; publishing each version after the Arabic review (ALY-66).
