## Question it answered
How did other brands grow through distinctive social content, and what should ALYR's first 100 posts look like as a result? It is a brief for the design team: evidence first, then five series, 23 templates, five layout modes, the profile's first screen and posts 1 to 100.

## Key findings
- Overall finding: brands that grew fixed two or three visual constants, ran a few named series in fixed weekly slots, made the product the picture, and judged posts by saves, sends and private conversations.
- Formats (rated strong): Socialinsider's 2026 study of 67.5M Instagram posts found carousels 0.55% engagement, Reels 0.52%, single images 0.37%. Metricool: carousels get about 9 times the saves of single images. Instagram named sends as the strongest signal for reaching non-followers. Brand Reels of 45 to 60 s did best across 9.77M Reels (Jan to Jun 2026).
- Small teams with fixed rules (moderate): Duolingo's TikTok ran on two people, Figma runs six platforms with four, Quartr's poster series came from three designers.
- Teach one or two steps from the product (moderate): Owner.com's problem-aware videos brought the most sales. Rule for ALYR: about 70% of posts teach or show owner life.
- Named series (weak to moderate): We Are Social found Target's series posts got 3 to 14 times the engagement of one-offs, with no published method; 13 practitioner sources repeat the advice.
- From zero followers, posts furnish a profile rather than build an audience (strong). So: no countdown, three pinned posts on day one, seed reach with videos as Trial Reels.
- Gulf (moderate): in one week of Zid's uploads, an office skit and podcast clips drew 3,412 to 4,208 views; product Shorts drew 241 to 712. Snapchat users in Qatar are 316% more likely than the GCC average to be Qatari nationals, and 47% more likely to swipe up. Snapchat bans links to other messaging apps.
- Legal limits (strong): Qatar's ministry fined a delivery platform QR10,000 on 22 Apr 2026 for advertising discounts without approval; prices must appear in Arabic, with penalties up to 2 years in prison or QR1M. ALYR's own rule: no prices or offers before the CR.
- Plan: five series on fixed weekdays (Owner Playbook on Sunday, Rule of the week fortnightly on Monday, FIG on Tuesday, Shop talk on Wednesday, Shipped on Thursday) carry about 80% of posts. Counts: Playbook 20, FIG 18, Rule 9, Shop talk 18, Shipped 17, one-offs 18. 32 of 100 posts are gated (CR, approvals, consent, slogan or date), with a 12-post reserve bank.
- Nominal start Sun 15 Nov 2026, about 24 weeks at 4 or 5 posts a week. Phase 1 targets: 1,000 engaged followers in Qatar, 10 owner conversations a month from content, saves plus sends above 3% of reach on carousels. The north star is qualified owner conversations a week.
- Eleven principles, including: the product is the picture; one post, one idea, one signal; Arabic is an edition, not a translation; no AI words or visuals; every link carries a source code.
- Not read in full: the page draws the 23-template table and the 100 post rows from scripts. This card records the counts, rules and series specs; it does not list each post or template.
