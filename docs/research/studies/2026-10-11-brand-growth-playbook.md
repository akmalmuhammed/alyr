## Question it answered
How should alyr be branded, how should it talk, what content and sales materials does it need, and how does it grow in Qatar? It was the founder's first full brand and go-to-market plan, with a companion visual sheet (the alyr Brand Sheet).

## Key findings
- Positioning: the local tech partner that sets everything up for you, not another app. Brand idea "We do the tech. You run the business." / «علينا التقنية، وعليك شغلك». Master line kept from the old site: "Go digital without the IT team."
- Promise: live in 48 hours, your customers stay yours, a person answers on WhatsApp. Proof to show: a named merchant, a before/after number and a setup time.
- Architecture: one master brand, lowercase "alyr", with plain product names (alyr Chat, Pay, Books, Sites), each with its own colour. Model cited: Stripe, Shopify, Square, Careem.
- Visuals: keep the old site's cobalt #2747F5, Bricolage Grotesque and Geist; add Alexandria and IBM Plex Sans Arabic, a sand neutral and real RTL. It found white text on the Chat green fails contrast (3.37:1).
- Voice: helpful, plain-spoken, local, crafted; numbers over adjectives; Gulf Arabic on social and WhatsApp, MSA on contracts and invoices. Never "AI-powered" as the headline, never invented stats, never attack delivery apps by name.
- Content: four pillars (shop-floor stories 35%, owner playbook 30%, build with us 20%, Doha rhythm 15%), five posts a week from one weekly shoot, and a 30-day calendar. Measure saves and shares above 3% of reach.
- Sales: demo on the owner's own phone with their own menu (8-minute script), a 10-slide deck, an A5 bilingual leave-behind, five WhatsApp scripts. Draft prices QAR 199 / 449 / 899 a month, 0.5% of online payments, free 14-day pilot.
- Growth plays: Founding 25 programme, district-by-district sweeps, merchant-gets-merchant referrals (target 30% of new customers by month 9), a WhatsApp owners' club, micro-creators, payment-provider partners, SMEs Go Digital in month 1.
- Evidence it cited: Toast gets 1 in 5 deals from referrals and in-person meetings convert about 3x (SaaStr); 45% of buyers want pricing transparency (TrustRadius); about 60% of SMBs regretted a software purchase (Capterra 2025); 74.6% trust a business more when they can message it (Meta/Kantar 2025); 57% expect ROI within 3 months (G2 2024).
- Compliance note: Qatar's Ministry of Commerce suspended talabat for a week in September 2025 for misleading information, so every published number must be exact.
- It listed claims it could not verify and did not use (for example Linear's "$35K total marketing spend"), and said the Material Design and Apple HIG RTL pages could not be opened by its tools.
- One decision it left to the founder: how alyr is spelled in Arabic (ألير was a placeholder).

## What we decided because of it
- D-025: the site took the playbook's positioning and voice but kept the design system's look; held back "live in 48 hours", QAR 199, the free pilot and "licensed Qatari payment partner".
- D-040: the playbook's visual identity (cobalt, a colour per product, lowercase "alyr", the "a" mark, Bricolage and Geist) is retired; its positioning, voice and content plan still apply.
- D-034: prices became "from QAR 449", no free plan or trial, first month paid with money back.

## What changed since, and conflicts
- Retired: cobalt look, lowercase "alyr", the four product names and colours, QAR 199/449/899, the free 14-day pilot, "live in 48 hours" (D-025, D-034, D-040).
- The product line-up is now Chat, Bookings, Agent, Accounts, Shelf, Docs (D-035, D-046); there is no Pay, Books or Sites.
- Positioning moved on: D-047 led with the owner's problem, then D-048 made ALYR "the tech team your business never had." "We do the tech. You run the business." survives as a footer line.
- The playbook's founder-led posts and founder on camera conflict with D-034 (no founder name or photo on the site) and with D-043 on the unmerged `company-introduction` branch (the brand speaks alone).
- Its first audience (restaurants and cafes) differs from later priority lists (bakeries and wholesale first, per the 22 Oct audit).

## Use it for
- The voice rules, Arabic register by channel, objection answers, the demo script and outreach scripts, the measurement table, and the evidence library. Read the full source before reusing any of its numbers or its growth plays; ignore its look and prices.
