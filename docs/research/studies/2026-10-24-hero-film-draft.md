## Question it answered
What is the 90-second hero film, frame by frame, if `docs/growth/films-brief.md` is run as written? It is a draft for the founder, and nothing in it is approved.

## Key findings
- It confirmed three references and used them: Apple at Work "The Underdogs: Escape from the Office" (2022, Silver Lion, Creative B2B), Slack "So Yeah, We Tried Slack" (2014) and Shopify "Let's Make You a Business" (2019). The other references in the brief were not yet watched, so it does not cite them.
- Craft rules: one idea; contrast does the explaining (05:10 against 23:40, warm hands against cold phone light); "the transformation is the transition" (each object folds along 45° into the product screen that replaces it); sound plans the edit; two held frames; readable on mute.
- Three concepts: A "The other job" (recommended), B "The org chart" (a big company's chart collapses onto one owner; best for LinkedIn), C "One order" (one camera move following an order across the drawing). A carries B's line and C's single order inside it.
- 90 s at 24 fps in seven acts: two jobs, the scatter, check, set up, see, improve, the other job taken. A storyboard of 22 frames. Live action is hands and objects only, with no faces. Qirfa Bakery throughout, Dafira Salon for the booking beat.
- Voice-over of about 115 words. Lines 8 and 9, "Big businesses have a team for this. You have us.", are the homepage's own words. Closing line: "You have two jobs. We take on the other one." Arabic is written in MSA, runs about 10% longer and gets its own edit.
- A cue sheet: pings rising from 96 to 128 BPM, a hard cut to silence at 0:27, then a pen on paper as the loudest sound. Six rising Volt ticks, one per product, and a two-note sonic logo. No "Arabian" flavour cue.
- Cut-downs at 60, 30, 15 and 6 s, 9:16 recomposed, a LinkedIn cut from concept B, and a silent homepage loop.
- The honesty pass: no payment shown going through (no provider, ALY-17); no weekly WhatsApp report (waits on Meta); Docs said as set up, never live; reminders only when someone presses Send; no prices; no AI words.
- Production: route A (all in code, the animatic), route B (hybrid: one day of macro inserts with no faces; recommended), route C (full cast; not recommended). Six founder decisions.
