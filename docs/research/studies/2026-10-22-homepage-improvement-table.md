## Question it answered
How should the homepage be rebuilt so every visitor finds their own problem and sees their day with ALYR? It goes element by element (now, verdict, new words, new design) through 12 sections and the product-page template, with Arabic drafts and the founder's open questions.

## Key findings
- Six rules: the visitor's problem first; one action everywhere ("Message us on WhatsApp"); agentic, shown honestly (every step marked "ALYR does this", "your team taps once" or "next"); plain words, 8 to 15 per line; show, don't describe; proof you can check (price, money-back, a named person, the CR number).
- New page order: header, hero, problem finder (new), solutions by business, see it work (new), ALYR Agent, we guide you, all products, price, trust, questions, closing.
- Hero: H1 "Stop losing orders in the chat.", sub-line "Your agent in WhatsApp handles the routine. You run the business.", a "What do you run?" business picker, a "SAMPLE · fictional bakery" chip in the first screen.
- Problem finder: nine problem cards with their fix and product, plus one "next" card (payment links) with the dashed "not yet" style.
- Business tabs in sales order: bakeries, wholesale, boutiques, restaurants, salons, home services, something else; each with "your day", "with ALYR", products, "not yet" and a pre-filled message.
- "See it work": one order step by step; counter "ALYR did 4 of these 7 steps on its own. Your team tapped once."
- Agent section: "Your agent tells you what needs you, and why." with three reasons it is smart: compares like with like; ignores numbers too small (a percentage only when both weeks have 20 or more); shows its working.
- Price: first-month total line; worked result example (20 orders in 30 days); refund within 14 days; an optional founding offer (first 10 shops, half-price setup).
- New FAQ items, including "Is this AI?" ("does not use an AI model today"), "Will I lose my WhatsApp number?" (to check against Meta's coexistence rules first) and "How soon can I take orders?".
- Ten founder questions: WhatsApp number and reply time; naming a real person; setup fee QAR 1,500 or 2,500; when to switch the offer on; picker or plain cards; tab order; Shelf and Docs prices (suggested QAR 150 each); founding offer; the worked example; "within a week".
- Read in full. The Arabic drafts cover only the headline lines; the doc says the rest (tab bodies, flow steps, product template) would be drafted in the copy pull request.
