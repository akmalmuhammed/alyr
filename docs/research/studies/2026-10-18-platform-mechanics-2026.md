## Question it answered
How do Instagram, Threads, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Snapchat, LinkedIn, X, Facebook and WhatsApp Channels rank and spread content in 2026: their signals, formats, sizes, penalties and AI rules, and what that means for a small B2B brand in Qatar? Four research passes ran in parallel. The search budget ran out partway through and Reddit and X could not be read, so some points are marked unverified.

## Key findings
- **Instagram.** The head of Instagram named watch time, likes and sends as the top three signals (January 2025). Sends matter more for reaching non-followers. Carousels lead engagement in every dataset: Socialinsider 0.55% vs Reels 0.52% and images 0.37% (2025, by followers); Buffer medians carousels 6.9%, Reels 3.3% (by reach). Reels reach 1.36x more people than carousels (Buffer). Metricool (24.4M posts): carousels get 9x the saves of single images, and Reels average 8.5 s of watch time. Buffer: 3 to 5 posts a week is the sweet spot (+12% reach per post vs 1 to 2).
- **Instagram rules.** Hashtags capped at 5 (18 Dec 2025). Since 30 Apr 2026, accounts that repost others' work lose recommendations, now for photos and carousels too. Third-party watermarks (TikTok, CapCut, Canva) cut reach, but your own logo is fine. Professional accounts' public posts are indexed by Google since 10 Jul 2025. Clickable links in posts need Meta One Advanced ($49.99 and up, 15 Sep 2026); bio links, Story stickers and DMs stay free. Skip rate counts people who leave within 3 seconds.
- **TikTok.** Finishing a longer video is a strong signal, and follower count is not a direct factor (TikTok, 2020). Buffer (11.4M posts): more posting raises the ceiling, not the floor (median post stays at about 460 to 506 views; top 10% rise from 3,722 to 14,401). Best engagement at 15 to 30 s (6.0%); most views at 2 to 3 minutes (Socialinsider, 6M videos). Realistic AI content must be labelled. Creator Rewards are probably not open in the Gulf.
- **YouTube Shorts.** Up to 3 minutes. Since 31 Mar 2025 every start or replay counts as a view. 200 billion Shorts views a day (CEO letter, 21 Jan 2026). From 15 Jul 2025, mass-produced, templated or repetitive content is not monetised. Animation and AI-assisted scripts need no AI disclosure.
- **Snapchat.** Spotlight posts must be at least 5 s. Snap's 2024 guidelines rank down unoriginal content, engagement bait, off-platform links and QR codes, other platforms' names or logos, and wholly AI-made video from outside Snapchat, even when labelled. Qatar and the rest of the GCC are eligible for Snap payouts.
- **Gulf reach (DataReportal Digital 2026, ad reach, not users).** Qatar: Facebook 2.40M (76.6%), YouTube 2.31M (73.8%), LinkedIn 1.80M members, Instagram 1.70M (54.3%, +13.3%), Snapchat 1.24M (39.5%, +19.6%), X 650K (down 11.2%); TikTok 2.95M is flagged as too high. Snapchat reaches 72.9% of Saudi Arabia, so it matters more there.
- **LinkedIn.** Rebuilt its feed on LLM retrieval and a "Generative Recommender" (12 Mar 2026) that predicts dwell, comments and shares, and filters engagement bait. Document carousels are the top format: Buffer median 21.77% vs video 7.35% and links 3.81%. Replying to comments lifts engagement about 30%. A save is worth about 5x a like (AuthoredUp). Links in the post body cost reach, though sources disagree on how much (18.8% to 60%). "AI slop" classifiers now cover posts beyond one's network (views of AI slop down 40%).
- **X.** The 2026 Grok-based code (open-sourced 20 Jan 2026) weights a reply 5.0, a reply between mutual follows 15.0, a copied link 20.0, and a like 0.66; reports cost -234. Free accounts get a median under 100 impressions a post; Premium about 600 (Buffer, correlation). X said in July 2026 that it no longer penalises links.
- **Facebook.** In the US in Q2 2026, posts with a link got only 1.1% of Feed views; 36.6% of views came from recommended posts. All videos are now Reels (June 2025). Unoriginal reposting loses distribution (14 Jul 2025). A test caps link posts for pages without Meta Verified (from December 2025).
- **WhatsApp Channels.** The Updates tab is used by 1.5 billion people a day (June 2025). Viewer location and language weigh most in the directory, so name and describe a channel in both languages. Channels need a real phone number, cannot be run from the API, and allow up to 16 extra admins. Paid promotion and subscriptions are announced but not confirmed in Qatar.
- **Posting times.** Every published "best time" study is Western and assumes a Monday-to-Friday week. Qatar works Sunday to Thursday, so test locally.

## What changed since, and conflicts
- The facts are six days old and still hold. Several points are marked unverified in the source (TikTok bio-link rules, Snapchat Spotlight length, WhatsApp Channel paid features in Qatar). Check those before quoting them.
- Its takeaways lean on founder-led LinkedIn and "real human footage" of the founder. That conflicts with D-043 (on branch `origin/company-introduction`, not merged): the brand speaks alone, with no founder face on profiles.
- Its point that a WhatsApp logo or call to action may count as promoting another platform on Snapchat matches the brand rule of no WhatsApp logo.

## Use it for
- Read the full source before choosing formats, sizes, posting rhythm or link placement for any platform, or when a platform rule (originality, AI labels, links) matters for a post.
- Use its Qatar, UAE and Saudi reach tables when ranking channels for the GCC.
