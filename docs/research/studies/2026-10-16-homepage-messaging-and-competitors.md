## Question it answered
What should the homepage say, section by section in English and Arabic, to get a visited owner to send ALYR a first message? And how do WhatsApp-commerce and point-of-sale competitors sell themselves on their homepages?

## Key findings
- Competitor homepages, fetched 16 Oct 2026. Toast refused the fetcher. DineNORDER (Qatar, QSTP) had no site that could be found.

- What they share: self-serve first (no one promises a person in the shop), proof by scale, prices behind a link, and AI as the headline for WhatsApp tools. The Qatar pages localise only the headline.
- Five gaps ALYR can own: setup in the shop by a person; a QAR price on the page; risk reversal instead of logos; the owner's control (who answered, who dropped the order) instead of "growth"; and one partner for the chat and the rest of the tech.
- Who lands on the page, most valuable first: the owner the founder has just visited; a referral; someone from social; then programme reviewers. The page's one job is the first message, and its second job is to say what the founder says in person.
- Full copy for ten sections in English and draft Arabic. The Arabic was marked as needing native review (ALY-66). Hero: "Go digital. Without the IT team." with the eyebrow "We do the tech. You run the business." and the button "Message us on WhatsApp".
- Price: show "From QAR 449 a month" with a numeric setup fee, because "plus setup" alone reads as a hidden cost. Plus the first month with money back if an agreed result is missed.
- A 31-row claims table checked every line against the code: payment links run on a stand-in page; connecting a shop's own number was not built; "hosted in Qatar" is not true (demo database in Frankfurt); and "AI assistants" are not in the product.
- "What not to say": no logos, counts or ratings; no "live in 48 hours"; no "licensed payment partner"; no "Meta partner" or WhatsApp green; no "AI-powered"; no "free trial".
- Five hero lines, ranked. "Go digital. Without the IT team." was first. "Know every order your team takes." was flagged as the owner-control angle no competitor uses.

## What we decided because of it
- D-034 (16 Oct) cites this study: no competitor in Qatar shows a price, so a price on the page matches what the founder says at the counter, and a money-back month replaces the logo wall.
- The build brief (`04-brief.md`) used its copy for the 16 Oct redesign. The founder's table (`00-founder-decisions.md`) settled the setup fee, the month-to-month term, and no founder name.

## What changed since, and conflicts
- **Hero line replaced** three times: "Your business just got an agent." (D-037), "Stop losing orders in the chat." (D-047), then "The tech team your business never had." (D-048).
- **CTA:** "Message us on WhatsApp" was never live. The site ships in the email state (hello@alyr.qa). Since 24 Oct, "Book a check-up" is the one action on every page (D-048 update).
- **Prices are off the public site** until the commercial registration (D-034, 17 Oct). The price section, hero price and money-back lines show only when the offer switch is on. The term now has 7 days' notice.
- **Positioning:** "ALYR Chat is the first product" became a suite led by ALYR Agent (D-035). Credit accounts is now ALYR Accounts; Shelf and Docs were added (D-046).
- **Who it is for:** "built first for restaurants and cafes", with "clinics and salons soon", conflicts with D-037 (no clinics), D-039 (nothing "coming") and D-043 (every owner-run business).
- **The founder's name and photo** in the trust section were removed (D-034).
- **Claims table:** many rows have moved, for example the Catalog (D-039) and Docs (D-042). Recheck against `docs/status.md` before you reuse a row.
- **Still valid:** the competitor scan and the five gaps, the visitor priorities, the "what not to say" list, and most FAQ objections.

## Use it for
