## Question it answered
What should a director (a person, a studio or an agent using HyperFrames or Remotion) be told so they can make ALYR's films for the "tech team" positioning? It is a paste-in prompt, not research. Its answers are in `films/DIRECTION.md` and `docs/growth/hero-film.md`.

## Key findings
- The director must study award-winning films first and confirm each reference exists. Named references: Apple at Work "The Underdogs", Slack "So Yeah, We Tried Slack", Shopify "Let's Make You a Business", Square, Mailchimp, Stripe Sessions, Linear, Vercel, Figma Config. Studios: Buck, ManvsMachine, Ordinary Folk, Oddfellows, Giant Ant.
- Nine principles to name: one idea per film; story before features; show the product doing; rhythm; transitions with meaning; sound leads picture; kinetic type readable on mute; restraint; the first two seconds and the last frame.
- Who ALYR is, from D-048: "The tech team your business never had.", "Book a check-up", and the loop check, set up, see, improve. "Not a WhatsApp tool."
- The problem is "the scatter": forty unread chats, staff on their own phones, prices in a notebook, stock in Excel, transfer screenshots, an empty chair, the debt notebook, the owner still working at 23:40. "The villain is the scatter, not the owner and not technology."
- Starting idea: "The other job" (every owner has two jobs; ALYR takes the one nobody sees), with the line "You have two jobs. We take one." A seven-beat, 90 s hero on the loop, and two more concepts asked for.
- The synergy is the story: one order or booking moving through the products, never six separate tools.
- Six 30 s product films on one six-beat template (pain, friction, fix, hand-off, result in Agent, end card), each with a pain and a visual idea.
- 12 additions: the human visit, placements and sizes, cut-downs, sound off, Arabic as its own film, a sonic identity, casting without clichés (no dunes, camels, falcons), production routes with cost tiers, a series system, success measures, the one CTA, and review before release.
