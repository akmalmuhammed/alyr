## Question it answered
What should the company homepage look like if alyr is a suite of small-business products starting with WhatsApp? It is a working homepage mock-up on the "design system v0.2" (the cobalt look), with a product suite, a live chat demo and a sign-up form.

## Key findings
- The page labels itself as placeholder: "product names, prices and figures are drafts until confirmed."
- Hero: eyebrow "Digital transformation for small business", headline "Go digital without the [IT team | big budget | new app | paperwork | headache]" with a rotating word, and an animated canvas of alyr at the centre with four products orbiting.
- Trust line under the hero: "Set up in a day", "No developers needed", "Pay monthly, cancel anytime".
- Product suite: alyr Chat "Live first"; alyr Pay, Books and Sites "Planned"; a "More on the way" card (loyalty, staff scheduling, delivery).
- Chat spotlight: "The chat is the checkout", with Order, Book, Pay, Manage, and a scripted chat at a sample shop priced in US dollars ($8.50).
- A four-step approach (Connect, Automate, Get paid, Grow) and five industries (restaurants, clinics, salons, retail, home services).
- A stats band (3x more orders answered after hours, 42% fewer no-shows, 1 day setup, 0 apps) explicitly marked "Sample figures for layout only."
- Closing call: "Bring your business online this week" with a work-email sign-up form (preview only; nothing is sent).
- A marquee of placeholder customer names and a footer with "Help centre" and "Partner programme" marked Planned.

## What we decided because of it
- Nothing directly. The Brand & Growth Playbook (same day) reviewed this site and asked for prices in QAR, an Arabic version, WhatsApp contact instead of work email, and the sample stats removed. D-023 then made the site company-first, and D-025 took the playbook's words on the design system's look.

## What changed since, and conflicts
- Look retired by D-040 (Carbon and Volt).
- Products replaced by Chat, Bookings, Agent, Accounts, Shelf, Docs (D-035, D-046); a product not built is not shown (D-046), so "Planned" rows are out.
- Sample stats, dollar prices, "Set up in a day" and a placeholder customer marquee all break the honesty rules (no invented metrics or logos) in docs/brand.md.
- Clinics are no longer an audience (health data waits on the lawyer, per the films doc).
- The live homepage line is "The tech team your business never had." with "Book a check-up" (D-048).
