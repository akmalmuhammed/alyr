## Question it answered
How should ALYR's films look and what should they say? CREATIVE.md (18 Oct) set the direction for three 60-second product films. DIRECTION.md (24 Oct) replaces it with one 90-second hero film and six 30-second product films on one system, built for the "tech team" positioning.

## Key findings
- **18 Oct (CREATIVE.md):** three 60 s films (Bookings "The empty chair", Chat "The chat is your counter", Agent "What needs you, first"). Each is a 16:9 master that also cuts to 9:16 and 1:1, told in type with the sound off, and closes on the old line "We do the tech. You run the business." Sound composed in code at 100, 118 and 108 BPM, mixed to −14 LUFS.
- What sells, from SaaS video research it cites (advids, vidico, Komet, Atomik): hook in the first 3 s with the viewer's own life; assume the sound is off; show the real product by second 10; one action per problem; short. Salon no-show vendor claims (Rekaz, Infobip) were read for understanding only, never quoted.
- What both versions refuse: invented numbers (no "cut no-shows by 89%"), anything not built (deposits, card payments, several chairs at once), AI words, the WhatsApp logo or green.
- **24 Oct (DIRECTION.md):** one idea, "ALYR takes the other job". Line: "You have two jobs. We take one." It hands over to the homepage H1 "The tech team your business never had." and "Book a check-up". Story: Huda, owner of the fictional Qirfa Bakery, at 23:40. A check-up visit draws her business as dashed lines, which turn solid as one order travels Shelf, Chat, Accounts, Docs and Agent. It ends at 21:10 with the light off.
- 20 craft principles, each tied to a film the author confirmed exists: Slack "So Yeah, We Tried Slack" (Sandwich, 2014), Apple at Work "The Underdogs" (2019), Shopify "Let's Make You a Business" (2019), Squarespace "A Tale as Old as Websites" (2025, Cannes Film Craft Gold), Mailchimp "Momentum" (BUCK, 2020), and others. It corrects the brief's dates and drops references it could not confirm (Stripe Sessions films, Linear "Asks", Notion Mail, Arc Search).
- Sound-off facts it uses: 79% of LinkedIn feed videos are watched muted (LinkedIn), and captions lifted view time by about 12% (Meta). 112 words of voice-over in 90 s.
- An honesty table changes 11 of the brief's moments so nothing claims what is not live. The order goes on account, not paid by link (no payment provider yet). The Sunday report is shown with its "waits for WhatsApp approval" condition. Statements are dropped. Docs shows a receipt and says "Set up for you". No "your number", "live", clinics, prices or AI.
- Three hero concepts: A "The other job", B "Big team, one owner" (a split screen) and C "Dashed to solid". It recommends A with C as its middle act, and B's line as a 6 s bumper.
- A series system: open cold on the pain, close on the fold, arrive-settle-confirm, one Volt signal a frame, and seven transitions each with one meaning. Six-beat 30 s product films, rotating across the three sample businesses.
- Production: the hybrid route. One live-action shoot day in Doha (a bakery, a salon chair, an AC van, four actors with releases, the visitor seen only from behind) and everything else in code with HyperFrames. Voice actors and a composer commissioned; costs as quotes. Make the ALYR Chat film first, in code.
- Arabic is its own film. It recommends Gulf Arabic for the voice and MSA on screen; this departs from the design system and needs the founder's yes. 10 open questions for the founder.
