## Question it answered
What rules must any ALYR slide, deck or social post follow, while ALYR has no customers, no usage numbers and no photos of real shops? This is the working rulebook, drawn from the brand and the playbook's formats, not a study with new research.

## Key findings
- Principle: the product is the illustration, honesty is design, one idea at a time. Growth drafts; the founder approves and publishes.
- Formats: slide 1920 × 1080 (96 px margins, at most 25 words, headline under 8 words); feed post or carousel 1080 × 1350 (6 to 8 slides); story, reel cover or TikTok 1080 × 1920 (no text in the top 250 or bottom 340 px); LinkedIn 1200 × 1500, English first.
- Look: Paper or Carbon only, never cobalt, sand or gradients. The 12-column grid, one Volt element per slide, the 45° cut and fold as the only graphic device, and the logo from master files at least 140 px wide. Nothing under 32 px on a 1080 canvas; headlines 72 to 120 px.
- The picture is the product: real screens marked "Sample" once in their own chrome. No stock photos, skylines, dhows, coffee pots, arabesque, 3D phones, AI imagery or emoji.
- Words: master line for decks "We do the tech. You run the business." MSA on slides and LinkedIn; light Gulf Arabic on Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat and Status where asked. Written in Arabic first, never machine-translated, natively reviewed. A greeting before any pitch, and no religious phrases in sales copy outside Ramadan and Eid.
- Honesty: no numbers about ALYR's customers or results; no testimonials or merchant faces without written consent; market facts only with a named, dated source; no prices until the CR, then only the decided wording.
- Retired playbook slides: "Set up in 48 hours", three plans, "local team", "licensed payment partner", "your data stays in Qatar", a pilot before/after table, and the founder photo.
- A 10-slide sales deck outline, from the cover with the master line to one next step, with price only after the CR and a live demo on the owner's phone.
- Done-when checklist: checked against `honesty.md` and `docs/status.md` that day, safe areas right, Arabic marked for review, samples captioned, saved as a draft.
