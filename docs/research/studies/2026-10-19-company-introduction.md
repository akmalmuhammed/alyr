## Question it answered
Who is ALYR, what does it say about itself in 10 seconds, 30 seconds and 2 minutes, and how does it win its first clients while it has no commercial registration (CR)? It was meant to be the one source for the site, deck, leave-behind, visit script and every profile.

## Key findings
- The founder decided on 19 Oct: every owner-run business, with no single target sector (each lead records its sector, so the sales tool shows which sectors buy fastest). The brand speaks alone, with no founder name or photo; people who visit shops introduce themselves as the ALYR team. Qatar first, then the GCC.
- The line: "We do the tech. You run the business." The 10 seconds: ALYR sets up and runs the tech for owner-run businesses in Qatar, on the WhatsApp their customers already use. Arabic drafts wait for the native review (ALY-66).
- 17 standard answers, each true on 19 Oct. Not for (yet): clinics and health data, large chains with their own IT, and businesses that do not use WhatsApp. "Who uses it?" gets the honest answer "no business yet", plus a demo on the owner's phone.
- Never say: "AI", "smart" or "learns"; "live" or usage numbers; a price, an offer or "live in 48 hours" before the CR; "keep your number" before ALYR is a Meta Tech Provider; any Meta endorsement; a competitor's name in an attack; anything about the founder.
- Trust without a face, from things an owner can check: ALYR's own WhatsApp number, the CR number and address in the footer, a visit kit (staff card, leave-behind, sample QR card, demo on the owner's phone), Google Business Profile and LinkedIn, the money-back first month, and a first case study with written consent.
- Six sector openers, each the pain in the owner's words, for example salons: "How many bookings didn't show up last week?"; groceries: "Who owes you money right now, and how much?"
- Phases. A, before the CR: visits logged in the sales tool, ending in a non-binding letter of interest, with no signature and no money. It proposes "founding shops": the first ten get their price fixed for 12 months in return for case-study consent and a referral. B: up to 10 paying shops. C: 10 to 50, including Web Summit Qatar (31 Jan to 3 Feb 2027).
- Measured weekly: qualified owner conversations; visits, demos and letters by sector; after the CR, shops paying, live and still paying in month 3.
- Open for the founder: ALYR's WhatsApp number, the legal name, the setup fee gap (site QAR 1,500 against back office QAR 2,500), founding shops, and whether "Agent" reads as AI in 2026.

## What we decided because of it
- D-043 (written on this branch; the PR is not merged, so D-043 is not yet on main's `docs/decisions.md`). Its rules are followed elsewhere: brand-only voice, no founder face, every owner-run business, Qatar then GCC.

## What changed since, and conflicts
- The positioning moved on. D-048 (23 Oct) makes ALYR "the tech team your business never had", with "Book a check-up" as the one action. The 10 s and 30 s pitches here lead with WhatsApp, which D-048 says is "one product and the way in, never the whole story".
- The product list predates D-046: ALYR Shelf and ALYR Docs are now named products. Payroll is not announced as "next" on the site (D-039, D-046).
- D-051 (23 Oct): a client pays before products open. The "Day 1 to Day 30" plan should be read with it.
- It conflicts with growth drafts and research that recommend founder-led posts (`2026-10-18-growth-content-drafts-phase1.md`, research 03). D-043 chose the brand anyway.
- Still valid: the never-say list, trust without a face, the sector openers and the pre-CR limits (D-034).

## Use it for
- Writing any profile, visit script, leave-behind or deck: start from the answers and the never-say list, then update the positioning lines to D-048.
- Planning the first client visits before the CR.
