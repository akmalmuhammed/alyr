## Question it answered
Which words do owners in Qatar type, in Gulf Arabic and English, about WhatsApp orders, bookings and no-shows, credit and debts, stock, and receipts, and which sector page (ALY-232) or owner guide (ALY-233) should answer each?

## Key findings
- 1,115 Google suggestions (616 English, 499 Arabic) from 198 seed phrases, collected with Qatar as the country on 25 Oct 2026. No volumes: Keyword Planner needs the founder's Google Ads account.
- Category phrases with "Qatar" return nothing ("whatsapp ordering system qatar", "salon booking app qatar", «نظام طلبات واتساب»). Owners search how to make the WhatsApp Business app do the job: auto reply, catalogue, multiple users, two phones. A WhatsApp Business setup guide answers more queries than any page.
- Debts: the owner's word is «دفتر الديون» / «تسجيل الديون» / «برنامج ديون العملاء»; «البيع بالآجل» returns religious rulings. English is South Asian: "khata app", "udhar app", "credit book app". The site's «الحسابات الآجلة» does not match.
- Stock: «برنامج كاشير», «برنامج جرد», "pos system qatar", nearly always with "free" or "price". ALYR is not a POS; an honest "Do you need a POS?" guide meets them.
- No-shows: searches ask for a policy template, a fee and reminder wording. In Arabic, «حجز موعد» is swamped by embassies and hospitals.
- Qatar rules with real demand: home-business licence, the 2026 e-commerce licence (Decision 25 of 2026), Arabic invoices. Dated guides could be the cited local source, after the company lane checks the facts.
- Gulf how-to is «كيف اسوي ...»; spellings vary (واتساب / واتس اب, بزنس / بيزنس, أعمال / اعمال).
- Reddit r/qatar could not be read (blocked); the Qatar Living forum no longer opens on its new site; "People also ask" and AI Mode were not available.
- Ten new questions for the monthly AI question set (ALY-240), at the end of the map.
