# alyr Brand & Growth Playbook
 · 

## Executive summary
Brand alyr as the local tech partner that sets everything up for you, not as another app: "We do the tech. You run the business." The evidence from Toast, Foodics, Linear and Mercury, and from B2B buyer research, says small-business owners trust people, proof and plain prices more than polish or hype. alyr's edge over global WhatsApp tools and delivery apps is being local, bilingual and hands-on.
The visual sheet with logo directions, palette, Arabic and Latin type, social templates, deck slides and the field kit is here: alyr Brand Sheet.
Ten decisions this playbook makes

Architecture: one master brand, alyr, with plain product names (alyr Chat, Pay, Books, Sites), each with one colour. This is the Stripe and Shopify model, and Careem's single mark across services.

Lines: keep the site's "Go digital without the IT team" as the master line; the brand idea is "We do the tech. You run the business." / علينا التقنية، وعليك شغلك.

Promise: live in 48 hours, your customers stay yours, a person answers on WhatsApp.

Voice: helpful, plain-spoken, local, crafted. Numbers over adjectives. Gulf Arabic on social and WhatsApp, MSA on contracts and invoices.

Visuals: keep the existing site system (cobalt #2747F5, Bricolage Grotesque + Geist) and add Alexandria + IBM Plex Sans Arabic, a sand neutral, and real RTL. Fix white text on the green Chat buttons, which fails contrast.

Site fixes: prices in QAR, an Arabic version, "Message us on WhatsApp" instead of "Work email", and remove sample stats until real numbers exist. Qatar penalises misleading claims.

Content: four pillars led by real shop-floor stories, owner tips that help, building in public, and Doha's calendar. Posts go out 5 days a week from one weekly shoot. TikTok, Facebook and YouTube have the widest reach in Qatar; WhatsApp is the most used.

Sales: the demo is the owner's own menu on their own phone. A 10-slide deck, an A5 bilingual leave-behind and five WhatsApp scripts cover the journey from referral to paid customer.

Growth: start with a Founding 25 programme, sell district by district, and run merchant-gets-merchant referrals. Add a WhatsApp owners' club, Doha micro-creators, and partnerships with payment providers. Apply to SMEs Go Digital in month 1.

Governance: one owner signs off everything public against a 12-point checklist. Each month, measure recall, saves and shares, visit-to-paid rates, time to first order, support response time and NPS.
One decision only you can make: how alyr is pronounced and spelled in Arabic. I used ألير as a placeholder. It sets the Arabic wordmark and every Arabic line.
Next 14 days: fix the site items in decision 6, print 200 leave-behinds, film the first two merchant videos, and launch the Founding 25. Have a native Gulf Arabic speaker review all Arabic copy before anything is printed.

## 1. Brand strategy
alyr should be positioned as the local tech partner that sets up and runs the software for you, not as another app. Restaurant owners in Doha already have too many apps and tablets; what they lack is someone who does the tech for them, in their language, and answers on WhatsApp. That is the gap global tools (Wati, respond.io) and delivery apps leave open. Assumption, to be tested in the first 40 restaurant interviews from the Qatar plan.

### Positioning statement
For owner-run restaurants, cafes and service businesses in Qatar who lose orders and time in WhatsApp chats and pay heavy delivery-app commissions, alyr is the digital partner that turns their chats into orders, bookings and payments. Unlike global chatbot tools or delivery apps, alyr sets it up for you in 48 hours, in Arabic and English, keeps your customers yours, and is one call or message away when something breaks.

### Brand idea and promise

Brand idea: We do the tech. You run the business. (Arabic: علينا التقنية، وعليك شغلك)

Promise to the owner: Live in 48 hours, your customers stay yours, and a real person answers on WhatsApp.

Proof we must be able to show: a named local merchant, a before/after number (orders, no-shows, commission saved), and a setup time.

### Iconography, illustration and photography

Icons: 24px grid, 2px stroke, round caps and joins, outline style (as on the site). Filled only for active states.

## 4. Product UI/UX rules
The product has two faces with different users: the customer inside WhatsApp (anyone, any phone, often in a hurry) and the owner and staff on the dashboard (busy, interrupted, often on a phone at the counter). Design every screen for the second user holding a phone in one hand during the Friday lunch rush.

### Seven principles

Fewest taps to an order. Customer path: greeting → menu → cart → pay or cash → confirmation. Five steps maximum, no account creation.

Use WhatsApp's native parts first (lists, reply buttons, catalogue, Flows) before sending people to a web page. Every link out is a drop-off point. Assumption; measure it.

The board is a ticket rail. Columns New → Preparing → Ready → Delivered, big cards, one tap to move, a sound and a badge for new orders. It should feel like the paper rail staff already know.

Bilingual by default. Every message template exists in Arabic and English; the customer's language is detected from their first message and remembered.

A person is always one tap away. Every bot flow has "Talk to the shop" (customer side) and "Message alyr support" (owner side).

Show money clearly. QAR, two decimals in UI, totals bold, fees itemised, receipts instant.

Never lose an order. Offline banner, retries, and a daily summary at closing time.

### Trust and payment moments

Payment link message shows: shop name, order number, itemised total in QAR, the licensed provider's name, and "Secure payment".

The link opens a page with the shop's logo, not alyr's; alyr appears only as "Powered by alyr" in small type. The merchant's brand is what the customer trusts.

Receipt arrives in the chat within seconds, with order number and a "Need help? Reply here" line.

Refunds: owner taps Refund → confirms amount → customer gets a message with the expected days.

Opt-in for broadcasts is explicit, with "Reply STOP to unsubscribe" (أرسل إيقاف لإلغاء الاشتراك) on every marketing message; this follows WhatsApp Business Platform policy and protects the number's quality rating.

### RTL rules

Set dir="rtl" and lang="ar" on the root; use CSS logical properties (margin-inline-start, padding-inline-end, inset-inline) everywhere so one stylesheet serves both.

Mirror layout, navigation, progress and "back/next" arrows. Do not mirror: media play buttons, clocks, phone numbers, prices, order numbers, brand logos, and checkmarks.

Keep Latin product names (alyr Chat) as embedded LTR runs; wrap them in <bdi> so punctuation lands correctly.

The phone mock in RTL flips bubble sides: incoming on the right, outgoing on the left, as WhatsApp itself does in Arabic.

Test every screen with long Arabic strings (Arabic text is often 20–30% wider in UI at equal legibility; assumption) and with mixed Arabic + numbers.

References: Material Design bidirectionality and Apple HIG right to left.

### Plays, in order

Founding 25 programme (months 0–3). Like Linear's hand-picked beta (First Round): 25 Doha restaurants get free setup, a locked launch price for 12 months, a "Founding merchant" window sticker and a direct line to the founders. In return: a filmed testimonial and permission to share their numbers. Scarcity makes it easy to say yes.

District sweeps. Sell street by street (Al Sadd, Old Airport, Bin Mahmoud, Al Wakrah, The Pearl), like Toast's dense-territory field model. Every live merchant is a reference two doors away. The window sticker and table QR codes are the local billboard.

Merchant-gets-merchant referral. One free month for the referrer and the new merchant per paid signup. Ask on day 30 when results are fresh (script 5 in section 6). Track "how did you hear?" on every signup. Target 30% of new customers from referrals by month 9 (assumption).

alyr Growth Club. A WhatsApp Community for owners, with one tip a week, a monthly breakfast at a member's cafe, and early access to new features. It mirrors Zid's named merchant growth programme (Arab News) and Snoonu's "Tamkeen" merchant fund (Gulf Times), at a size alyr can afford.

Micro-creators, not celebrities. Doha's top food creators are mid-sized (most under 35k followers) with high engagement (Modash). Pay or barter with 5–10 of them to order from alyr merchants on camera, with tracked links. Notion found creator signups 50–100x cheaper than search or social ads (Passionfroot). Follow disclosure rules; Qatar's influencer licensing is being drafted (Marhaba).

Partner channels. Payment providers (SkipCash, SADAD, Dibsy) refer merchants who need ordering; food distributors and POS resellers co-sell, as Toast does with US Foods. Offer partners a share of the first year.

Fortnightly "What's new at alyr" in Arabic and English (WhatsApp Channel, LinkedIn, site). Linear's changelog showed momentum to customers, hires and investors (Linear).

Two named seasons a year. Bundle features into a pre-Ramadan release and a September release, the way Shopify does with Editions (source). Ramadan pre-orders on WhatsApp is a ready-made campaign: Doha venues already sell iftar and suhoor boxes that need 24–48 hours' notice (Marhaba).

Own a data story (month 9+). Publish "How Doha orders on WhatsApp" from anonymised alyr data, as Stripe does with Atlas (Stripe) and Toast with its sales comparison tool. Data-backed content is what buyers rate highest (Edelman–LinkedIn 2024).
