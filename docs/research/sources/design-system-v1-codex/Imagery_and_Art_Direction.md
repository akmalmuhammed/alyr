# Imagery and Art Direction

## Point of view

Show transformation as work becoming visible: decisions, workflows, people collaborating, operational detail, environments in Qatar/GCC and tangible outcomes. The visual system should feel observed and directed by humans.

## Use

- Documentary images of real teams, environments and service delivery.
- Close operational details: handoffs, documents, dashboards, workshops and field activity.
- Quiet architecture, materials and daylight that echo Carbon, Mist and Volt.
- Real client context with consent and sensitive data removed.
- Diagrams built from the Command Fold geometry and signal lines.

## Avoid

- Glowing robots, synthetic brains and generic “AI future” visuals.
- Handshakes, floating holograms, stock call-centre poses or staged boardrooms.
- Unmotivated cyan/purple gradients and excessive glass effects.
- Images that exoticize Qatar or reduce the GCC to skyline clichés.
- Screenshots containing client data, QIDs, passports or confidential documents.

## Image treatment

- Prefer neutral, slightly cool grading with accurate skin tones.
- Keep contrast deliberate and avoid crushed blacks.
- Use Volt as a graphic annotation, never as a blanket color wash.
- Corners are square or subtly 8–12px rounded; avoid bubbly shapes.
