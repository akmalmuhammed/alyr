**What was asked.** A growth and scaling strategy for ALYR's launch, built on how content really spreads in 2026. Social media plans for Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn and X. 2. **The launch gate.** Until the Qatar commercial registration (CR) exists, ALYR may not sell, show prices or run paid ads (memory and `docs/company/customer-agreement/notes.md`). So the plan runs in phases: build an audience and trust now, sell after the CR.
3. **Platforms you didn't name.**
   - **Snapchat:** 1.24M in Qatar, +20% a year, skewed to young Qatari nationals with high income.
   - **YouTube Shorts:** 2.31M reach; a free cross-post.
   - **WhatsApp itself:** a Channel, Status, an owners' Community, and click-to-WhatsApp ads after the CR.
   - **Google Business Profile.**
4. **Growth beyond social:**
   - in-person sales turned into content;
   - a "booked with ALYR" mark in every customer's chat;
   - partners (payment providers, incubators, suppliers);
   - referrals;
   - events (Web Summit Qatar, 31 Jan to 3 Feb 2027).
5. **Two audiences, two voices.** Owners (Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat, Facebook, WhatsApp, in Arabic and English). The ecosystem: partners, investors, press and future hires (LinkedIn and X, founder-led, in English).
6. **A production engine, not a list of posts.** Templates in code that render ALYR's real design system, a weekly rhythm, an approval checklist, and an AI policy that keeps the brand from looking machine-made.
7. **Honesty as a competitive edge.** No AI claims, no invented numbers, sample businesses labelled. In a market where Qatar suspended a delivery app for misleading claims (playbook, section 6), this is a feature.
8. **Legal checks before any campaign** (section 12): consent to collect leads (Qatar's privacy law, PDPPL), Arabic in ads, whether marketing before the CR is allowed, and influencer rules.

## 2. Strategy in one page

**The model.** ALYR grows the way Toast, Foodics and Fresha did with small businesses: in person first, with proof made visible and spread by owners to owners. Content does three jobs in that loop:

1. **Make the founder's visit expected.** An owner who has seen ALYR on Instagram or Snapchat says yes to a 10-minute demo.
2. **Show the product honestly and often.** Drawn chats, the agent's rule lines and real screens: the "working drawing" look no competitor has.
3. **Turn every live customer into proof** (after the CR): their numbers, their face and their words, with written consent.

**The flywheel.** Visit → demo on the owner's own phone → set up → their customers see "Booked with ALYR" in the chat → proof post (with consent) → neighbours and followers reach out on WhatsApp → the next visit is warm.

**The one metric that matters (north star): qualified owner conversations a week.** That is a WhatsApp chat, DM or email from someone who runs a business in Qatar. Every post points to one, and every chat link carries a source code so we know which post started it.

**Why this works for ALYR specifically (from research 03 and the competitor scan):**
- The bar in Qatar is low. Local SMB fintechs have about 3,000 to 12,000 followers, so a few thousand engaged owners would make ALYR the visible one.
- Founder posts beat brand posts on LinkedIn. The SkipCash CEO's post got 146 reactions; the company's post on the same news got 17.
- Gulf event and national-moment posts are the strongest content for WhatsApp tools; generic product posts are the weakest.
- No WhatsApp-tool competitor posts in Gulf Arabic with a premium, product-first look. Wati, respond.io and Gallabox are global, English-first and template-heavy.
- **What actually produced paying small-business customers for others was not views** (research 02, 21 cases). It was:
  - in-person selling;
  - doing the setup for the owner;
  - the product appearing in front of the owner's own customers.

  Examples:
  - Podium pre-sold about 10 businesses door to door before building.
  - lemlist's founder closed the first 100 himself.
  - Calendly's links, Hotmail's footer and GlossGenius's booking links in stylists' Instagram bios turned every use into an ad.

  Content makes those three work faster; it does not replace them.
- **A direct competitor for salons is already here:** Fresha charges QAR 149.95 a month in Qatar (research 02). ALYR's difference is WhatsApp-first, set up in person, in Arabic, with the agent's rules. Never attack Fresha by name.

## 6. The content system

### 6.1 Five pillars (updated from the playbook)

| Pillar | Share now (before the CR) | After the CR | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Owner playbook** | 35% | 25% | One useful thing, with or without ALYR: WhatsApp Business settings, no-shows, Ramadan pre-orders, Instagram-to-chat ordering |
| **Product truth** | 25% | 20% | The product shown exactly as it works, with sample businesses labelled: FIG drawings, the agent's rules, real screens |
| **Build with us** | 25% | 15% | The founder, the visits, what shipped this week, lessons. Real only |
| **Shop-floor stories** | 0–5% | 30% | Real owners, their numbers and their words, with written consent. Starts with interviews (street questions) before customers exist |
| **Doha rhythm** | 10% | 10% | National Day (18 Dec), Ramadan (starts about 8 Feb 2027), Eid, summer, Web Summit Qatar |

### 6.2 Named series (recognisable, repeatable, easy to make)

Series build habit and are faster to make. Each has a fixed look in the studio.

| Series | Platform | Rhythm | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Owner playbook No. N** | IG, FB, LinkedIn, TikTok photos | Weekly | 6–8 slide carousel: cover, one step per slide, a "save and send" end card |
| **FIG. N** | IG, LinkedIn, X | Weekly | One product drawing with its FIG caption: an order in the chat, a booking, a reminder, the agent's feed |
| **Rule of the week** | IG, LinkedIn, X, Snapchat | Weekly | One of ALYR Agent's real rules explained: what it counts, why it matters |
| **Shipped** | LinkedIn, X, IG Stories, WhatsApp Channel | Every Thursday | What was built this week, as a card plus a founder post |
| **Friday rush** | TikTok, Reels, Snapchat | Fortnightly | Street question to owners ("How many messages do you get on a Friday?"), with their consent |
| **On the road** | Snapchat, IG Stories, LinkedIn | During visits | The founder visiting shops (faces only with consent) |
| **Myth / fact** | IG, TikTok, X | Fortnightly | "You need an app to take orders." → "Your customers already have WhatsApp." |

### 6.3 What makes posts spread in 2026 (research 01, 04)

- **Instagram** ranks on watch time, likes per reach and **sends per reach**; sends matter most for reaching new people. So write posts an owner would forward to a partner or a staff group: checklists, scripts, "send this to…".
- **The first 3 seconds decide a Reel.** Instagram now shows a skip rate. Put the result or the problem on screen in the first second, in words.
- **Carousels win engagement; Reels win reach.** Carousels get 9× the saves of single images (Metricool, 2026).
- **Original only.**
  - Instagram (April 2026) and Facebook (July 2025) cut reach for accounts that repost others' work.
  - No CapCut, Canva or TikTok watermarks; ALYR's own logo is fine.
  - Each platform gets its own export.
- **Keywords over hashtags.** Instagram allows up to 5 hashtags, and they don't add reach. Write captions that use the words owners search for in English and Arabic. Professional Instagram posts are indexed by Google.
- **Links:** none in Facebook or LinkedIn post bodies (put them in the first comment). Profiles, Story stickers and DMs carry the chat link.
- **Comments are distribution:**
  - replying lifts engagement about 21% on Instagram and about 30% on LinkedIn;
  - on LinkedIn, 3+ commenters in the first hour multiplies reach;
  - the founder replies to every comment in the first hour.
- **AI.**
  - Photorealistic AI people or scenes must be labelled on Meta, TikTok and YouTube.
  - 73% of people trust ads less when they suspect AI (Harris, June 2026).
  - Snapchat ranks fully AI-made video from outside the app below human-made video, even when it is labelled.
  - ALYR's motion graphics are made in code from the real design system, so they need no label. **Real faces are always real people.**

### 6.4 Hook library (first line or first second)

- The moment: "Friday, 1 pm. 38 unread messages." (only with a real count, from an owner's permission or the founder's own test)
- The rule: "A customer waits 10 minutes. You hear about it."
- The swap: "You don't need an app. Your customers already have WhatsApp."
- The how-to: "Five settings that stop lost orders."
- The cost: "A no-show costs you the chair and the hour."
- The founder: "I visited [n] shops in Al Sadd this week. Here's what every owner asked for." (real number only)
- Arabic examples:
  - «كم طلب ضاع عليك اليوم بالواتساب؟»
  - «موعد ما حضر؟ هذا الحل.»

### 6.5 Arabic

- Write Arabic first for Arabic-first channels (Snapchat, Arabic Instagram). Never machine-translate.
- Gulf Arabic on social and WhatsApp. Simple Modern Standard Arabic on the site. Formal Modern Standard Arabic on contracts.
- **A native Gulf speaker reviews every Arabic line before it is published** (this is already a launch-gate item).
- Product names stay in Latin script. Western numerals for prices, times and phone numbers. Numbers and Latin words sit in their own isolated text runs.
- The pronunciation and Arabic spelling of "ALYR" is still the founder's call (playbook). Posts write ALYR in Latin until he decides.

## 7. The look: premium, from ALYR's own system

The social look is the site's look carried into posts.

**Design elements:**
- **Light first.** Paper `#f6f7f3`, with Carbon `#15171a` for command moments: rules, manifestos, the agent.
- **Volt `#b7f34a` only as a signal, once per image:** the underline on the one word that matters, the progress segment, a tick, the main action. Never as text on light (it is only about 1.2:1 there), never as a background.
- **Type.** Instrument Sans (Latin), IBM Plex Sans Arabic, IBM Plex Mono for the eyebrow, counters, times and FIG captions.
- **The 45° cut corner** on the Carbon plate that holds every product drawing, mirrored in Arabic.
- **FIG captions** on a hairline ending in a tick: "FIG 01 · An order in the chat · Sample, fictional bakery".
- **Neutral drawn chats.** No WhatsApp logo or green; the customer in Carbon and the business in white.
- **Motion.** Things arrive along the logo's 45° diagonal, settle, and one Volt signal confirms them. The fold (three Carbon planes) is the transition. Lines stay on screen for (words ÷ 3) + 1 seconds.
- **Sizes and safe zones** (the `alyr-deck-and-social` skill, with research 04):

| Format | Size | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Feed and carousels | 1080×1350 (4:5), the skill's size | The studio draws at 1080×1440 for Instagram's 3:4 grid and keeps all text inside the middle 1080×1350, so every post is written as both (`NN.png` 3:4, `NN-4x5.png` 4:5). LinkedIn and Facebook get the 4:5 |
| Story and vertical video | 1080×1920 | Keep 250 px top, 380 px bottom and 180 px on the buttons' side clear; the buttons are on the left in Arabic |
| X | 1600×900 | Landscape |
| Link previews | 1200×630 | |

**Templates built (studio, section 8):**
- `cover`
- `step`
- `list`
- `end`
- `statement`
- `chat` (FIG plate)
- `feed` (ALYR Agent)
- `quote` (real quotes only)

Each works in English and Arabic, light and dark, at every size.

**Rules the studio enforces** (`alyr-deck-and-social`): nothing under 32 px on a 1080 canvas; Arabic set about 10% larger; one Volt element per image (the logo aside); every sample captioned once; no prices, customer numbers, faces or AI words; Arabic marked for native review.

**Figma.** The Figma Starter plan's 20 tool calls a month were used up on 18 Oct for the design system. The social kit therefore comes as a local plugin, like the design-system builder: the founder imports it once in Figma desktop, and it builds the editable templates and the first batch as frames, with no tool calls used (`D:\alyr-figma-builder\social\`, see its README). Code stays the source of truth; Figma is where a designer tweaks.

## 11. Measuring

**North star:** qualified owner conversations a week, and how many become a demo and then a paying customer.

**Attribution.** Every link and QR carries a source code (for example `alyr.qa/?s=ig-p05`, and later the WhatsApp prefilled text "Hi ALYR (ig-p05)"). The founder asks every new owner "Where did you hear about us?" and the answer goes in the back-office client notes.

**By platform (check weekly; act monthly):**

| Platform | Look at | Ignore |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram | Sends and saves per reach, 3-second hold (skip rate), profile visits → link taps | Likes alone, follower count |
| TikTok | Average watch time, completion, search views, profile visits | Views alone |
| LinkedIn | Comments from the right people, saves, profile views from Qatar, DMs | Impressions |
| Snapchat | Story completion, profile subscribers from Qatar | |
| WhatsApp Channel | Followers, reactions, replies to the founder | |
| All | Conversations started (north star), cost per conversation once ads start | |

**The monthly review** (one page, with the operator agent): the numbers above, the 3 best and 3 worst posts and why, one change for next month.

**Early targets** (assumptions to test, not promises):
- 10 qualified conversations a month from content by the end of Phase 1.
- Saves plus sends above 3% of reach on carousels.
- 1,000 engaged followers across Instagram and Snapchat in Qatar before launch.

## 12. Rules, risks and what the lawyer must answer

**Questions for the lawyer (ALY-51), before any campaign** (research 03):
1. May ALYR market (posts, a waitlist) before the CR, if nothing is sold and no price is shown?
2. Does promoting our own product on our own pages need the Ministry of Culture's advertising licence (about QAR 5,000 a year, reported Oct 2026)?
3. Does Law No. 7 of 2019 require Arabic in social ads and WhatsApp templates, and how prominent must it be?
4. What PDPPL consent and wording do we need to collect leads, and to message people who opt in?
5. What disclosure do we need when paying creators in Qatar?

**Platform rules that shape the content:**
- Originality (Meta, TikTok, YouTube, Snap).
- AI labels for anything realistic.
- No engagement bait ("comment YES").
- Snapchat ranks off-platform links, QR codes and other apps' names below everything else.
- WhatsApp Channels can't be run from the API, need a real phone number, and limit admins.

**Brand risks:**
- Showing a feature that isn't live: every post is checked against product truth.
- Over-promising: no "48 hours" and no "keep your number" until true.
- Arabic errors: native review.
