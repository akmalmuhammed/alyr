## LinkedIn

### L1 · Why I'm building ALYR (pin this)

> Most owner-run businesses in Doha already run on WhatsApp.
>
> Orders come in as voice notes. Bookings are a screenshot of a calendar. Payment is "send me the transfer screenshot". The owner answers all of it, between customers, until midnight.
>
> I'm building ALYR so that chat works like a shop counter: orders, bookings and payment links in the conversation the customer already started, and one screen that tells the owner what needs them today.
>
> Three things I've decided:
> 1. We set it up for you, in your shop. Software alone doesn't fix this.
> 2. Arabic and English, properly. Not a translation.
> 3. No black box. When ALYR flags something, it shows the rule it used, so the owner always knows why.
>
> We're not live with customers yet. I'll share what we build and what owners teach us along the way.
>
> If you run a business in Qatar, I'd like to hear how your week looks. My DMs are open.

### L2 · The first real order (document carousel, 5 slides from the studio)

> On 12 Oct, a real phone sent "Hello" to our test number.
>
> It got the welcome menu, tapped "Place an order", opened its own order page, ordered, paid on our test payment page (no money moves yet) and got the confirmation back in the chat. Then it booked an appointment, and that was confirmed too.
>
> One round trip. Ten seconds of a customer's time. Weeks of work behind it.
>
> What I learned building it:
> → The hard part isn't the chat. It's the 24-hour window, templates, retries and reminders that arrive when they should.
> → Owners will judge the whole product by the first message their customer sees.
> → Arabic needs its own design, not a mirror of the English.
>
> Next: the first businesses, once ALYR is registered.

### L3 · Why our "agent" has no AI (contrarian; high discussion potential)

> Everyone is shipping an AI agent. Ours has none, on purpose.
>
> ALYR Agent is the screen an owner opens first. It says what needs them today, like "2 customers waiting for an answer" or "1 visit not marked", and how their week is going.
>
> Every line comes from a rule you can read, printed on the card:
> RULE · the customer wrote last and has had no answer for 10 minutes.
>
> A salon owner doesn't need a model guessing. She needs to know why her phone is telling her something, and to trust it on a Friday at 6 pm.
>
> We'll add smarter things when they're reliable. Until then: rules you can check.

*Image:* `p04-rule-waiting-en` or `p03-agent-feed-en`.
