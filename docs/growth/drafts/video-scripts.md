# Short-video scripts: first batch

Drafts, 18 Oct 2026. For Reels, TikTok, Shorts, Snapchat and WhatsApp Status, all from one vertical master (1080×1920).

**Safe zone:**
- Text stays out of the top 250 px, the bottom 380 px and the 180 px on the buttons' side.
- The buttons are on the left in Arabic.

**Rules for every script:**
- The message is on screen in the first second.
- Captions are burned in, in both languages.
- Each line stays on screen for (words ÷ 3) + 1 seconds.
- No WhatsApp logo.
- Sample businesses are labelled.
- Real people only with written consent.

**Start with what exists.** The launch film and the Bookings, Chat and Agent films (60 s, 16:9, `D:\alyr-video\out`) get 9:16 and 1:1 cutdowns of 15 s and 6 s from their owner, the motion videos session. Build V1 only if the Agent film's cutdown doesn't already carry the feed.

Type **P** is made in code from the studio (no filming). Type **F** is filmed on the founder's phone.

## V1 · "This afternoon, done for you" (P, 12 s)

Made in HyperFrames from the `feed` template. The design is the same as `p03-agent-feed-en`.

| Time | On screen | Motion |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.2 | "This afternoon," | Arrives along 45°, settles |
| 0.6–2.4 | "done for you." with the Volt underline | The underline draws in once |
| 2.4–3.2 | The Carbon plate with the cut corner, "ALYR · Running" | Plate slides in along 45°; the dot lights once |
| 3.2–8.6 | Four events, one every 1.3 s: booking confirmed, reminder sent, booking link sent, "2 customers waiting for an answer" | Each row arrives, then its tick fills Volt. The last row gets an empty tick and its rule line |
| 8.6–10 | "RULE · no answer for 10 minutes" | The rule's Volt hairline draws down |
| 10–12 | Logo and "alyr.qa" | The fold closes along 45° |

- **Caption (EN):** What ALYR did for a salon this afternoon, while the owner had her hands full. Sample salon.
- **Sound:** a soft tick on each event; −14 LUFS.
- **Arabic version:** mirrored diagonal and Arabic text.

## V2 · "The order, in the chat" (P, 15 s)

The FIG 01 chat builds message by message:
1. the customer asks to order;
2. the order link arrives;
3. a cut to the drawn order page: two items, the total, pay or collect;
4. back to the chat: confirmation and "Track your order".

End card: "Orders from the chat. ALYR Chat." Labelled "Sample, fictional bakery".

## V3 · "Five settings that stop lost orders" (F + P, 30–40 s)

- **0–2 s:** the founder to camera, phone in hand: "Your WhatsApp is your shop counter. Five settings, five minutes."
- **2–35 s:** a screen recording of the WhatsApp Business app on a test phone (no customer chats visible), with each step's slide from `p01` as an overlay: hours, away message, quick replies, labels, chat link.
- **End:** "Save it. Send it to an owner who still writes orders on paper."
- **Arabic:** recorded separately by the founder or a native speaker, not dubbed.

## V4 · "Friday rush" street question (F, 20–30 s, fortnightly)

- **0–2 s:** caption "We asked Doha shop owners one question" / «سألنا أصحاب المحلات بالدوحة سؤال واحد».
- **2–25 s:** 3–4 owners answer: "How many WhatsApp messages do you get on a Friday?" One line each, captioned in both languages.
- **End:** "We're building something for that. Follow along."
- **Consent:** written consent from each person before filming is used, and faces only when given.

## V5 · "On the road" (F, Snapchat and Stories, 3–5 clips a visit day)

The founder walks into an area ("Al Sadd today"), shows the street, the coffee, the list of shops to visit. **No owners' faces or shop names without consent.** One line of what was learned at the end of the day.

## V6 · "Rule of the week" (P, 8–10 s)

The `p04` statement animated: "A customer waits 10 minutes." then "You hear about it." with the Volt underline, then the rule line types in mono, then the logo. One per rule, as a series.

## Production notes

- **P videos:** HyperFrames in `D:\alyr-video\hyperframes`, from the same tokens as the studio. Render, check frames, then sound (`D:\alyr-video\PLAN.md`).
- **F videos:** shot on the phone in 4K at 30 fps, vertical, with a clip-on microphone. One shoot day a week, on Monday.
- **Exports:** a clean export for each platform, with no other app's watermark (originality rules).
- **Voice:** the founder's own voice in both languages until a Gulf Arabic AI voice passes a blind test with 3–5 listeners. If AI is used for anything realistic, label it on each platform.
